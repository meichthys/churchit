🏛️ Foundations Manual

The foundations module contains features related to founding and core beliefs/principles of the church.

⛪ Churches

The Churches list holds your church. Once Enable Multi-Church is switched on in Church Features, it can also hold branch or satellite churches: mark the main church Is Group, then add each branch with the main church as its Parent Church. There is always exactly one main church (the one without a parent).

What each church shows on the public website. Tick Publish to put the church on the site, then fill in:
- About and Mission Statement, which its About Us page uses
- Image, a photo or logo for its card on the Locations page
- Address, Phone and Email, which the footer and the Contact Us page use
Leave Phone or Email blank and the church shows whatever is in Contact Us Settings, which is shared by the whole site. The Website manual covers how visitors move between locations.

These details also print. Anything you print for a branch church, such as a giving statement, carries that church's own name and contact line rather than the main church's. Legal Name and Tax ID work differently: leave them blank on a branch and it prints the main church's, because the main church is usually the registered charity that issues the receipt.

In multi-church mode every church-specific record (Person, Family, Prayer Request, Function, Fund, Collection, Bulletin and so on) gains a Church field, shown near the top of the form and as a standard list filter. New records default to the church of the person saving them. Records that belong to another record (a sign-up to its function, a care request to its person) take that record's church automatically, and a family belongs to the church of its head of household. You can add such a record only for a record of your own church or a shared one: you cannot book another church's room, move money out of its fund, or record a care request, an alms request or a prayer for its people. The Church Directory still lists a family under every branch that has one of its members. Single-church sites never see the field.

Some records can be handed to every church instead of being kept by one. These carry a Shared with all churches tick box: songs, sermons, sermon series and handouts, vendors, rooms, locations and assets, the occasions churches hold together, which are functions and meeting minutes, and the two things money runs through, funds and ministries. Sharing is one record at a time, never a whole type: nothing is shared until somebody ticks the box on that record. Tick it and every church can see and use it, whichever church shared it, and the Church field disappears because the record no longer belongs to one.

Shared By on the record names the church that shared it, and you can filter a list by it to separate what you shared from what was shared with you. Open a record another church shared and a note at the top says so, with the tick box locked.

Only the church that shared a record can stop sharing it, or delete it. Untick the box there and the record goes back to that church and leaves everyone else's lists, and anything attached to it, such as a booking of a shared room, goes back with it. A branch that no longer wants something shared with it has to ask the church that shared it, so one branch cannot take a resource away from the rest. Anything attached to a shared record is shared along with it, such as a booking of a shared room or a sign-up to a shared function, so every church sees the same diary and the same list of names. Gifts and expenses are never shared: each one keeps the church that gave or spent it, so every report shows a church only its own money. A shared fund or ministry therefore shows its joint figures to every church that can see it: the fund's balance and list of transactions, the ministry's running total of what has been spent on it. People and attendance are never shared.

A Budget tracks the spending of its own church. Tick Include Branch Churches on it to count what the churches beneath it spend as well, which is how the main church budgets for everyone at once.

The Church record also holds the church's Mission Statement and a Church Verse (a Bible reference, such as John 3:16). It can also carry an Image, such as a logo. Bulletins print the verse on the back cover and the image on the front cover when the Church Verse and Church Image sections are switched on. The Church Directory prints the verse on its cover, and the image as well when Church Image on Cover is ticked. Bulletins and the Church Directory cover print the Website. A branch that leaves Legal Name, Tax ID, Default Bible Translation or Website blank uses its parent's.

Turning Enable Multi-Church on stamps the main church on every existing record and gives every existing user a permission for the main church with branches hidden, so nothing becomes visible to anyone by accident. It cannot be turned off again while branch churches exist.

Who Sees Which Church

Everyone sees the records of their own church, and nothing from the others. Someone at a branch sees that branch's events, prayer requests and giving. The main church's records stay out of their way, and theirs stay out of the main church's.

One people directory

People and families are the exception. Every church can look up every Person and Family, so a branch can find a visitor from the main church, choose a main-church pastor as a counselor or speaker, or check in a family from another campus. The Church field on each person shows which church they belong to.
- Only a person's own church can change them. Another church sees the form as read-only.
- Only a person's own church can add a Background Check, Care Request, Care Assignment or Visitation Log for them. You can open a Follow-up Card only on a Follow-up Workflow of your own church. A workflow that starts by itself, such as on a first check-in, still opens a card for a visitor from another church.
- To move someone to another church, use a Member Transfer.
- Prayer requests, care, counseling, visitation and giving stay with each church, whichever directory setting you use. One exception while the directory is shared: finance and care staff of another church can see a person's Giving and Care figures in lists, reports and exports, but not on the Person form. Check-In, People Editor and Church Manager users of every church can also read a person's Medical Notes. To keep all of these inside each church, tick Keep People and Families Private to Each Church.

To hide each church's people and families from the other churches, tick Keep People and Families Private to Each Church in Church Features. The Check-In Station still finds everyone at a function that is shared with all churches.

Where a person's church comes from

From the Church field on their Person record. Fill that in, link them to a login (the Invite to Portal button does this for you), and they can see that church and no other. There is nothing else to set up.
If they later move to another church, change the same field, or confirm a Member Transfer with a To Church. What they can see moves with them.
A staff login is different. Linking it to a person by hand does not change its churches, so set them in User Permission. A later change of the person's Church field, or a Member Transfer, still moves them.

People who belong to one church

Most people. They never have to choose a church, so the app stops asking: the Church field is hidden on their forms, the Church filter is hidden on their lists and reports, and anything they create belongs to their church automatically.

Seeing the branches as well

Someone at a church that has branches beneath it still sees only their own church to begin with. To bring the branches in, choose Include Branch Churches from the menu at the top left. While the branches are included:
- a blue note sits at the top of every page, so nobody forgets they are looking at more than their own church
- that same menu item now reads Hide Branch Churches, which puts things back
- lists and reports gain a Church box. Leave it empty to see every church at once, or pick one church to see only that church.
- ticking Expand filters to branches on the blue note changes that last point: picking a church then shows its branches too. This is remembered for that person alone.

People who see everything

Two kinds of account: the built-in Administrator, which the app never restricts, and anyone who has not been given a church. Their reports still open on the main church, so clear the Church box to see every church at once.

Assigning and sharing

With more than one church, you can assign a record (Assign To) or share it only with someone who can see that record's church. Assigning it to anyone else stops with a message, because the assignment would let them read a record of another church. A record of one church cannot be shared with everyone.

If someone sees the wrong thing

Check the Church field on their Person record first, since that is what drives everything else. What it creates behind the scenes is a User Permission, which an administrator can look at or change there directly. A change made by hand stays until that person's church or login changes, at which point it is set again from their Person record.

Note: Without multi-church, only one Church record is allowed. With it, only one main church (a church without a Parent Church) is allowed; every other church must have a Parent Church that is marked Is Group.

📔Beliefs

Beliefs are the foundational principles the church stands for.

When adding church beliefs, note the following:
- Type the Bible references each belief rests on, such as Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:8-9. They show beneath the statement on the Beliefs web page.

📈Weekly Metrics

Weekly Metrics keeps one record for each church and each week, in the Summary sidebar. It holds the attendance of the week, the first-time visitors, the active members, the people in groups, the people who serve, and the giving and givers of the week. Churchit writes these records itself every night. You cannot add or change one by hand.

Each night, Churchit writes this week and counts last week again. So attendance, first-time visitors and giving can change when someone records a late check-in or a late collection. Active members, people in groups and people serving are the counts on the day of the record. Churchit writes them only for the current week, so last week keeps the numbers of its last day.

Fill the past year. Open Weekly Metrics and click Fill the Last 52 Weeks. Only Church Manager and System Manager users see this button. It fills attendance, first-time visitors and giving for the weeks before last week, and the nightly job writes last week. A week that had no record before shows 0 for members, groups and serving, because Churchit cannot know those numbers for the past. A week that had a record keeps its numbers.

What the numbers count. Attendance adds the attendance of each function that starts in the week. A function that is shared with all churches counts one time, for the church that shared it. The schedule of a repeating function does not count. Giving adds every submitted collection of the week, the same total as the Weekly Giving chart. It includes sign-up and form payments and refunds. Givers counts each person who gave in the week one time.

Only Church Finance, Church Manager and System Manager users see the giving numbers. Church Staff see the other numbers. The Summary workspace shows the Attendance by Week chart, and Church Finance and Church Manager users also see the Giving by Week chart. If you change the first day of the week in System Settings, new records start on the new day, and the weeks before and after the change overlap.

🔗Send records to other systems

This part is for a System Manager. Churchit can send a record to another system when someone adds or changes it, for example to a mailing tool or a chat room. You do not need custom code.

Send a record with a Webhook

Open Webhooks and add one:
- Set DocType to the record to send, such as Person, Function Sign-Up or Connection Card. Set Doc Event to when it goes, such as after_insert for a new record.
- Set Request URL to the address that the other system gives you.
- Tick Enable Security and type a Webhook Secret. Give the same secret to the other system. Each request then carries a signature in the X-Frappe-Webhook-Signature header, so the other system can check that the request came from your site.
- Set Request Structure to JSON. In JSON Request Body, put only the fields that the other system needs, for example {"first_name": "{{ doc.first_name }}", "email": "{{ doc.email }}"}.
- With more than one church, type doc.church == "CHURCH-ID" in Condition, with the ID of the church from its Church record. Then only that church's records go.

Send an alert to a chat room

Frappe sends alerts to Slack. Mattermost and Matrix accept the same alerts:
- In the chat app, make an incoming webhook and copy its address. In Mattermost this is an Incoming Webhook. In Matrix it is a webhook of the hookshot bridge.
- Open Slack Webhook URL, add one with a Name, and paste the address in Webhook URL.
- Open Notifications and add one. Set Channel to Slack, choose the Slack Webhook URL in Slack Channel, and set Document Type, Send Alert On and Message.
- With more than one church, type doc.church == "CHURCH-ID" in Condition.

Note: Never send care, medical, pickup or giving fields to another system, in a Webhook or in an alert. The other system does not protect them as Churchit does.

👥 People Manual

The people module contains features relating to the people of the church (i.e. Members, Families, Leadership. etc).

The People workspace contains number cards and graphs. The cards count people, members, families and groups, this week's visitations, birthdays and anniversaries, and the members who joined in the last 30 days. The graphs show people added, new members and baptisms. There are also links to various reports (Birthdays, Roles, Letters, etc).

👤Person

Church people include guests, members, leadership, and other people who interact or are associated with the church.

When adding a person, note the following:
- 'Membership' tells how the person is connected to the church: Member, Regular Attender, Visitor or Contact. When you add a 'Membership' life event, Membership changes to Member. If needed, you can create more membership types.
- 'Status' is Active for everyone who still takes part in the church. When someone leaves, set it to Inactive and pick the 'Inactive Reason'. When you add a 'Death' life event, the status changes to Inactive and the reason to Deceased. The person is then left out of the birthday and anniversary reports and cards, the bulletin, the Church Directory, the Rolodex, the Check-In Station and Church Emails. A couple's anniversary is left out when either spouse has died. If needed, you can create more member statuses.
    - Reports, the Church Directory, the Members card and the bulletin count a person as a member only when Membership is Member and Status is Active.
    - The Visitor Log shows new people whose Membership is Visitor or empty.
- Record birth, baptism and other life events in the 'Life Events' table. The 'Birth' event fills 'Birth Date' and 'Age', so you do not type them in. The 'Age' field shows only when the age is known: the birth year is known, and so is the date of death of anyone who has died.
    - When you know only the month and day of a birthday, enter any year in the Date and tick 'Year Unknown'. The birthday still counts, but 'Birth Date' and 'Age' stay blank.
    - A 'Death' event ticks 'Deceased' and fills 'Deceased Date' from the event's date. Both fields show only for a person who has died. The age then stays at the age the person died. It is blank when the date of death is not known.
    - 'Life Stage' is Adult or Child. It comes from the person's 'Household Role' in their family. A person with no household role is an Adult from the age set in People Settings (18 unless you change it), and a Child before that. Life Stage is blank when neither is known.
    - Ages and life stages are brought up to date every night. When a Church Manager changes 'Adult Age' in People Settings, every life stage is checked again a few minutes after saving.
    - Birthdays (This Week) and Birthdays (This Month) list each birthday on its date this year, with the age the person turns in 'Turning'. When the birth year is unknown, the birthday is still listed and 'Turning' is blank. In a year with no February 29, a February 29 birthday is listed on February 28.
    - In the People report, 'Birthday' is blank when the birth year is unknown. In Member Demographics, those members count under Unknown Age.
- If the person has any roles in the church, they should be indicated in the 'Positions' section. 
   - If a position type does not exist, you can create new role types as needed.
- Photos should be added for members. The photos will be used in a member directory in a future versions.
- An email address or phone number may be on one person only, which stops the same person being entered twice. Phone numbers match however they are written, so '(202) 555-0147' and '202.555.0147' are the same number.
    - When two people really use the same one, such as a couple's shared email or a child using a parent's phone, enter it on both people and leave 'Shared From' blank on the owner's row, usually the person who uses it to sign in to the portal. On everyone else's row, set 'Shared From' to the owner: the list offers only the people who already have that email or phone. Any number of people may share one email or phone this way.
    - A family may use a member's email or phone without setting 'Shared From'.
- Email Bounced on an email row shows that email to this address came back. Churchit ticks it when a bounce reaches the church's email account. Church Emails do not send to the address while the box is ticked. Churchit clears the box when you change the address. Untick it yourself when you know that the address works again.
- 'Middle Name' sits between 'First Name' and 'Last Name', and 'Nickname' under 'Full Name'. 'Other Names' is a closed section. Click it to add a Prefix (Mr., Mrs., Dr., Rev.), Suffix (Jr., Sr., II, III, IV) and Previous Last Name (for example a maiden name). When you search for a person in any Person field, the nickname and the previous last name are searched too. 'Display Name' is filled in for you: the nickname (or the first name when there is no nickname), the last name and the suffix, for example "Bob Smith Jr.". 'Full Name' stays the first and last name.
- 'Medical Notes' is for health information the church needs to know, such as a condition or a medicine. Only Church Manager, Church Office and Church Check-In Volunteer logins can see it, and only Church Manager and Church Office logins can change it. Other staff logins do not see the field, and members do not see it on their Personal Details page. It is never printed. The Check-In Station shows it to check-in volunteers. Changes to Medical Notes, and to other fields that only some logins can see, do not show in the timeline of the person.
- 'Emergency Contact Name' and 'Emergency Contact Phone' are for someone with no household adult in Churchit, such as a visiting child. The same users who see Medical Notes see them, and the Check-In Station shows them with the medical notes.
- 'Allergies' can also be changed by the member on their Personal Details page. Check-In Settings can print it on name tags, and the Check-In Station shows it.
- 'Source' shows how the person was first added: Desk (typed in on the Person form), Import (by a Data Import), Check-In (added as a New Visitor at the Check-In Station), Connection Card (added from a guest's Connection Card), Portal (added at a first portal sign-in), Sign-Up (added from a guest's sign-up on the Register page) or Form (added from a guest's answer to a form). It is set once and never changes. People added before Churchit recorded this have no Source.
- When the 'Status' is Inactive, pick the 'Inactive Reason' from the list. Add your own reasons, for example Moved away, on the Inactive Reason list. Two reasons are set for you and cannot be deleted or renamed: Deceased (when you add a 'Death' life event) and Transferred to another church (when you confirm a Member Transfer out). 'Inactive Since' is the day the Status changed to Inactive. It is cleared when the person becomes Active again. People who were already Inactive before this was added get the day from the record's history when the history shows the change. Otherwise it stays blank.
- The activity list at the bottom of a Person form shows each time a staff member opened the record, for example "Jane viewed this".
- The Connections tab shows the person's records in four groups. Involvement: functions attended, function sign-ups, groups, ministry team assignments, missionaries they belong to and background checks. Care: prayer requests, prayers, visitations, care requests, care assignments, counseling cases and alms requests. Giving: collections, online donations and giving statements. Membership: member transfers. Each one shows only to the roles that can read it, and counts only the records you can open, so another church's records about the person are not counted.
- Letters from this person can be added in the 'Contact' tab
- Tick 'Hide from Directory and Bulletin' to leave a person out of the printed Church Directory, and out of the birthdays and anniversaries in the bulletin. Members can tick it themselves on their Personal Details page in the portal.
- The 'Family' section shows the person's family and marriage. If a family has been selected, the person will be displayed in the "Family Members" section on the `Family` form.
- 'Marital Status' is Single, Married, Widowed or Divorced. If needed, you can create more marital statuses. The 'Spouse' and 'Anniversary' fields show only when the person is Married or Widowed.
    - When a Married person gets a spouse, the spouse's document is linked back as Married and the anniversary is shared.
    - When you set a person to Divorced or Single, the marriage ends for both people. The spouse and the anniversary are removed from both, and the former spouse gets the same marital status.
    - A Widowed person keeps the spouse and the anniversary. When you add a 'Death' life event to a married person, their spouse becomes Widowed.
    - When a spouse is added, the couple shares one family. If only one of them has a family, the other joins it. If neither has one, a family named after the person being saved is created, with that person as its primary contact. If they are in different families, no one is moved.
    - A Family can be automatically created from a person by clicking the 'New Family From Person' button. This requires a 'Last Name' to be set, and makes the person the Family's primary contact. The button is there for anyone who is not already the primary contact of a family, so a married son can start his own family.
    - When a married person moves to another family, their spouse moves with them if the spouse was in the same family.

To give a person access to the Church App Portal, the church person must have an associated email address. 
Click the "Invite to Portal" button on the 'Person' form to create and associate a new user with the 'Person'. Only users who can change the person can invite them. If a login with that email address already exists, Invite to Portal links it to the person instead. You cannot link a login that another person already has, or a login of a church you cannot see. Linking a staff login, with this button or in the 'Portal User' field, does not change which churches the staff member sees. An administrator sets that in User Permission.
    - The user will be created with 'Church User' permissions and linked with the 'Person' (This should give the user access to their own information as well as information that is shared with them).
    - The user should receive an invitation/welcome email (assuming email settings have been setup correctly).

Staff logins

Portal access is for members. Staff and volunteers who work in the desk need a user with a Role Profile that matches their job:
- Church Staff: looks up people, events, groups and ministries, and keeps their own prayer requests, sign-ups and room bookings.
- Church Office: also adds and edits people, families, groups and member transfers.
- Church Care Team: also handles care, counseling, visitation, background checks, and prayer and alms requests.
- Church Treasurer: also handles collections, expenses, funds, budgets and giving statements.
- Church Check-In Volunteer: uses the Check-In Station only.
- Church Manager: everything, including settings.

To give someone several jobs, add more than one Role Profile to their user. Each person only sees the workspaces, reports and dashboards their roles can use.

Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a code as well as the password when someone logs in, so a stolen password alone does not open the church's records. We recommend it for every staff login, because staff can see personal and medical details.
To turn it on:
1. Open System Settings and go to the Login tab.
2. Under Two Factor Authentication, tick 'Enable Two Factor Auth'.
3. Choose the 'Two Factor Authentication method': OTP App (a code from an authenticator app on a phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), SMS (set up SMS Settings first) or Email.
4. Save. Each user sets up the method the next time they log in.
When you turn it on, 'Two Factor Authentication' is ticked on the All role, so everyone is asked for a code, members included. To ask only staff, open the All role and untick 'Two Factor Authentication'. Then tick it on the Church Staff, Church Manager and Church Check-In roles. Every staff login holds one of these roles.

Single sign-on

With single sign-on, people use one account for Churchit and for other apps. A System Manager sets it up. The Integrations workspace is hidden, so use the links here.
Sign in to Churchit with another account. Use this when your church already has accounts in Google, Keycloak or Authentik.
1. Open Social Login Key. Choose the 'Social Login Provider': Google, Keycloak, or Custom for Authentik and other OpenID Connect providers.
2. Type the 'Client ID' and 'Client Secret' that the other system gives you. For Keycloak, type the address of the realm in 'Base URL'. For Custom, type a one word 'Provider Name', for example authentik, and the 'Authorize URL', 'Access Token URL' and 'API Endpoint' of the other system. Set 'Redirect URL' to /api/method/frappe.integrations.oauth2_logins.custom/authentik.
3. Set 'Sign ups' to Deny. Then only people who have a user in Churchit can sign in. Churchit finds the user from the email address.
4. Tick 'Enable Social Login' and save. In the other system, set the redirect address to your site address followed by the 'Redirect URL' of the key.
The login page then shows a button for the provider.
Use Churchit accounts in another app. Use this for an app that supports OpenID Connect, such as a wiki or Nextcloud.
1. Open OAuth Client. Type the name of the app in 'App Name'.
2. Set 'Scopes' to openid. Type the redirect address that the app gives you in 'Redirect URIs' and in 'Default Redirect URI'.
3. In 'Allowed Roles', add the roles that can use the app. For all staff, add Church Staff, Church Manager and Church Check-In. A user with none of these roles cannot sign in to the app. If you leave it empty, Frappe adds Desk User, so every desk login can use the app.
4. Set 'Token Endpoint Auth Method' to Client Secret Post. The app cannot get a token with Client Secret Basic.
5. Save. In the app, type the 'Client ID', the 'Client Secret' and the discovery address: your site address followed by /.well-known/openid-configuration.
The first time a person signs in to the app, Churchit asks them to allow the app to see their full name, email address, picture and roles.
Other apps can also add themselves as an OAuth Client without a login. If you do not need this, untick 'Enable Dynamic Client Registration' in OAuth Settings.

School grade

Type the year that a child finishes high school in 'Graduation Year'. Churchit shows the school grade in 'Grade' and updates it each day. The grade is Kindergarten in the school year that starts 13 years before graduation, then Grade 1 to Grade 12, and then Graduated. Before kindergarten, 'Grade' is empty. The grade goes up on the first day of the month in 'School Year Starts In' in People Settings. The default is August. If your school year starts in January, the school year is the calendar year. When you change the month, the grades change soon after you save. For example, when the school year starts in August, a child with Graduation Year 2030 is in Grade 8 on July 31, 2026 and in Grade 9 on August 1, 2026.

Envelope numbers

'Envelope Number' on a person is the number on their offering envelopes or the reference on their bank gifts. Two people in one church cannot have the same number. People in different churches can. Type the number in any Person field, for example in a donation row of a collection, to find the person. A signed-in member sees their envelope number on the Give page as the payment reference to use. A People Import file can fill it in with an Envelope Number column. When you merge two people, the person you keep gets the number.

Member QR Card

The Member QR Card print format prints a wallet card for a person. The card shows the church name, the person's name and photo, their Person ID, and a QR code of the Person ID. Open the Person, click the print icon, and select Member QR Card. The card is 3.375 × 2.125 in, the size of a bank card. At the Check-In Station, click Scan Card and show the card to the camera to find the person and their family.

Skills, interests and spiritual gifts

Use the 'Skills, Interests and Gifts' field on the Person form to record what the person can do, what they like to do and their spiritual gifts. Select one or more Skills. Each Skill has a category: Skill, Interest or Spiritual Gift. Churchit starts with twelve spiritual gifts from Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12, Ephesians 4 and 1 Peter 4. Church Managers and People Editors can add more Skills or change them. All churches use the same list of Skills. To find the people with a skill, open the Person list in Report view, add a filter on Skill (Person Skill), and save the report. You can then use it as a smart list (see Lists of People). When you merge two records of one person, the kept person gets the skills of both.

Church fields

A System Manager can add the church's own fields to the Person form and the Family form, for example a T-shirt size or a locker number. Do these steps:
1. Open Customize Form and select Person or Family.
2. In the fields table, find the row 'Church Fields' at the end. Add the new field below that row. Do not set 'Mandatory'. Churchit adds people and families without church fields, for example from a Connection Card, an import or My Household, and a mandatory field stops these additions.
3. To show a Person field only to staff with more access, set its 'Perm Level' to 1. Then only Church Managers, People Editors and Check-In users can see it. Do not set a Perm Level on a Family field, because then nobody can see it.
4. Click Update.
The field shows on the Church Fields tab of the form. You can filter a list of people on it and save the list as a smart list. A church field is the same for every church on the site. When you remove the personal data of a person, Churchit clears all of that person's church fields. It also clears the church fields of their family when it clears the family.

Relationships to other households

Use the 'Relationships' table on the Person form to record a relative or other person who lives in another household, for example a grandparent. Select the Relationship and the Person. You record the relationship on one person only. The other person sees it on their Connections tab, in the Relationships group. Click it to see the people who recorded a relationship with them. You cannot select the person themselves or a person in the same family. Use the Members table of the Family for people in one household. When the directory is private to each church, you can select only people of your churches. When you merge two records of one person, the kept person gets the relationships of both, and a relationship with themselves is removed. When you remove the personal data of a person, or delete a person, Churchit removes all relationships that other people have with them. When you remove the personal data, Churchit also removes the relationships on that person.

Merge two records of one person

When one person has two records, merge them. Open the record you do not want to keep, click ⋯ > Merge Into…, select the person to keep and click Merge. Only users who can delete people, such as a Church Manager, can merge, because a merge deletes a record.
- Everything that names the removed record moves to the kept record: attendance, giving, groups, ministry teams, care, prayers, comments, attachments and the history of changes.
- The kept record keeps its own values, except the values that you choose from the removed record (see below). Each empty field gets the value of the removed record. A spouse comes with the Marital Status of the removed record.
- The emails, phone numbers, addresses, life events, positions, letters, skills and relationships of the removed record are added to the kept record. A value that the kept record has already is not added again, and the primary email, phone and address of the kept record stay primary.
- The portal login moves to the kept record when the kept record has none.
- A list that names a person once, such as the members of a group, the attendance of a function or the members of a family, keeps one row for the person.
- The kept person stays in their own family. A kept person with no family joins the family of the removed record.
- A Possible Duplicate for the two records is marked Merged.
- When both records came from an import, the kept record keeps its own Imported ID. A comment on the kept record names the other ID. The next import from that system adds that record again, so merge the two in that system too.
- The removed record is deleted for good. It is not kept in Deleted Documents.
Churchit does not merge two people when both have a portal login, when they have different spouses, when they are married to each other, or when they belong to different churches. You cannot undo a merge. The Rename dialog's 'Merge with existing' follows the same rules. You cannot change the ID of a person in any other way.

Choose the value to keep. When both people have a value for a detail and the values are different, the merge dialog shows a table. Each row is one detail, and each column is one person. Select the value to keep in each row. The person you keep has their own value selected by default. Details that only one person has go to the person you keep automatically. You can choose only the details that you can edit. A Portal User or an Envelope Number that the other person still has cannot move in a merge. To give it to the person you keep, clear it on the other person first, and then set it on the person you keep.

Remove the personal data of a person

A Church Manager or a System Manager can remove the personal data of a person who asks the church to forget them. Open the person and click ⋯ > Remove Personal Data. The record stays with the name Anonymized, so their giving and attendance still count in reports.
- Churchit removes the name, emails, phone numbers, addresses, letters, photo, medical notes, emergency contact, allergies, anniversary, skills, how they heard of the church, church fields, portal login and Imported ID. The Gender becomes Unknown, and the person is hidden from the directory.
- The Birth life event is removed. The other life events keep their type and date, so the counts for each year stay correct, but they lose their location and notes.
- The person leaves their family and their spouse. The spouse keeps their own Marital Status. Churchit removes the relationships on the person and the relationships that other people have with the person. A family with no members left is anonymized too. A family that keeps members loses the person's first name from its name, so "Smith - John" becomes "Smith".
- Records that name the person keep the link, but the name and email address of the person are removed from their titles and text.
- The history of changes, the comments and the attachments of the record are deleted, and so are those of the records about the person, such as background checks, care requests, counseling cases and giving statements. Emails and texts sent to the person lose their address and text, but a Church Email still counts them as sent. The follow-up actions that failed for the person lose their error message.
- The person's open Follow-up Cards are closed. Their Card Closed actions do not run, and no other workflow starts from them.
- The person's answers on each function sign-up are deleted, and the answers of the other people on the sign-up stay. The name, email and phone they typed on their own sign-ups are removed, also on a guest sign-up made with one of their email addresses that was never linked to them.
- The person's Person Notes, Connection Cards and Form Responses are deleted, and so are the Pickup Restrictions that protect them as a child.
You cannot undo this. The portal login is disabled. When a member asks for their data to be deleted and a System Manager approves the Personal Data Deletion Request, Churchit removes the personal data of their record in the same way.

Remove personal data on a schedule. In People Settings, set 'Remove Personal Data After (Years Inactive)' to the number of years. Once each day, Churchit then removes the personal data of people who have been Inactive for that number of years or more. It removes the same data as Remove Personal Data and adds a comment to the person that gives the reason. It does up to 50 people each day, the people who became Inactive first. People who have died are kept. 0, the default, never removes data. The Personal Data Due for Removal report shows who is next and the Removal Date of each person. Only Church Managers and System Managers see this report. To keep the data of a person, set their Status back to Active before that date.

Visitations

Visitations are visit of a person by a church representative. 
When adding a visitation, note the following:
-  The visitation type can be changed, or new visitation types can be added.

Person Notes

Person Notes keep what staff know about a person, for example a phone call, a visit or something to remember. To add one, open the person, click ⋯ at the top and choose Add Person Note. Pick a Category, write the note and save. You stay on the person's form. The Date is today unless you change it. When the note records a call or a visit, pick how you were in touch in Contact.

A person's notes show in the Activity list at the bottom of their form, on the day each note records. Tick Pinned to keep a note at the top. Tick Alert to show the note in red at the top of the person's form, for example "Hard of hearing. Face him when you speak." Only people who may read the note see the alert.
You see only the notes your role may read. Other notes do not show in the Activity list, in the Person Note list or in reports. Notes cannot be shared with other users.

Note Categories

A Note Category decides who reads and adds its notes. A Church Manager sets the categories up. If you have more than one church, every church uses the same categories. Only a Church Manager of the main church can add or delete a category, or change its roles or Private. A branch can still change the Description.
- Roles That Read Notes: the roles that can read notes in this category. Leave it blank to let all staff read them.
- Roles That Add Notes: the roles that can add and change notes in this category. Leave it blank to let all staff add them.
- Private: only the person who wrote a note can read it. A Church Manager cannot read it either.
Churchit starts with three categories. Every staff member reads General. Only the care team and Church Managers read Pastoral Care and Benevolence. Benevolence holds the case notes of alms requests. The text from a person's old Notes field is now a note in General. If a role can add notes in a category but cannot read them, its users cannot open a note again after they save it.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Family

Families are groups of people who are related.

When adding a family, note the following:
- After adding a family, you may want to link people to the family. When a person is linked to a family, that person will be displayed in the "Family Members" list.
- Set the 'Primary Contact' to the family member the family is named after. You can pick only a person in the "Family Members" list.
- On a site with more than one church, a family belongs to the church of its 'Primary Contact'. You can make someone the primary contact only when you can see their church. In the same way, you can put a person into a family of another church, from the Person form or the Family form, only when you can see both churches.
- In the "Family Members" list, the 'Relationship to Primary Contact' of each member tells what they are to the primary contact, such as Wife or Son. The primary contact's own row tells what they are in the family, such as Husband.
    - The primary contact and their spouse are labelled Husband and Wife from the 'Spouse' field on the person. No other member can be Husband or Wife.
    - A member's spouse who has no relationship yet gets one from the member: a Son's wife is a Daughter-in-law, a Daughter's husband is a Son-in-law, and a Brother's wife is a Sister-in-law.
- When you change the 'Primary Contact', the family is renamed after the new primary contact, such as 'Doe - John' to 'Doe - Jane'. Each relationship changes to the relationship to the new primary contact, so the mother of the old primary contact becomes the Mother-in-law of the new one. A relationship that cannot be worked out is cleared, and a message names the members whose relationship you must set by hand.
- When the primary contact leaves the family, the family has no primary contact until you set a new one.

Household roles

Each member of a Family has a 'Household Role': Adult, Other Adult or Child. An Adult is a responsible adult of the household, such as a parent. An Other Adult lives in the household but has no authority over the children, such as a grandparent, a grown son or a lodger. A Child is a dependent.
When you save a Family that has a Primary Contact, Churchit fills in each blank Household Role:
- The Primary Contact and their husband or wife are Adults.
- A son, daughter, stepchild or grandchild younger than the adult age in People Settings is a Child.
- Any other member whose age is known and is at or above the adult age is an Other Adult.
- If Churchit cannot tell, for example for a member with no relationship and no birth date, it leaves the role blank. Set it by hand.
Churchit never changes a Household Role that someone set. If you link a husband or wife after the Family has a Primary Contact, Churchit can already have made the spouse an Other Adult. Then set the spouse's Household Role to Adult by hand. The Household Role also sets the person's Life Stage: a Child is a Child, and an Adult or Other Adult is an Adult.
A letter or mailing label can address a family by its Adults, for example "John & Jane Smith", or "John Smith & Jane Doe" when their last names are different. A nickname is used in place of the first name, and people who have died are left out. A family with no Adult is addressed by the family name. In a print format, write {{ salutation(doc.family) }}.

Merge two families

When one household has two Family records, open the family you do not want to keep, click ⋯ > Merge Into…, select the family to keep and click Merge. The members, emails, phone numbers and addresses move to the kept family, without duplicates. The kept family keeps its name and its Primary Contact. A kept family with no Primary Contact takes the Primary Contact of the removed family. A member from a family with a different Primary Contact loses their 'Relationship to Primary Contact', so set it again by hand. Each 'Household Role' stays. Only users who can delete families can merge them. You cannot undo a merge. The removed family is deleted for good. It is not kept in Deleted Documents.

Authorized Pickups

The Adults of a household may always collect its children after check-in. To let another person collect them, such as a grandparent, an aunt or a neighbor, open the Family and click + next to Authorized Pickup in Connections, or open Authorized Pickups from the People workspace. Set the 'Person', the 'Relationship' and any 'Notes', for example "Sundays only". The person must have a Person record. An Adult or a Child of the household cannot be an Authorized Pickup, and a person is listed only once for a family. An Other Adult of the household, such as a grandparent who lives there, can be one. The Verify Pickup step of the Check-In Station shows these people with their photos. When you merge two families, the kept family gets the Authorized Pickups of both families, each person once. When you delete a family, its Authorized Pickups are deleted. Do not write custody details in the notes. Use a Pickup Restriction.

Who sees Authorized Pickups. Church Manager and Church People Editor users read and change the Authorized Pickups of the families of their own church. No other role can open or list them. When a family moves to another church, its Authorized Pickups move with it. Check-in volunteers see them only at Verify Pickup, for the children of the code they type.

Pickup Restrictions

A Pickup Restriction names someone who may not collect a child, for example because of a court order. It applies also to an Adult of the child's household. Pick the 'Child'. Pick the 'Restricted Person', or type their name in 'Restricted Name' when they have no Person record. In 'What the Volunteer Sees', write what the check-in volunteer must know, for example how to know the person and whom to call. Set 'Valid Until' to the last day of the restriction, or leave it empty. Write the 'Reason' and attach the 'Court Order' as a private file. Only Church Manager and Church People Editor users can open a Pickup Restriction. At Verify Pickup, the Check-In Station shows the restricted person, the child, 'What the Volunteer Sees' and the last day, but never the reason or the court order. It refuses to release the child to the restricted person. Only a Church Manager can override this, with a reason that Churchit records on the Function Check-In. A page to the parents from the Check-In Station does not go to the restricted person.

🏠Member Portal

My Household

Members see their household on the My Household page in the portal. The page shows the Family photo, the Family name, the mailing address and the living members with their Household Role. It shows the age of each Child when the birth date is known.
- A member clicks Edit to change their own Personal Details. An Adult of the household can also click Edit for each Child who is younger than the 'Adult Age' in People Settings, or whose birth date is not known.
- An Adult can click Change photo to put a new photo on the Family. The photo is a public file, so other members can see it in the Member Directory. Other members cannot change the Family.
- An Adult can add a child. See Add a child below. Members cannot add other people or remove people. The page tells them to contact the church office. A member without a Family sees only their own name.
- When check-in is open for one of today's functions that allows self check-in, the page also shows Check In Before You Arrive. See Pre Check-In in the Ministries manual.

Add a child

An Adult of a household can add a child on My Household. Click Add a Child, then enter the first name, last name, birthday and gender, and click Add Child. The child must be younger than the Adult Age in People Settings. The child joins the household with the role Child and the relationship Son or Daughter. The new person is a Visitor with the source Portal and shares nothing in the member directory. If the child may already be in the directory, a Possible Duplicate opens for staff to check. A Child or an Other Adult of the household cannot add a person. To add an adult or remove someone, the member contacts the church office.

Personal Details

On the Personal Details page, a member can type their Birthday. Churchit records it on the person's Birth life event, so the Birth Date and the Age change too. When the year of the Birth life event is unknown, the Birthday is empty until the member types the full date.
In the Directory section, a member can check 'Hide me from the member directory'. This also leaves them out of the printed Church Directory and out of the birthdays and anniversaries in the bulletin. A member can also choose to show their email, phone, address and birthday in the Member Directory.

Member Directory

The Member Directory page in the portal lists the people of the member's church. To turn it on, check 'Member Directory' in People Settings. When it is off, the page and its portal menu item do not show.
- The directory lists only people whose Status is Active and whose Membership is not Visitor or Contact. It leaves out people who have died and people who checked 'Hide from Directory and Bulletin'.
- Only people the directory lists can open it. A person who hides from the directory can still open it. Other people see a note that tells them to contact the church office.
- Each Family is one entry, with the Family photo and the names of its members. A person without a Family has an entry of their own.
- It shows the primary email, the primary phone, the mailing address and the birthday of a person only when that person shares it. A birthday shows the day and the month, not the year.
- Each entry has a Save contact button. It downloads a contact card (a .vcf file) with the entry's name and only the email, phone and address that the entry shows. A phone or a computer adds the card to its contacts.
- The directory shows only public photos. When you attach a photo on the desk, clear 'Private' if members may see it.
- A member types in the search box to find a name on the page.

In People Settings, the Member Directory section has these fields:
- 'Member Directory' turns on the Member Directory page in the portal.
- 'New People Show Their Email', 'New People Show Their Phone', 'New People Show Their Address' and 'New People Show Their Birthday' set what a person shares when you add them. A change applies to the people you add after it. People already on file keep their own choice. People that Churchit adds from a Connection Card or a portal sign-in share nothing until they choose.

Portal accounts

When a person signs in to the portal for the first time, Churchit connects the login to a Person. Churchit looks in the church of the login for a person who has the same primary email and no Portal User. If it finds one person, it connects the login to that person. If it finds no person, it adds a Visitor with the name and email of the login, and the Source is Portal. People who look like the new Visitor go to Possible Duplicates. If it finds more than one person, it connects nobody and puts those people in Possible Duplicates; merge them, or set the Portal User on the correct person. A Visitor that Churchit adds this way shares nothing in the Member Directory. Staff logins are never connected this way. This only applies when your church lets people sign up on the website, or when you add a Website User by hand.

Send Details Link

To let a person correct their details without a login, open the Person and click ... > Send Details Link. You see this item when you can change the person and the person has a primary email. The site must have an outgoing Email Account. Churchit emails a link to the Update Your Details page. On that page the person can change their name, gender, birthday, allergies, email addresses, phone numbers, marital status and anniversary, and what they show in the Member Directory. They cannot change their addresses or Shared From; the office changes those. The link works one time, for 7 days. After the person saves, the link stops working. To send a new link, click Send Details Link again. The timeline of the Person shows when you sent a link.

👥Group

Groups are collections of people with a related purpose (e.g. Sunday School classes, ministries, small groups, committees, etc.).

When adding a group, note the following:
- Check 'Public' if anyone can join the group. (Members can join public groups in the portal. See Joining a Group.)
- Check 'Show in Portal' if users should be able to see their group membership in the portal.
- Members can be added in the 'Members' section. Each member is assigned a Group Role (e.g. Leader, Member).
- A group can be linked to a Ministry via the Ministry form.
- Check 'Show in Directory' to list the group's members in the Church Directory. They are listed only when the directory's 'Group List' and 'Group Members' options are ticked.

Group Types

A Group Type is a kind of group, such as Small Group, Class, Support Group or Youth Group. When you choose a 'Group Type' on a group, the form fills in the defaults of the type: 'Public', 'Show in Portal', 'Requires Approval' and 'Capacity'. You can change each value after that. A change to a type does not change the groups that already use it. If you have more than one church, every church uses the same types.

Meetings. A group meets in Functions. On the group, click Actions, then Set Up Meetings, to make the recurring schedule of the group (see Group Meetings in the Ministries manual). 'Meets' on the group shows when and where the group meets, for example Weekly on Tuesday at 7:00 PM, Main Hall. To change it, change the schedule Function. The connections at the top of the group show its meetings, its join requests and its files.

Group Leaders

A person leads a group when their row in 'Members' has a Group Role with 'Leads the Group' ticked. Churchit ticks it on the Leader role. Tick it on each other role whose people lead a group, for example Teacher or Host. The leaders of a group record its attendance, message its members and decide its join requests.

Joining a Group

Members join a group on the Groups page of the portal or on the Find a Group page of the website. They can join only an Active group that has 'Public' and 'Show in Portal' ticked.
- 'Capacity' is the most members the group takes. When the group is full, nobody can join. 0 means no limit.
- After the 'Close Enrollment On' date, nobody can join or ask to join.
- When 'Requires Approval' is ticked, a member who clicks Join sends a request. Churchit makes a Group Join Request and emails each leader of the group. A member who asks again sees the same request.
- A leader clicks Approve or Deny on the Lead a Group page of the portal. Staff who can edit the group can open the Group Join Request and click Approve or Deny. Approval adds the person to the group with the Member role. The person gets one email with the answer.
- A full group cannot take an approved request. Raise its 'Capacity', or deny the request.

Background Checks for a Group

In the Requirements section of a group, tick 'Needs Background Check' if the people in the group must be cleared to serve. In 'Required Checks', choose the check types they need. If you choose none, any current, cleared check is enough. In 'Checks Apply To', choose Leaders to check only the people whose Group Role leads the group, or Everyone. When you add a person, or change the role of a person, Churchit looks at their checks for today. If 'Block People Without Checks' is ticked, you cannot save the group until the person has the checks. This applies also when a leader approves a join request. If it is not ticked, the group saves and Churchit shows a warning. People who were in the group before are not checked again.

Group Files

A Group File is a file that only the members of a group can open, for example study notes or a schedule. Staff add one in the desk: choose the group, type a title and upload the file. The leaders of a group add one on the Group Files page of the portal. Members see the files of their groups on that page and open them there. People who are not in the group cannot open a file, even with its link. Keep 'Private' ticked when you upload: Churchit does not accept a public file for a group. A leader cannot change or delete a file in the portal. Staff do that in the desk.

Find a Group

The Find a Group page of the website lists the groups that a visitor can join: Active groups that have 'Public' and 'Show in Portal' ticked, with their type and description. Find a Group in the top menu of the website opens it. Visitors filter the list by type and by day. When a group has a schedule Function, the page shows when the group meets and the city of the meeting place. The map shows each group near its meeting place, never at the exact address. A visitor who clicks Join signs in first and then comes back to the group. A group that requires approval shows Ask to Join, then Requested. A full group shows Full, and a group past its 'Close Enrollment On' date shows Closed. If you have more than one church, the page shows the groups of the selected church.

Lead a Group

A leader opens Lead a Group in the member portal. The page shows each group that they lead: the members, the email and phone of each member who shares them, the requests to join, and the meetings of the last 8 weeks. A member who leads no group sees You do not lead a group.

Record the attendance. Open a meeting, choose Present or Absent for each member, and click Save. If the group did not meet, tick The group did not meet and click Save. A check-in from the Check-In Station stays as it is. Each save adds a comment on the Function that names the leader.

Attendance reminders. The day after a meeting, each leader gets an email that asks them to record the attendance. No email goes when the attendance is recorded or the group did not meet. Only leaders who allow Group Messages get it, and only when the site has an outgoing email account.

Message the group. A leader writes a subject and a message under Message the group and clicks Send. Each living member who allows Group Messages gets one copy that names the leader. The leader gets a copy too. When the site can send texts, the leader can choose Text message. A leader can send 10 messages in one hour. Each message adds a comment on the Group. Members reply to the leader by the email or phone of the leader.

The Email Templates Group Attendance Reminder, Group Message, Group Join Request and Group Join Decision hold the text of these emails. A church can change them. Group Messages is a Communication Purpose: a person who withdraws their consent for it gets none of these emails.

Group Attendance report. The Group Attendance report has one row for each group that met in the dates you choose, the last 12 weeks unless you change them. It shows the meetings held, the average number of people who attended, the current members and the attendance rate. A meeting marked Did Not Meet, a meeting in the future and the schedule of the group do not count. Only Confirmed, Assumed and Checked-In attendance counts. Filter by Group Type or by Group.

2. Select which options you want for the report. They are in groups, and the directory preview is updated after each selection:
    - People:

        - Hide Non Active People: This leaves out everyone whose Status is not Active, with their birthday and their anniversary. A couple is left off the anniversaries when either person is not Active. A family whose primary contact is not Active is listed by its surname only. This option is off by default.
        - Group by Family: This will list each family together under the family name, with the primary contact first, then their spouse, then the other members by name. When it is off, each person is listed by name.

        - Photos: This will display available photos in the directory.
        - Positions: This will show the person's church position under their name in the directory
        - Membership: This will show how the person is connected to the church, such as Member or Visitor, next to their name.
        - Family Relationships: This will show each person's 'Relationship to Primary Contact' from their Family, such as Husband, Wife or Son. When it is off, no relationships are printed.

        - Phone Numbers: This will show each person's primary phone number.
        - Emails: This will show each person's primary email address, and the email address of each missionary.

        - Birthday List: This will include a list of all the people's birthdays near the end of the directory.
        - Anniversary List: This will include a list of people's anniversaries near the end of the directory. Each couple is listed by their own names, such as 'John & Mary Smith'.
        - Missionaries: This will include a list of missionaries at the end of the directory, with the names of the people in each missionary's People table. Missionaries whose Status is Inactive are left out. If the missionary is marked as 'sensitive', only the name, people, photo, and agency will be shown. A person in a listed missionary's People table is left out of the family and individual pages, so they are not shown twice.
        - Notes Page: This will add a lined page at the end of the directory for handwritten notes.
    - Groups:
        - Group List: This will include a list of the groups at the end of the directory, with each group's name and description. Groups whose Status is Inactive are left out.
        - Group Members: This will list the members of each group that has 'Show in Directory' ticked, by first name, several to a line. People that the directory leaves out, such as hidden people, are left out of the groups too. This option shows when Group List is ticked.

📇Rolodex

Every staff login can open it from the People workspace, or with the Rolodex button on the Person and Family lists. The Family list opens it showing households.

- Use the buttons on the front of a card to call, text, email or map the person.

- Switch to Households to see one card per family. People without a family keep a card of their own.
- Click Filters to narrow the cards by membership, status, group or position, and by church when your site has more than one. Saving cards
Tap the bookmark on a card to save it, for example to build a list of everyone to call this week. Saved cards get a ribbon and gather in a tray at the bottom of the page. On a household card, the bookmark at the top saves the whole family, and each person has a bookmark of their own.
- Your saved cards stay with your login, so a list built on a computer is waiting on your phone.
- From the tray you can Email everyone saved (their addresses go in BCC, so no one sees the others), Print their cards, or Add to Group if you can edit groups. Clear empties the tray.

🗺️Map of Addresses

Click Map of Addresses in the People sidebar to see the addresses on a map. Click a pin to open its address.

📨Member Transfers

Member Transfers record people transferring into or out of the church.
- Set the Direction (In/Out), the other church, and update the Status as the transfer progresses.
- When you confirm a transfer Out to a church that is not one of your branches, the person's Membership changes to Contact, their Status to Inactive, and their 'Inactive Reason' to Transferred to another church. The Member Transfer keeps the name of the other church.
- When you confirm a transfer In, the person gets a 'Membership' life event dated that day, which names the other church. Their Membership changes to Member and their Status to Active. Nothing changes for a person who is already an active member.
- A 'Letter of Transfer' can be printed from the form.
- With multi-church on, a move between your own branches is a transfer with a To Church: confirming it moves the person (and their portal access) to that branch.

📈Membership Changes

Churchit keeps a record each time a person is added and each time their Membership or Status changes. The Membership Changes report lists these changes for a date range, the last 90 days unless you change it. Each row shows the date, the person, the field, the value before the change and the new value. Use the Field and New Value filters to find, for example, everyone who became a Member this year. Changes made before this report was added come from each person's change history.

🪜Assimilation Funnel

The Assimilation Funnel report shows how far new people go after their first visit. Set 'First Visit From' and 'First Visit To'. The default is the last 3 months. The report lists each living person whose first attended function is between these dates. For each person it shows four steps:
- First Visit: every person in the list.
- Came Back: the person attended a function again within the number of weeks in 'Came Back Within (Weeks)'. The default is 8.
- Connected: the person is in a group or has served on a ministry team.
- Member: the person's Membership changed to Member between the first date and today.
A person can get to a step without the step before it. The numbers at the top and the chart show how many people got to each step.

🛤️Pathways

A Pathway is a path people take through the life of the church, for example Came Back, Joined a Group, Baptized and Serving. Each step is a smart list: a saved Report Builder list of people or a saved Attendance Count. A person has done a step when they are on the step's list.
To make a pathway, first save a smart list for each step, for example a list of people whose Groups count is 1 or more. Then add a Pathway, give it a name, and add one row for each step, in the usual order. In Smart List, pick the step's list.

Click Open Progress on the pathway, or open the Pathway Progress report and pick the pathway. The report lists each living person who has done at least one step, with a tick for each step done, the number of steps done, and the first step that is not done yet. A person can do a later step before an earlier one. The numbers at the top and the chart show how many people have done each step. With more than one church, a pathway counts only the people of its own church.
To start a follow-up when a person reaches a step, add an On Smart List trigger with the step's list to a Follow-up Workflow.

🧹Clean Data

Possible Duplicates

When you add a person, Churchit compares the name, email and phone with the people already in the directory. If the new person may already be there, a yellow note shows at the top of the form. The note gives a link to each person and the reasons. The note does not stop you from saving.
Each week, Churchit looks for people in the same church who may be the same person. It adds each pair to the Possible Duplicates list. It does not pair people in the same family. It does not add a pair again after someone closes it. An import adds pairs for the people it adds.
The Possible Duplicates card on the People workspace shows how many pairs are open. Open a pair to see the two people side by side. A gray row has the same value for both people.
- Click Merge to make the two people one person, then select the person to keep. See 'Merge two records of one person' above. Only users who can delete people see Merge.
- Click Not a Duplicate when the two people are different people. Churchit does not pair them again.

Churchit gives each pair a score from 0 to 100. The same email, phone, birth date and name make the score higher. A different first name, birth date, gender or suffix (for example Jr. and Sr.) makes the score lower. Churchit knows common nicknames, so Bob and Robert can be the same person. A pair must reach the Duplicate Match Threshold in People Settings. The default is 80. A higher number finds fewer pairs.

Find a person by phone or email

In a field where you select a person, you can type a first, middle or last name, a nickname, a phone number or an email address. Type four or more digits of the phone number, in any format. Type the email address with its @ sign.

People Data Quality report

The People Data Quality report shows living people whose records need attention. Each row shows one problem and, when it helps, the value to fix. The problems are: No Way to Reach (no email, phone or address on the person or on their family), Invalid Email, Invalid Phone Number, Email Bounced, Mail Returned, Possible Duplicate, Child Without Birth Date and No Family. Use the Problem filter to see one problem at a time.
When mail sent to an address comes back, tick Mail Returned on that address row of the person. Mailing labels then leave that address out.
When email sent to an address comes back, Churchit ticks Email Bounced on that email row. Church Emails then do not send to that address. Ask the person for a new address. When you change the address, Churchit clears Email Bounced. You can also untick it yourself.

📥Import People

You can add people from another church system or from a spreadsheet. Churchit uses Frappe's Data Import tool. Data Import reads the file, shows a preview, and adds the people in the background. Church Managers and System Managers can import people. A Church Manager sees only the Data Imports they made.
Each row of the file is one person. Churchit makes a Person for each row and a Family for each household. The people of one household share one Family and its address.
Churchit keeps the ID that each person and household had in the old system. When you import a newer file from the same system, Churchit updates these people. It does not add them again.

Import from Planning Center

1. In Planning Center People, open the gear menu and click Import & Export CSV.
2. Click Start Export. Planning Center sends you an email with a link. Download the CSV file. If the download is a ZIP file, open it and use the CSV file in it.
3. In Churchit, in the People sidebar, click Data Imports, then click Add Data Import. Set Document Type to Planning Center Import.
4. For the first import, keep Import Type as Insert New Records. For a later import, select Insert or Update Records.
5. Save, upload the CSV file and click Next. Do not change the file. Churchit reads the Planning Center columns as they are.
6. Map each value that Churchit does not know to a value of your church, or skip its rows. For example, map the Name Prefix "Mr." to "Mr".
7. Click Import.
Household Primary Contact sets the Primary Contact of the Family. A person with Child set is a Child of the household. The other people of the household are Adults.

Import from Rock RMS

Rock RMS cannot export all people at once. Make a report one time. Then export it to Excel each time you import.
1. In Rock, open Tools, then Reports, and add a report for the Person entity.
2. Add the fields below to the report. For each field, set Column Header to the name in this list: Id, Family Id (the Primary Family Id field), Family Name, Family Role (the Age Classification field), Title, First Name, Nick Name, Middle Name, Last Name, Suffix, Gender, Birthdate, Anniversary Date, Marital Status, Connection Status, Record Status, Inactive Reason, Is Deceased, Email, Mobile Phone, Home Phone, Work Phone, Home Address Street 1, Home Address Street 2, Home Address City, Home Address State, Home Address Postal Code, Home Address Country. You must add Id, Family Id and First Name. The other fields are optional.
3. Run the report and click Export to Excel.
4. In Churchit, in the People sidebar, click Data Imports, then click Add Data Import. Set Document Type to Rock RMS Import.
5. For the first import, keep Import Type as Insert New Records. For a later import, select Insert or Update Records.
6. Save, upload the Excel file and click Next.
7. Map each value that Churchit does not know. For example, map the Connection Status Attendee to Regular Attender, and the Record Status Pending to Active.
8. Click Import.
Rock has no head of household. Churchit makes the first Adult of each family in the file its Primary Contact. You can change the Primary Contact on the Family.

Import from Breeze

1. In Breeze (Tithely ChMS), click the gear icon to open Account Settings. Click Manage Account, then View More, then Export.
2. Select People and click Export. Download the spreadsheet.
3. In Churchit, in the People sidebar, click Data Imports, then click Add Data Import. Set Document Type to Breeze Import.
4. For the first import, keep Import Type as Insert New Records. For a later import, select Insert or Update Records.
5. Save, upload the file and click Next. Do not change the file. Churchit reads the Breeze columns as they are. Make sure that the Birthdate column uses the date format mm/dd/yyyy. If it does not, change the date format of that column before you import.
6. Map each Status that Churchit does not know to a Membership, for example Attender to Regular Attender, or skip its rows.
7. Click Import.
People with the same Family value share one Family. The Head of Household is the Primary Contact, and the Family gets their name. A family with no Head of Household gets its first Adult as Primary Contact. A person with the Family Role Child is a Child of the household. A Spouse or an Adult is an Adult. For Unassigned, Churchit sets no role. A person with a known birth date at or over the 'Adult Age' becomes an Other Adult. Set the other roles on the Family.

Import from a spreadsheet

1. In the People sidebar, click Data Imports, then click Add Data Import. Set Document Type to People Import, save, and click Download Template to get the columns.
2. Put one person on each row. Give each person a Person ID. Give the people of one household the same Household ID. Set Primary Contact to Yes for one person of each household. Set Household Role to Adult, Other Adult or Child.
3. If the people come from another system, type its name in Imported From. Otherwise leave it blank.
4. Upload the file, look at the preview, and click Import.

The preview and after the import

A row that cannot be imported stops the import, for example a row with no first name. Correct the file, or click Skip Row. A value that Churchit does not know stops the import until you map it or skip its rows. A note does not stop the import. For example, a note tells you which Person a row updates, or that an email address is not valid and Churchit leaves it out.
The import log shows the Person that each row added or updated. Each imported Person has the Source Import. Imported From and Imported ID show where the person came from.
To remove what an import added, see Undo an import below.
An update changes only the fields that have a value in the file. It adds new email addresses, phone numbers and addresses. It does not remove the old ones.
Churchit compares each new person with the people already in Churchit. If two people may be the same person, Churchit adds a Possible Duplicate. Review each Possible Duplicate and merge the people that are the same.

Undo an import

You can remove the people and families that one import added, for example after an import with wrong columns. Do it soon after the import, before people add attendance, gifts or notes to the new records. You need the right to delete people and families, which a Church Manager has.
1. Open the Person list and click Filter. Add 'Source' equals Import, 'Imported From' equals the system, for example Planning Center, and 'Created On' between the day of the import and the same day. For a spreadsheet import with no Imported From, use only Source and Created On.
2. Make sure that the list shows all the people. If it does not, click 500 at the bottom of the list.
3. Tick the box in the list header to select all the people. Click Actions > Delete, and then click Delete.
4. Open the Family list. Filter on 'Imported From' and 'Created On' in the same way. Select all the families and delete them in the same way.
What happens:
- Churchit removes each deleted person from their family and from the record of their spouse. It also deletes the Possible Duplicates of the person and the relationships that other people recorded with the person. When a person who stays shares the phone number or email address of a deleted person, the person who stays keeps the phone number or email address.
- Frappe does not delete a person that a later record names, for example a person who attended a function after the import. It shows the records that it did not delete. Keep these people, or delete the later records first.
- Frappe does not delete a family that still has a person, so the families of the people who stay also stay.
- When you delete more than 10 records, Frappe deletes them in the background and shows a message when it is finished.
The undo removes only the people and families that the import added. It does not change back the people that an Insert or Update Records import updated. The addresses of the deleted families stay in the Address list. A System Manager can delete them there.

🙋Connection Cards

A Connection Card is the welcome card a guest fills in on their phone, at /connection-card on your website. They tell you their name, email and phone, whether they are new, and what they would like to do next. After a guest sends a card, Churchit looks for them in the directory.
- If one person has the same email or phone and the same first and last name, the card is linked to that person, and Churchit does not change that person's details.
- If not, Churchit adds the guest as a new person with the membership Visitor. Any close matches go to Possible Duplicates for you to check.
- Then each follow-up workflow that starts on a Connection Card opens a follow-up card for the person.
The Connection Card list shows every card, with its status, its person and when it was processed. A Church Manager can also add a card in the desk from a paper card, and Churchit processes it the same way.
When a Connection Card adds a new person, the person's 'How They Heard' field gets the answer that the guest gave on the card. When the card is linked to a person who is already in the directory, Churchit does not change that person's 'How They Heard'. You can also type it on the Person form.

Print a QR code for the form

Open the Connection Card web form and click QR Code. If you have more than one church, pick the church that the cards are for. A guest who scans that code sends their card to that church. Leave Church empty for the main church. Click Download to save the code as an image for your bulletin or welcome card. Every published web form has this button, so you can do the same for the prayer request form.

What guests can tick

The boxes under "I Would Like To" come from the Connection Card Interest list. Churchit starts you with Join a group, Serve on a team, Learn about baptism, Learn about membership and Talk with a pastor. You can rename them, delete them or add your own. To start a follow-up workflow when a guest ticks one, add a Connection Card trigger to the workflow with that interest in Which. A Connection Card trigger with no value starts on every card.

Who came with the guest

In Who Is With You? a guest can add the people who came with them: first name, last name, relationship and birth date. When Churchit adds a new Visitor for the guest, it also makes a Family with the guest as the Primary Contact. It adds each person from the list as a Visitor in that Family, with the relationship the guest chose. A person with no last name gets the last name of the guest. A Wife or Husband becomes the spouse of the guest. A birth date becomes a Birth life event, so the Family can set the household role. People who look like someone on file go to Possible Duplicates. If the card matched a person who is already on file, Churchit adds nobody from the list, because a card never changes a person on file. The list stays on the card; add these people by hand if you need them. A card marked Spam adds nobody.

Spam and busy Sundays

The form has a box that people never see. Robots fill it in. A card with that box filled in gets the status Spam and nothing else happens; the sender is not told. If a real guest's card was marked Spam, add the person yourself.
Each internet address can send the form at most 10 times a minute. On a busy Sunday, guests on the church Wi-Fi share one address. A guest who sees a "too many requests" message can send the card again a minute later.

🧭Follow-up

Follow-up Workflows

A Follow-up Workflow is a list of steps that you use to look after a person, for example Welcome Call, Coffee Invitation and Connect to a Group. Add a workflow and give it a name. Add one row for each step, in order. In Assign To, choose who takes the step. In Due In (Days), say how many days they have to finish it. A step with no user is not assigned to anyone. The person in Assign To must be able to see your church's records.
When you save the workflow, Churchit makes a board for it, with one column for each step. Click Open Board to see it. If you change the steps, the board changes the next time you save. You cannot remove a step while open cards are on it. Move those cards first.

Follow-up Cards and the board

A Follow-up Card is one person on one workflow. It shows the person's phone number and email address, the step it is at, and when it is due. A person can have only one open card on a workflow. To add a card by hand, open the person, go to Connections and click + next to Follow-up Card.
To move a card, drag it to the next column on the board, or open the card and click Move To. When a card moves, the person for the new step gets a to-do and one email, and the due date starts again from the new step.
Click Snooze to hide a card from the board until a date. On that date it comes back by itself. Click Close when the follow-up is done, and write what happened in Outcome. Closing the card also closes its to-do. When two records of one person are merged, only the older open card on each workflow stays open.

Starting cards by themselves

In Start a Card When on a workflow, add a row for each thing that should start a card. Leave Which blank to start on any value, or choose one:
- Connection Card: a person fills in a Connection Card. Which is the interest they ticked.
- First Check-In: a person checks in for the first time ever. Which is the Function Type. For a Function shared by all churches, the person's own church does the follow-up.
- Signed Up: a sign-up for a function becomes Confirmed. Which is the Function Type. A card starts for each attendee Person. A guest's card starts when the guest's sign-up is linked to a person. An attendee who has only a typed name gets no card.
- Form Submitted: a person sends a form. Which is the Question Set. For a set shared by all churches, the person's own church does the follow-up.
- Life Event Added: a life event is added to a person, for example a Baptism.
- Membership Type Becomes and Member Status Becomes: a person's membership changes to this value.
- On Smart List: once a day, everyone new on a saved list of people gets a card. Each person gets a card from the list only once, even if they leave the list and come back. People who have died are left out.
If a person already has an open card on the workflow, no second card starts.

Start a workflow from another workflow

In Start a Card When, add a row with the trigger Step Reached or Card Closed, and in Which choose the other workflow. For Step Reached, type the Step to start only when a card reaches that step. Leave Step empty to start at every step. When a card on the other workflow reaches the step or closes, the person gets a card on this workflow. The new card shows the first card as its Source. A workflow cannot start itself, and it can start only from a workflow of the same church. A card closed when two people are merged does not start a workflow. When a card reaches the step again after it was reopened, the person gets a new card on this workflow only if their earlier card on it is closed.

Actions on a workflow

A workflow can do work by itself when a card opens, reaches a step or closes. Open the Follow-up Workflow and add a row in the Actions table. In When, choose Card Opened, Step Reached or Card Closed. For Step Reached, type the Step, or leave it empty for every step. In Action, choose one of these:
- Send Email: choose a Purpose and an Email Template.
- Send Text: choose a Purpose and type the Text Message.
- Add to Group: choose a Group of your church.
- Add Tag: choose a Tag.
A message can use {{ first_name }}, {{ last_name }}, {{ display_name }}, {{ full_name }} and {{ step }}, the step the card is on. The workflow does not save when a message uses another name. A message goes only to a person who allows that Purpose. A text goes only to a person who agreed to texts.
Each action runs one time for each card, event and step. When a card goes back to a step, the action does not run again. When two people are merged and one of their cards is closed for that reason, the card closed does not run its Card Closed actions. To see what ran, open Follow-up Action Runs. Done means the action ran. Skipped means there was nothing to do, for example the person withdrew consent, has no address, or is already in the group. Failed shows the error, for example when the site has no outgoing email account. A failed action does not run again.

Follow-up volunteers

Give a volunteer the Church Follow-up Team role profile. A volunteer sees only the cards assigned to them, on the board and in the list, and can move, snooze and close those cards. When a card moves to another person's step, or is closed, the volunteer no longer sees it.

Reminders, following and notices

On any card, click ⋯ and choose Remind Me to get a reminder at a time you pick. Choose Follow to get an email when the card changes. Follow works only when "Send Notifications For Documents Followed By Me" is turned on in your user settings.
To be told when a card is overdue, open Notifications, duplicate "Follow-up Card Overdue" and turn on the copy. It emails the person assigned to an open card one day after its due date. For anything else, make your own Notification on Follow-up Card, for example when Step or Status changes, or on Person.

🤝Care Requests

Care Requests record "need help" requests from members (meal help, hospital, grief and so on). Members can send them from the portal.
- When you pick a person, Caregiver is filled in from their Care Assignment. You can change it.
- Due On is set from the urgency: Urgent 1 day, High 3 days, Medium 7 days, Low 14 days. When you change the urgency, the due date is counted again from today. You can also type a date.
- When the caregiver has a desk login, the request goes on their To Do list, dated when it is due, and they get a notice. When you choose a new caregiver, the request moves to that person's list. When you resolve or close the request, it leaves the list.
- A caregiver with no desk login gets nothing, and Churchit tells you so. Let them know yourself.
- On a site with more than one church, the caregiver must be able to see the request's church.
- The Care Request Overdue notification can email the caregiver and the care team the day after an open request was due. To use it, duplicate it and turn the copy on.
- New Care Request Types can be added as needed.

🛡️Background Checks

Background Checks record the screenings a person has had so leaders can see who is cleared to serve. Churchit does not run the check itself: order it from whichever provider your church uses, then record the result here.
- Pick the person and the Background Check Type (Criminal Background, Sex Offender Registry, Child Abuse Clearance, Driving Record, or your own). Each type sets how many years a cleared check stays valid.
- Note the provider and their reference number so the report can be found on their site. The report itself can be attached.
- Move the status from Requested to Pending to Cleared or Not Cleared as the check progresses. A completed date is required for a result.
- Expires On is filled in from the type's validity when a completed date is entered, and can be changed. Cleared checks are marked Expired automatically once that date passes.
- The most recent check is shown on the Connections tab of the Person form, above all of the person's checks.
- Child Safety Training is a check type for a child safety course. It stays valid for 2 years. Record it as Cleared when the person finishes the course.
- The Background Check Expiring notification can email the care team 30 days before a cleared check expires. To use it, duplicate it and turn the copy on.
- A ministry team position can require some check types and block people without them. See Positions in the Ministries manual.

👥Care Assignments

Care Assignments map a person to a deacon or care-team member for a date range. An assignment is considered active while the current date falls within its start/end dates.

💰 Finances Manual

The finances module contains features related to the finances of the church.

💵Collections

A collection tracks donations from people for specific functions.

Each time a collection is taken, a New Collection should be created. When creating a new collection note the following:
- Although not required, It is recommended to link the collection to the Function in which the collection is being taken.
    - By regularly linking functions to collections, it will be possible later to run reports to see collection amounts for specific function types, etc.
- Remember: After saving a collection, it will be in a draft state. In order to finalize the collection, you will need to 'Submit' the collection. Once submitted, it cannot be modified unless you cancel and amend it. Do not cancel and amend the collection of an online gift: cancelling it marks the gift Refunded, and Churchit does not let you amend it.
    - See the 'Terminology' section in the Help guide for more information about document states (draft / submitted / cancelled / amended / etc).
- After submitting the collection, you can click the Bank Reconciliation Report button on the top right of the form. This report can be used to compare with bank deposit reports.

The Finances workspace, contains links to view the collection list and various collection reports.

🤲Donations

A donation tracks the amount given from a person toward a church fund. For tracking monies given to people in need, see Alms below.

When adding donations to a collection, note the following:
- After entering each donation, the collection total and fund totals will be updated in real-time.
- If a single donation check specifies multiple funds, enter it as two separate donations (one for each fund).
- Additional payment types can be added if needed.
- When entering a donation from a specific person (i.e. Check donation or Cash Envelope), it is highly recommended to link the donation to the person.
   - By regularly linking donations to persons, end-of-year statements and individual person donation reports can be created.

Envelope numbers. A person can have an Envelope Number: the number on their offering envelopes or the reference on their bank gifts. When you enter a gift, type the envelope number in the Person field of the donation row to find the giver. Two people in one church cannot have the same number.

Non-cash gifts. When someone gives property, such as equipment or goods, and not money, add a donation row with the Payment Type Non-Cash. Type a description of what was given in Description, and keep the amount 0. Churchit does not let you save a non-cash row with an amount or without a description, because the church describes the gift and does not state its value. The giver's statement shows the description in place of an amount. Non-cash gifts do not change fund balances or collection totals. You can mark another Payment Type as Non-Cash on its form.

The Finances workspace, contains links to view donation reports. 

The donation and expense reports count only submitted collections and expenses. A report or a giving statement for a date range includes all of its last day, so a gift at 11:59 PM on the last day counts.

Giving on the Person form. A gift counts toward a person's First Gift, Last Gift and Lapsed Giver when its collection is submitted. A draft or cancelled collection does not count. Lapsed Giver is ticked when the last gift was more than 90 days ago but within the last year. The Lapsed Givers list, under Collection Reports, shows these people with their first and last gift dates and when they last attended. A Church Manager can have it emailed to a finance user with ⋯ > Setup Auto Email. Only Church Finance, Church Manager and System Manager users see these fields.

💳Online giving

Members and guests give online on the Give page. Churchit uses the payment gateways of the Payments app, and it works with two of them: Stripe and PayPal. To set up online giving:
1. A System Manager enters the account keys in Stripe Settings or PayPal Settings. Only a System Manager can see and change the gateway settings and keys.
2. Open Giving Settings. Add the gateway in Payment Gateways, mark one row as the default, and tick Enable Online Giving.
3. On each fund that people can give to, tick Allow giving to this fund.

Limits of the two gateways.
- Stripe cannot ask for 3-D Secure, the check in which the bank asks the card holder to confirm the payment. When a card needs 3-D Secure, the gift is declined. In the UK and in the EU many cards need it, so churches there use PayPal.
- PayPal Settings holds one PayPal account for the whole site, so every church that uses PayPal gets its gifts in that one account. Stripe can hold one account for each church: add one Stripe Settings record for each account.
- The payment page of each gateway shows the card fields in a frame inside a page of your site. Thus the PCI DSS 4.0 rules for scripts on payment pages apply to your site: keep a list of the scripts on these pages with the reason for each, and make sure that you find out when a script changes. Ask your payment processor what it needs from you.

Online giving with more than one church. Each gateway row in Giving Settings has a Church. Name a church and that gateway serves only it, which is how each congregation's gifts reach its own account; leave it blank and every church can use it. A church with its own gateways uses only those. Wording Per Church does the same for the thank you message and the statement acknowledgment, so a branch's receipts carry its own words.

Online gifts. Online giving works with Stripe and PayPal. Giving Settings refuses a gateway of another kind, and the Give page does not offer one. When the gateway confirms a payment, Churchit compares the amount and the currency that the gateway charged with the Online Donation:
- When they agree, Churchit records the gift in a Collection and sets the status to Paid.
- When they do not agree, Churchit records nothing and sets the status to Needs Review. The Church Finance users of the gift's church get an alert that gives the charged amount and the gift amount. Check the payment in the gateway before you do anything else.
- When the gateway charged a gift but the gift stays Pending, for example because the confirmation did not arrive, the Church Finance users of its church get an alert each day. Open the gift and click Record Charged Gift. Churchit checks the payment again and records the gift one time.

Processing fees

A giver can add the processing fee to a gift, so that the church gets the full gift. Open Giving Settings. In the gateway row, tick Offer Fee Cover and type the card rate in Fee Percent and Fixed Fee, for example 2.9 and 0.30. Fee Percent must be less than 100, and neither field can be less than 0. The Give page then shows "Cover the processing fee" with the amount, worked out with the card rate. Churchit calculates the fee on the server from these two fields, so the giver cannot change it. The fee is part of the gift: the Online Donation shows it in Covered Fee, and the collection, the receipt and the giving statement show the full payment.

Give links and QR codes

Open a fund that has Allow giving to this fund ticked and click Give Link. The dialog shows a QR code and the address of the Give page with this fund chosen.
- Click Download QR Code to get the code as an SVG file for a bulletin, a poster or a giving card.
- Click Copy Link to copy the address.
- To fill in an amount, add &amount=25 to the end of the address.
- To make a tap to give tag, write the address on an NFC tag with a phone app.
With more than one church, the address names the fund's church, and that church must be published.

Text-to-give

With text-to-give, a person sends a text to a phone number and gets back a link to the Give page. Churchit does not receive texts, so your text provider sends this reply. Churchit does not log these texts, and they are not on any timeline. To set it up:
1. Get a separate phone number for giving from your text provider, for example Twilio. Do not use the number that sends your Church Emails and other texts, because the reply goes to every text that comes in.
2. Get the link. For the Give page, the link is your site address followed by /give, for example https://yourchurch.org/give. For one fund, click Give Link on the fund, then click Copy Link. This link looks like https://yourchurch.org/give?fund=FUND-0001. To fill in an amount, add &amount=25. With more than one church, the link also names the church, for example https://yourchurch.org/give?church=CHR-02&fund=FUND-0001.
3. Set the number to reply with the link to every text. A provider that has keyword replies can reply only to the texts that have the word GIVE. In Twilio, add a TwiML Bin with this text, and put your link in it: <Response><Message>Thank you for giving. Give here: https://yourchurch.org/give</Message></Response>. In the bin, write each & in the link as &amp;, for example https://yourchurch.org/give?church=CHR-02&amp;fund=FUND-0001. If you do not, Twilio does not accept the bin. Then open the phone number in Twilio. Under A message comes in, choose TwiML Bin and this bin, and save.
4. Send a text to the number from your own phone. Make sure that the link comes back and opens the Give page.
The text provider handles STOP replies to the giving number, as for all texts. The Communications manual tells how, in Text replies and STOP.

Other ways to give

Open Giving Settings and write your bank transfer, standing order or mailing details in Other Ways to Give. The Give page shows this text below the form, also when online giving is off. A church can have its own text: add a row for it in Wording Per Church and fill in Other Ways to Give. A church with no text of its own shows the site text.
A signed-in member who has an envelope number sees it on the Give page as the payment reference to use. Enter these gifts in a collection, and type the envelope number in the Person field of the donation row to find the giver.

Give a guest gift to a person

A guest who gives without logging in has no person on the gift, so the gift is not on a giving statement. To find these gifts, click Gifts with No Person on the Finances workspace or in the sidebar. Open the Online Donation, set Person and save. The donation row of the gift's collection then names that person, with the person's email, and a comment on the collection says which gift changed. The next giving statement for that year includes the gift. Only Church Finance and Church Manager users can do this.

Record a refund

Refund the gift at the payment processor first. Churchit does not send the refund.
- For a full refund in the same year as the gift, open the gift's collection and cancel it. Churchit removes the gift from the fund balance and sets the Online Donation to Refunded. If the gateway confirms the payment again later, Churchit records nothing. You cannot amend the cancelled collection.
- If you already exported the dates of the gift to your accounting system, do not cancel the collection. Make a new collection with a negative donation row for the full amount, as for a partial refund. Thus the GL Journal shows the refund.
- For a partial refund, or a refund in a later year, make a new collection with the date of the refund. Add a donation row for the same person and fund with a negative amount, for example -20, and submit it. The giving statement for the year of the refund shows the lower total.

Event registrations and paid forms. A function sign-up or a form with a price takes an online payment through the gateways of the Give page. The Ministries manual tells how to set the prices. Each payment comes in as a submitted Collection with one row for the fund of the function or the Question Set, and no person, so it is not on a giving statement. It goes to the church of the function or the set, or to the church that shared it. When the gateway charged an amount that is not the amount due, or charged a record that is already paid, Churchit records nothing and alerts the Church Finance users of that church. Each day, Churchit also alerts them about sign-ups and form responses that a gateway charged but that are not recorded.

Registration payments by hand. For a sign-up paid in cash or by check, open the sign-up and click Payment, then Record Offline Payment. Churchit adds no Collection, so put the money in the weekly collection. When a payment arrives after a sign-up lost its seats, or when a paid sign-up is cancelled or declined, its Payment Status is Refund Due and Church Finance gets an alert. Refund it at the payment processor and cancel its Collection or add a negative donation row, as for a gift. Then click Payment, then Record Refund on the sign-up, or Record Refund on the Form Response. Only Church Finance and Church Manager users can do this.

🧾Giving statements

A giving statement lists a giver's gifts for a period, for the giver's tax records. In the Giving Statement list, click Generate Statements and enter From Date and To Date. Churchit makes or rebuilds the statements from the submitted collections in those dates.
- A statement shows only gifts to funds that have Tax Deductible ticked (see Funds below).
- A non-cash gift shows its description in place of an amount, and the total counts only money.

Email the statements

In the Giving Statement list, click Email Statements. Enter the same From Date and To Date that you used to generate the statements, then click Email. Churchit sends each statement to the giver's primary email at the time it sends, with the statement attached as a PDF. The emails go out in the background, so you can continue to work.
- A statement with no email address is not sent. Print it and mail it instead.
- A giver who unsubscribed from all email gets no statement email, and the statement is not marked as emailed.
- A statement that was emailed once is not sent again. If you generate the statements again and a gift on a statement changes, the next Email Statements sends the new statement. To send all of them again, tick "Send again to statements already emailed".
- Each email is written in the Access Log. To change the text of the email, edit the Email Template "Giving Statement".

Giving statements with more than one church. A statement is issued in your church, also for a giver from another church who gave at your collections. A giver who gave at two churches gets a statement from each. You cannot move a statement to a church that is not yours, or take one from another church.

📥Import giving history

You can add past gifts from another church system, from a spreadsheet or from your payment processor. Churchit uses Frappe's Data Import tool. Data Import reads the file, shows a preview, and adds the gifts in the background. Church Finance users, Church Managers and System Managers can import gifts.
Each row of the file is one gift. Churchit adds each gift as a donation to a draft collection. The gifts of one batch on one date go in one collection, and its Expected Total is the sum of these gifts. Check each draft collection and submit it. The gifts count in the funds, the reports and the giving statements only after you submit the collection. Churchit does not send a thank you email for an imported collection.

The file has these columns. You must fill in Gift ID, Date, Amount, Fund and Payment Type.
- 'Gift ID': the ID of the gift in the system it comes from, for example a Stripe charge ID. Churchit imports each gift one time.
- 'Date' and 'Amount': the date of the gift and the full amount that the giver paid.
- 'Fund' and 'Payment Type': a fund and a payment type of your church. A gift with the payment type Check also needs a 'Check Number'.
- 'Imported From' and 'Person ID': the system name and the ID of the giver from your People import. Leave 'Imported From' blank when you imported the people from a spreadsheet.
- 'Email': finds the giver when the Person ID does not. Only one person in your churches can have this email.
- 'Batch': the gifts of one batch and one date go in one collection. When you leave it blank, the batch is the name of the Data Import.
- 'Notes': text for the donation.

1. In the Finances sidebar, open Setup and click Data Imports, then click Add Data Import. Set Document Type to Giving Import. Keep Import Type as Insert New Records.
2. Save, and click Download Template to get the columns. You can also use the export of your payment processor (see below).
3. Upload the file and click Next. When the file has a column name that Churchit does not know, select the Churchit column that it agrees with, or do not import the column.
4. Look at the preview. A row that cannot be imported stops the import. Correct the file, or click Skip Row.
5. Click Import. Then open the Collection list, filter on Import Batch, check each draft collection and submit it.
If your church uses its own copy of the Donation Acknowledgment notification, set its Condition to doc.donations and not doc.import_batch before you submit imported collections. If you do not, each giver gets an email for each old gift.

The preview stops a row when:
- Churchit does not know its fund or payment type, or its fund belongs to another church.
- No person, or more than one person, has its Person ID or email.
- A donation already has its Gift ID. Thus a second import of the same file adds nothing. You can import the gift of a cancelled collection again.
- An earlier row of the file has the same Gift ID.
- Its payment type is a non-cash type, such as Non-Cash. Enter a non-cash gift in a collection with a description.
- An online donation that your payment processor charged has the same amount, date and email. This includes a Pending or Needs Review gift. Record that gift from the online donation, not from the import.

Import gifts from Stripe. Use this for gifts that givers make on the pages of Stripe, for example recurring gifts and bank debits. Churchit records the gifts from your Give page itself, so do not import them again.
1. In the Stripe Dashboard, open Payments, set the dates and click Export. Download the CSV file.
2. Open the file in a spreadsheet. Delete each row whose Description starts with "Online gift to the". These are the gifts from your Give page, which Churchit has already.
3. Delete each row whose Status is not Paid, and each row with an Amount Refunded.
4. Rename these columns: id to Gift ID, Created date (UTC) to Date, Customer Email to Email. Keep Amount. Delete the other columns.
5. Add the columns Fund and Payment Type, and fill in a fund and a payment type of your church on each row. You can add a Batch column, for example with the month.
6. Import the file with the steps above.
Stripe gives the date in UTC. A gift late in the evening can have the date of the next day.

📊Funds

Funds are used by churches to tag monies for a specific purpose. Some default funds are included (i.e. General, Building, Missions, etc), but additional funds can be created for your own purposes.

When adding/managing a fund, note the following:
- Any collections that contained donations the the fund will be listed in the 'Transactions' section at the bottom of the fund form.
- The transactions of a collection take the date of the collection, not the day it was submitted.
- Manual transactions can created for the fund if needed
    - If the transaction is a transfer between funds, then create a fund transfer instead. Fund transfers automatically add a transaction to the fund.
    - If the transaction is documenting an church expense, add an expense instead. By choosing the expense type, an associated fund transaction will automatically be created. 

Tax Deductible. Giving statements are tax receipts, so they show only gifts to funds that have Tax Deductible ticked. New funds have it ticked. Untick it for a fund whose gifts are not tax deductible, for example a collection that you pass on to another organization. A fund that is shared by all churches has one setting for every church.

The Finances workspace, contains links to view fund reports along with a graph showing the current totals in each fund.

Funds with more than one church. A fund belongs to one church until you tick Shared with all churches on it, which every church can then give to and spend from: a joint building fund, or one missions fund the whole organisation supports. Sharing a fund shares the pot: its balance and its list of transactions are the joint ones, and every church that can see the fund can see them. Only the church that shared it can stop sharing it. Each gift and each expense still belongs to the congregation that gave or spent it, so every report shows a church only its own money. A fund transfer takes money out of a fund of your church or out of a shared fund. Only the church that owns a fund can move its money.

🙏Pledges

A Pledge Campaign is a giving campaign that people promise to support, such as a building campaign. Give it a title, the fund that receives its gifts, a start date, an end date and, if you want, a goal amount. Each campaign belongs to your church and is not shared with other churches.

A Pledge records what a person promises to give in total to a campaign. Set Family to make it a household pledge: the gifts of everyone in that household then count. Frequency records how often the giver plans to give. It does not change the pledged amount.

Members can pledge on the portal at /pledge. The page shows only the campaigns of their own church that have not ended. Everyone who uses the page pledges only for themselves, so the login must have a Person record. The list on the page shows only your own pledges. Staff add pledges for other people on the desk. A member cannot change a pledge after the campaign ends.

The Pledge Progress report shows each campaign with its goal, the total pledged and the total received, and then each pledge with the amount received from its giver. A gift counts when its collection is submitted, it goes to the campaign's fund, its collection belongs to the campaign's church, and its date is from the start date to the end of the end date. A gift at 11:30 PM on the end date counts. Expenses and fund transfers do not change the report. Enter each gift in a collection as usual.

💸Expenses

Expenses are any transaction that requires an outflow of funds. 

When creating an expense, note the following:
- An expense type must be chosen. If needed, you can create a new expense type.

Expense Types

Expense types are used to categorize different expenses. This allows for more fine-grained financial reporting.

When creating an expense type, note the following:
- Each expense type is linked to a specific fund, so that when expenses of this type are created, a transaction for the related fund will also automatically be created.
- Expense types can be grouped in hierarchies. For example, you could have an expense type group of 'Waste' and then have sub-types of 'Trash' and 'Recycling'.
    - In the example above, the 'Waste' expense type would have the 'group' option checked and the 'Trash' and 'Recycling' expense types would have 'Waste' selected as the 'Parent Expense Type'.

Expense types with more than one church. Expense types are shared by every church, but the fund one names may not be. If a branch is told the fund belongs to another church, either tick Shared with all churches on that fund, or give the branch an expense type of its own pointing at a fund it can use.

🫴Alms

Alms are monies given by the church to someone in need. For giving to the church, see Donations above.

When creating an alms request, note the following:
- The Recipient is the person (or family) who would receive the alms
- With more than one church, the Requestor must be a person of your church. A request for a person of another church is made by that church.
- Once the alms request is Approved or Declined, the Status field should be updated.
- Once the alms have been distributed to the Recipient:
    - The Status can be set to Distributed.
    - An associated Expense can be created by clicking the Create Expense button at the top-right of the form. This will create and submit an Expense and automatically reduce the Fund associated with that Expense. Only a user who can edit the alms request can use the button. It makes one expense for each request, in the church of the request, and it is hidden when the request has an expense.

Emails. When someone adds an alms request, the Pastoral Care team and the Church Managers of its church get an email and a desk notification. To change it, open Notification and edit New Alms Request.

Case notes. On a saved alms request, click Case Notes. View Case Notes opens the notes about the person who gets the alms. Add Case Note adds a note in the Benevolence category. For a Family, the notes go on the primary contact of the family. Only the Pastoral Care team and the Church Managers can read and add Benevolence notes. The finance team does not see these buttons.

📊Budgets

Budgets track expected expenses vs actual expenses over a given time period.

When creating a budget, note the following:
- The total budget field will auto-update after adding new budget lines
- Each budget line consists of an expense type and an budgeted amount
- Notes can be added for each budget line

Clicking 'View' -> 'Budget vs Actual' will show a report comparing the budgeted amounts verses the actual amounts spent for each expense type listed in the budget.

🏪Vendors

Vendors are payees for Expenses.
- Set a Default Expense Type so it auto-fills when the vendor is chosen on an expense.
- Optionally record an address and contact details. A vendor's expenses appear in the 'Connections' section of its form.

📒Export to your accounting system

Churchit keeps its own fund ledger. If your church also uses an accounting system, you can send it a journal of the gifts, expenses and fund transfers.
1. Open GL Account Mappings and add one mapping for each record. Mapping For tells what the record is. Record is the fund, payment type, expense type or church. Account is the account number or name exactly as your accounting system shows it.
2. Map each fund to the account its money goes to. Map each payment type (Cash, Check, Card) to the account the money comes into. Map each expense type to its expense account. Map your church to the account that pays expenses.
3. Open the GL Journal report and set From Date and To Date. Each submitted collection, expense and fund transfer in those dates gives lines whose debits equal its credits. The Memo names the source document. It never names a giver.
4. If a record has no mapping, the report shows one message that lists every record to map. Add the mappings and run the report again.
5. Use the report menu to export the journal as CSV, and import the file into your accounting system.
The journal shows only submitted documents. If you cancel a collection, expense or fund transfer after you export its dates, the journal does not show a reverse line. For a refund after an export, use a negative donation row (see Record a refund).
With more than one church, each church keeps its own mappings, and the journal shows only the churches you can see. A shared fund needs a mapping in each church that uses it.

🤝 Ministries Manual

The ministries module contains features related to functions and specific ministries of the church.

🗓️Functions

Church functions are used to track attendance and for linking to other documents.

When creating a function, note the following:
- Default function types are provided, but additional function types can be created.
    - When creating a function type, you can choose an existing function as a 'template function'. This will allow you to click the 'Fill From Template' button when creating this type of function in the future to fill in most of the function details using the template function's values. Set the function's Start Date first: the schedule moves to that date and keeps its times.
- Other documents can be linked as items in the function's schedule (i.e. Songs, Sermons, People, etc)
- Attendance can be tracked (Additional attendance types can be added if needed)
    - To pre-populate attendance for a function, you can select a 'template function' in the related function type. Then clicking the 'Fill From Template' button will pre-populate all attendance records from the template function.
- 'Headcount' is the number of people in the room, named or not. See Headcount under Check-In Station.
- To publish a function to the public website, check the 'Publish' checkbox. A published function that lasts more than one day shows on the public calendar on each day from its Start Date to its End Date.

Service plan. Plan the order of a service in the Schedule table of the function. Tick Header on a row to make it a heading that starts a part of the service, such as Worship or Response. The bulletin prints a header row as a heading.
- For a song, select the Arrangement and the Key. When you leave the Key empty, Churchit uses the key of the arrangement, else the default key of the song. When you change the song or the arrangement and leave the Key as it was, Churchit sets the key again.
- An arrangement name that the song does not have stops the save. You can only add a song that you can open.
- Displayed (ticked by default) and Printed tell how the words of a song were copied. Tick Streamed or Recorded on the function when the service went out live or was recorded. The Song Usage report counts these uses for CCLI.
- Team Notes are only for the people who serve. They see them on the Ministry Team page. The bulletin never prints them.

Link Only. Tick Link Only under Publish to keep a published function off the Calendar page, the calendar feed, the home page and the list on the Register page. To show the function, share the link https://<your site>/calendar?event=<function ID>, for example /calendar?event=FUNC-0042. A Register link with ?function=<function ID> works too. Function IDs are numbers in order, so a person can guess the link. Do not use Link Only to keep a private event secret.

Online Meeting Link. Add an https link to join the function online, for example a video call. When the function is published, everyone sees Join online on the Calendar page, and the link is in the calendar feed. The link is also in the calendar of the Ministry Team page and in the calendar file of a sign-up. Churchit refuses a link that does not start with https:// or that has a space in it.

Streamed. Tick Streamed on a function that you stream live. When the church of the function, or a church above it, has a Live Stream Link on its Church record, the public Calendar page shows Watch live on the function from 15 minutes before it starts until it ends. The button opens the /live page of that church. The Website manual explains the /live page.

On the public Calendar page, a visitor clicks a published function to see its details: the time, the address with an Open map link, the rooms of its approved Room Bookings, Join online when it has an Online Meeting Link, and Watch live while a streamed function runs. When the address has a map position, a small map shows a pin. Churchit finds the position when the address is saved, if the address lookup is on. A booking that waits for approval or that was denied does not show. With more than one published church, the page of a church that has published branch churches shows Include branch churches. Tick it to also show the functions of those branches, each with its church. The Website manual explains the Subscribe and Download .ics buttons.

Attendance Total. Only Confirmed, Assumed and Checked-In rows count. Signed-Up and Absent rows do not count in the total, in the reports or on the dashboard. A function counts its attendance only from its Start Date. You can add rows before the function, for example from a roster, but its Attendance Total stays 0 until the day of the function. Each day, Churchit updates the totals of the functions of the last week.

Who changes an attendance row. A check-in, a sign-up and a member's change on the portal Attendance page each change only that person's row on the Function. A sign-up never replaces a row that says more, such as Checked-In, Confirmed or Absent. If someone checks a person in while you have the Function open, your save stops and asks you to reload the form, so no row is lost.

The Ministries workspace includes links to function and attendance reports.

Group filter. The Church Attendance, Function Attendance by Type and Function Attendance by Person reports have a Group filter and a Group column. Choose a group to see only the attendance at the meetings of that group. Leave it empty to see every function.

Hint: in the Function list view, change the view type to 'Calendar' to view the Functions on a calendar.

📋Attendance in the Portal

Members can see their own attendance on the Attendance page of the portal. It lists every function that has them in its attendance, newest first, with their attendance type (for example Checked-In or Signed-Up).
- Functions dated after today are not shown. A recurring function copies its attendance roster onto its occurrences weeks before they happen, so those rows are not yet a record of who came.
- To let members correct their own attendance, tick Members Can Change Their Attendance in Portal Settings. Each row then offers Confirmed (I was there) and Absent (I was not there), and the function's attendance total updates to match. A Checked-In row can only be changed in the desk, on the Function.

🔁Recurring Functions

A function can repeat on a schedule (for example, a weekly Bible Study). The function you set up is the template. Each day, Churchit makes the next occurrences of the template ahead of time, for the number of weeks in Create Weeks Ahead (8 weeks by default), so they show on the calendar early. Each occurrence copies the schedule, attendance roster, team needs, associations and other details of the template. It also copies the room bookings of the template that are Requested or Approved, moved to the new date.

To make a Function repeat:
- Expand the Recurrence section and check Automatically Recreate.
- Choose a Repeat Frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly). In Repeat Every, type 1 to repeat every time, or 2 to repeat every second day, week, month or year.
- For Weekly, choose the day in Repeat Day of Week.
- For Monthly, choose Repeat Monthly By. With Day of Month, type the day in Repeat Day of Month (for example, the 15th). A day after the end of a short month moves to the last day of that month. With Weekday, choose Repeat Week of Month and Repeat Day of Week (for example, the Second Sunday, or the Last Friday).
- Optionally set a Repeat Until date to stop making occurrences after that date.
- Add a date to Skip Dates when the function does not happen on that date (for example, a joint service). When you delete an occurrence, its room bookings are also deleted and its date is added to Skip Dates, so it is not made again.
- A changed rule applies only to the dates after the latest occurrence. To change occurrences that already exist, edit or delete them.
- If a room of the template is already booked at the new time, the occurrence is made without that booking, and a comment on the occurrence names the room and the time.
- A room that needs approval gets a booking with the status Requested, which waits for approval as usual.
- Uncheck Automatically Recreate at any time to stop making occurrences.
Tip: open a Ministry and click Create Recurring Function to start a new recurring function linked to that ministry. Each ministry's Recurring Functions list shows the recurring functions of the ministry.

👥Group Meetings

A Function can be a meeting of a Group. Choose the group in the Group field of the Function. Do not add a group in Associations: Churchit does not accept a new Group row there. The group must belong to the church of the Function, and a Function with a group cannot be shared with all churches. A Function that had a group in Associations before has it in Group now. When it had more than one group, a comment on the Function names the groups that stay in Associations.

To give a group a weekly or monthly meeting, open the group and click Actions → Set Up Meetings. A new Function opens with the group, the type Group Meeting and Automatically Recreate set. Choose the day, the time and the address, and save. This Function is the schedule of the group: Churchit makes each meeting from it, and each meeting keeps the group. Record the attendance on each meeting, not on the schedule.

Did Not Meet. When a group did not meet, tick Did Not Meet on the Function of that meeting. Churchit then sends no attendance reminder for it, and the Group Attendance report does not count it. A leader can also do this on the Lead a Group page of the portal (see Lead a Group in the People manual). Attendance Recorded On shows when a leader last saved the attendance.

📝Function Sign-Ups

Function sign-ups allow people to indicate their intent to attend a function and optionally bring/complete specific items.

When creating a sign-up, note the following:
- Only Functions that have Allow Sign-Ups ticked on the Registration tab are available in the 'Function' field.
- Each person can have only one sign-up per function. A Cancelled sign-up does not count.
- The Attending checkbox tells whether the person who signs up comes. While it is ticked, that person is in the Attendees table.
    - While the sign-up is Confirmed, each attendee Person has an attendance record on the Function with the status 'Signed-Up'. A record that says more, such as Checked-In, Confirmed or Absent, stays as it is.
    - When the sign-up stops being Confirmed, an attendee is removed, or the sign-up is deleted, Churchit removes the attendance record only while it is still 'Signed-Up'.
- If the Function has sign-up items defined, you can add rows to indicate which (and how many of those) items you will bring/complete.
    - The Signed Up quantity on each item in the Function document adds up the quantities of the sign-ups that hold seats (Confirmed, or Pending and not yet expired).

📦Sign-Up Items

Sign-up items represent things that are needed for a function (e.g. food, supplies, jobs, etc). They are added to a function's Sign-Up Items table with the quantity needed. As people submit sign-ups and indicate they will bring specific items, the Quantity Signed Up on the function is automatically totaled across all sign-ups and displayed on the Function document.

🌐Guest Sign-Ups and the Register Page

A person who is not signed in can sign up for a function on the Register page of the website. To allow it, open the Function, tick Publish on the Details tab, and tick Allow Sign-Ups and Allow Guest Sign-Ups on the Registration tab. The Register page shows only published functions that start today or later. The Register page is not in the website menu. Put a link to /register on a web page, in an email or in a QR code. To add it to the menu, open Website Settings, add a row to Top Bar Items with the Label Register and the URL /register, and save.

A guest types a name and an email, and can add a phone number and notes. A signed-in member signs up as their own person. A guest's sign-up has no Person at first. A moment later Churchit links it to the one person whose name and email or phone match. If nobody matches, Churchit adds the guest as a Visitor with the Source Sign-Up, and records possible duplicates for staff. If the matched person is already signed up for the function, the new sign-up stays without a Person and gets a comment that names the other sign-up. A guest never sees the name of anyone else.

Bringing others. On the Register page, a signed-in member can tick the people of their own household who come too. Anyone can click Add a person and type the name of someone else who comes. Each person takes a seat. When the function has selections, each person chooses one, and the registrant chooses their own. A registration can bring at most 20 other people.

🚦Status and Open and Close Dates

Each sign-up has a status: Pending, Confirmed, Waitlisted or Cancelled. Only Churchit sets it. To cancel a sign-up, open it and click Cancel Registration. A cancelled sign-up keeps its record, and the person can sign up again.

On the Registration tab, Sign-Ups Open On and Sign-Ups Close On set when members and guests can sign up. Leave a date blank for no limit. Outside these dates, the members' sign-up form and the Register page do not offer the function, and a member can still cancel. Staff can add a sign-up in the desk at any time.

🎟️Capacity, Waitlist and Approval

Capacity on the Registration tab is the number of people who can come. Leave it at 0 for no limit. Add Selections when people choose an option, such as Adult or Child, and give each one a price and a capacity if it needs them. When a function has selections, each attendee must choose one. On a sign-up, the Attendees table lists the people who come. A sign-up that only brings items, with Attending unticked, takes no seat. In the Sign-Up Items table of the Function, tick Limited on an item to refuse sign-ups when its Quantity Needed is reached.

Waitlist. When a capacity is full, a new sign-up gets the status Waitlisted, and the whole party waits together. A party that is larger than a capacity cannot sign up. When seats become free, Churchit gives them to the oldest waiting party that fits. Seats become free when a sign-up is cancelled, declined or deleted, when a party removes an attendee, when a payment hold ends, or when you raise a capacity. If you move a sign-up to another function, it counts as a new sign-up there. When that function is full, the sign-up is Waitlisted, and its seats in the first function go to the waitlist of that function.

Approval. Tick Sign-Ups Need Approval on the Registration tab to approve each sign-up yourself. A new sign-up is then Pending and keeps its seats until you decide. Open the sign-up and click Approval, then Approve or Decline. Only a Church Manager or a Church People Editor can approve or decline. An approved sign-up is Confirmed, or Pending with a payment hold when an amount is due. A declined sign-up is Cancelled, and its seats go to the waitlist. When an approved sign-up adds attendees or items, it is Pending again and waits for your approval.

💲Prices, Discounts and Payments

Prices. Give each Selection a price for each attendee, and give a Sign-Up Item a price for each one. Churchit works out the Amount Due of a sign-up on each save: the price of each attendee's selection, plus the price of each item times its quantity, less the discount. The registrant cannot change a price.

Discounts. Add rows to Discounts on the Registration tab. A Code discount applies when the registrant types its code in Discount Code on the Register page. Capital letters do not matter. An Early Bird discount applies to sign-ups made on or before its Valid Until date. A Group discount applies when a sign-up brings at least its Minimum Attendees. Use it only on a function whose selections have a price. A Freely Given discount applies to every sign-up. Each row takes its Amount off, or its Percent when Amount is empty. A Valid Until date ends any discount after that day. When more than one discount applies, only the largest one comes off. The amount due is never below 0. Registrants never see the codes.

Freely given. "Freely you have received; freely give" (Matthew 10:8). When a function has a price, its form asks you to consider giving it freely. Click Offer It Freely to add a Freely Given discount of 100 percent and save the function. Every sign-up is then free, sign-ups that wait for payment are confirmed, the Register page shows each price as freely given, and the function needs no Sign-Up Fund. With a Valid Until date the function is free only until that day, so it still needs a fund.

Payments. When a function has a price and is not freely given, choose a Sign-Up Fund on the Registration tab. The fund must belong to the church of the function, or be shared. A sign-up with an amount due is Pending and holds its seats for 24 hours. The registrant gets an email with a Pay now link, and the Register page shows the link after they sign up. When the payment arrives, Churchit adds a Collection with one row for the fund and confirms the sign-up. The row has no person, so the payment is not on a giving statement. If the sign-up is not paid in 24 hours, Churchit cancels it and gives its seats to the waitlist. If your church offers no online payment method, the email asks the registrant to pay at the church office.

Offline payments and refunds. When a person pays in cash or by check, open the sign-up and click Payment, then Record Offline Payment. Put the money in the weekly collection. If a payment arrives after the hold ended and the seats are gone, or a paid sign-up is cancelled or declined, the sign-up gets the payment status Refund Due and Church Finance gets an alert. Only a Church Manager can delete a paid sign-up. Refund the payment at the payment processor, then open the sign-up and click Payment, then Record Refund. Only Church Finance and Church Manager users can record payments and refunds. A paid sign-up cannot add anything that costs more, and cannot move to another function. Make a new sign-up instead.

✉️Questions, Emails and Reports

Questions. To ask questions when people register, make a Question Set and choose it in Question Set on the Registration tab. A function can use a question set of its own church or a shared one. The Register page asks the questions once for the registrant and once for each person they bring. A required question must be answered for each person, or the registration is not saved. You see the answers in the Answers section of each sign-up and in the Function Registrations report. If you change the questions later, sign-ups made before keep their answers. Staff cannot type answers in the desk, and the members' sign-up form does not ask the questions. To add a sign-up for a function with required questions, use the Register page.

Confirmation. When a sign-up becomes Confirmed, Churchit emails the registrant a confirmation with a calendar file of the function. It goes to the primary email of the person. A guest sign-up that has no person yet gets it at the email the guest typed. It is sent each time a sign-up becomes Confirmed: when it is confirmed, approved, paid or moved off the waitlist, again after it waited for payment or approval a second time, and when staff move it to another function. Pending and Waitlisted sign-ups get no confirmation. After a person registers, the Register page shows an Add to calendar link. To change the text, edit the Email Template Sign-Up Confirmation. To stop the email, delete that template. The Email Template Sign-Up Payment Due holds the text of the payment email. Churchit sends these emails only when the site has an outgoing email account.

Reminders. Set Reminder Days on the Registration tab to the number of days before the function to send a reminder. 0 sends no reminder. Each day, the registrant of each Confirmed sign-up of a function that starts within Reminder Days gets one reminder by email. If the site can send texts, a registrant who agreed to texts for Event Registrations also gets one text. A registrant who withdrew consent for Event Registrations gets no reminder. The other attendees get no reminder. To change the words of the email, edit the Email Template Sign-Up Reminder.

Reports. The Function Registrations report shows one row for each person who comes, with the sign-up, their selection, the status and the payment. Choose a function to see a column for each of its questions with the answer of each person. You can also filter by status. The Function Sign-Up Items report shows the items, with the name a guest typed for a guest sign-up.

More than one church. A sign-up belongs to the church of its function. The sign-ups of a function that is shared with all churches are shared too, so every church sees them.

📋Question Sets

A Question Set is a list of questions that your church asks people, for example when they register. The answers stay on the record that the person filled in, so you read them there.

To make a Question Set, open Question Sets (in the Settings workspace, under Forms) and click Add Question Set. Give it a Title. Then add one row for each question:
- Question: the question as people read it, such as T-shirt size.
- Answer Type: a line of text (Data), a paragraph (Small Text), a choice from a list (Select), a tick (Check), a date, a whole number (Int), an email address or a phone number.
- Required: tick this when people must answer. A required tick must be ticked, for example to agree to the rules of a camp.
- Choices: for a Select question, type each choice on a new line.

Each question gets an Answer Name, made from the question. Two questions in one set cannot have the same Answer Name. Do not change an Answer Name after people answer, because their answers are kept under it.

A question cannot depend on the answer to another question. To ask different people different questions, make a second Question Set.

Each answer keeps the question as it was asked, so a later change to the set does not change an old answer. A tick shows as Yes or No. A question that someone leaves blank shows no answer.

When you anonymize a person, the answers on the records that are theirs are removed, with the change history of those records. Answers on a record for a group, where the person is only one of the people listed, stay. A function sign-up is different: the answers of the person are removed, and the answers of the other people on the sign-up stay. When you merge two people, the answers stay.

In multi-church mode, a Question Set belongs to one church. Tick Shared with All Churches to let every church use it.

📝Forms

To use a Question Set as a form on the website, tick Publish as a Form. Anyone can then answer it at /form/new?question_set= followed by the name of the set, such as QSET-0001. Copy this address into a web page, an email or a QR code. The form asks for a first name, a last name and an email address, and then the questions of the set. When Publish as a Form is not ticked, the form says that it is not open.

Tick Members Only to let only signed-in members answer. A member's answer is linked to their own person record. Other people are asked to log in as a member.

Each answer is a Form Response, in the Settings workspace under Forms. After a guest sends a form, Churchit finds the person who has the same name and email, or adds them as a Visitor with the Source Form. Then the response shows Processed. To follow up a form, add a trigger Form Submitted to a Follow-up Workflow and choose the Question Set. Each response to that set opens one card for the person.

Before you charge for a form, remember "Freely you have received; freely give" (Matthew 10:8): a form with no Price is free. To charge for a form, give the Question Set a Price and a Fund of its church or a shared one. After a person sends the form, they go to the payment page. When the payment arrives, Churchit adds a Collection with one row for the fund, and the response shows Paid. After you refund a payment at the payment processor, open the Form Response and click Record Refund. Only Church Finance and Church Manager users can do this.

In multi-church mode, a response belongs to the church of its Question Set. A response to a shared Question Set is shared too, so every church sees it, and the own church of the person follows it up.

🙋Ministry Teams

A ministry team is the people who serve in a ministry, such as greeters, ushers or nursery workers. Churchit asks each person by email, or by text, if they can serve at a function, and keeps their answer.

🧩Positions

A Ministry Team Position is one job on a ministry's team (e.g. Greeter in the Hospitality ministry).
- Add the people who can fill the position under People. Only these people can be assigned to it.
- Check Needs Background Check when the position needs a current, cleared Background Check (e.g. Nursery Worker).
- Under Required Checks, pick the checks the position needs, for example Criminal Background and Child Safety Training. Each one must be cleared and still current on the day of the function. Leave it blank to accept any cleared check.
- Tick Block People Without Checks to refuse an assignment for someone without the checks. Without it, you only get a warning.
- When you add someone to the position's People who does not have the checks, you get a warning, but they stay on the list.
- Each row of People has Most Times a Month. 0 means no limit. When a person already has this many Pending or Accepted assignments to the position in the month of a function, a new assignment shows a warning. Members set this number themselves on the Ministry Team page, under How often you serve.
- On the Ministry, set the Team Coordinator, who gets an email when someone declines, and Remind Days Before: how many days before a function the people serving get a reminder. 0 sends no reminders. The ministry's positions show under Connections.

📋Team Needs and Assignments

On a Function, list the positions it needs, and how many people each needs, in the Ministry Team section. Recurring functions and Fill from Template copy these needs from the template. Notes on a need is a note to the people in that position. Each person sees only the note of their own position, on the Ministry Team page.
To ask a person to serve, make a Ministry Team Assignment from the Connections of the function: choose the position and the person. A warning shows when:
- a Ministry Team Block covers the date of the function, also a block that repeats
- the person already serves at another function at the same time
- the position needs a background check and the person has no current, cleared one
- the person already has their Most Times a Month for the position
- the person has no email address
A warning does not stop the save. The function, position and person cannot change after the save: delete the assignment and make a new one.

Fill Open Needs. To ask people to fill the open places of a function, click Ministry Team → Fill Open Needs on the Function. The Ministry Team Schedule and Ministry Team Grid reports have a Fill Open Needs button too. It fills every function in the dates, church and ministry of the filters.
- For each position, it takes people from the People of the position. It asks first the person who served longest ago, or never.
- It does not ask a person who is blocked on that date, who serves at the same time, who does not have a check the position needs, who already has their Most Times a Month, or who has died. It asks a person for one position of a function only.
- It does not ask a person again for a place they were already asked about, also when they declined.
- Each new assignment is Pending, and the daily job sends the requests. After a person declines, click it again to fill the place.
- It does not fill a function that has started.

✉️Emails and Answers

Once a day, each person gets one email that lists their new assignments. To send the waiting emails at once, click Send Requests Now on the Ministry Team Assignment list. Each assignment has a link to accept or decline it, with an optional reason. The link works without signing in. Members can also answer on the Ministry Team page of the portal, where they see the Schedule of each function they serve at. While they have not declined, the schedule also shows the key and arrangement of each song, the team notes, the note for their own position and the rehearsal media of the songs. A person can change their answer until the function starts.
- The Status of the assignment shows the answer: Pending, Accepted or Declined.
- When someone declines, the Team Coordinator of the ministry gets an email, and the place is open again in the schedule.
- A reminder goes out the ministry's Remind Days Before ahead of the function, to each person who has not declined.
- No email goes out until the site has an outgoing Email Account. Change the words of the four emails in Email Templates: Ministry Team Request, Ministry Team Reminder, Ministry Team Decline and Ministry Team Replacement.

Texts. If your site can send texts (a gateway in SMS Settings), each request and reminder also goes by text. A person gets the text only when they gave text consent for the Serving purpose. The text has the link to accept or decline. The emails go as before, to everyone asked to serve, when the site has an outgoing Email Account. If the site can send only texts, the requests still go by text. When you click Send Requests Now and none of your roles is in the Allowed Roles of SMS Settings, Churchit sends nothing to the people who get texts. The daily job sends them the email and the text.

To write to everyone serving at a function, click Ministry Team → Email the Team on the Function. The email goes to each person asked to serve who has not declined. They are in BCC, so no one sees the others' email addresses. The email is kept on the function's timeline, and a message names anyone without an email address.

🔄Replacement Requests

A volunteer who cannot come can ask someone else to take their place. On the Ministry Team page, or on the page that the request email links to, they click Ask Someone Else and choose a person. The list shows only the people of the position who are free then: no block, no other assignment at the same time, the checks the position needs, and not at their Most Times a Month. People already asked for this place are not on the list.
- The person they choose gets a request at once. The volunteer still serves until that person accepts. A volunteer can have only one request waiting at a time.
- When the person accepts, the volunteer's assignment changes to Declined with the reason Replaced by and the name, and the Team Coordinator gets the decline email, which names the person who takes the place.
- When the person declines, nothing changes for the volunteer, and the Team Coordinator gets no email.
- Either way, the volunteer gets the Ministry Team Replacement email.
- The Replaces field of the new assignment shows which assignment it replaces. You cannot set it by hand.

📅Calendar

Members can see the functions they accepted in the calendar on their phone or computer. On the Ministry Team page of the portal, they click Make a calendar link, then Subscribe, or copy the link into their calendar app (in Google Calendar: Other calendars, From URL). The calendar keeps itself up to date.
Anyone with the link can see where the member serves. Stop this link and make a new one replaces a link that was shared by mistake.

🚫Blocked Dates

A Ministry Team Block holds the dates a person cannot serve, such as a vacation. Check Whole Household to block everyone in their family. Members add and remove their own blocks on the Ministry Team page of the portal. On a site with branch churches, only the person's own church can add a Ministry Team Block for them. Staff at another church ask that church, or the person adds the block on the Ministry Team page.
Repeating blocks. A block can repeat. Set Repeat to Weekly, Every Other Week or Monthly on the Same Weekday. Monthly uses the weekday and the week of the month of From Date, for example the first Sunday. A block that starts on a fifth Sunday covers only the months that have a fifth Sunday. The dates from From Date to To Date repeat until Repeat Until. Leave Repeat Until empty to repeat with no end. A repeating block must end before it repeats: for example, a weekly block can be at most 7 days long. On the Ministry Team page, members choose Repeat and an optional Repeat until date when they add a block. Blocks only warn. They do not stop an assignment.

🗓️Ministry Team Schedule

The Ministry Team Schedule report shows the positions of each function in a date range: how many people each needs, how many accepted or did not answer yet, how many places are open, and who was asked. Check Only Open Positions to see the gaps. On a Function, Ministry Team → Schedule opens the report for that function.

The Ministry Team Grid report shows several weeks at once: one row for each position, grouped by ministry, and one column for each function that has team needs or assignments. Each cell shows the open places in red and the people asked: green for Accepted, orange for Pending and gray for Declined. Click an open place to make an assignment for that function and position. The grid shows 8 weeks by default, and at most 60 functions. For a longer range, choose shorter dates.

Each person's Last Served and Times Served (90 Days), on the Connections tab of the Person form, count the functions where they accepted an assignment. Pending and declined requests do not count. Filter the Person list on them to find, for example, people who have not served lately.

✅Function Check-Ins

Function check-ins are a record that a person has physically arrived at a function.

When creating a check-in, note the following:
- Each person can only be checked in once per function.
- Each check-in has a Kind: Regular, Guest or Volunteer. A visitor added at the Check-In Station is a Guest. A Volunteer check-in gets no security code, does not count in the room capacity and does not start a First Check-In follow-up card. The attendance row is Checked-In for every kind.
- Upon saving, the person's attendance record on the function will automatically be set to Checked-In.
- Deleting a check-in will remove the associated Checked-In attendance record from the function. A check-in of a person who was checked out or collected cannot be deleted.
- The Kind cannot be changed after the check-in is saved.
- The checkout fields (Collected By, Collected At, Released By, Override Reason, Overridden By and the Security Code) are set only at the Check-In Station. A check-in created here gets no security code, so use the Check-In Station for children who need a pickup tag.
- When 'Room Capacity' in Check-In Settings is Block, a check-in created here is refused when the room is full.

🖥️Check-In Station

The Check-In Station is the quickest way to check people in as they arrive:
1. Choose the Function at the top of the page. The station remembers the last function used on that computer and otherwise picks the next function on the calendar. The arrow in the field (or the function name above the Checked In list) opens the Function itself.
2. Type part of a name, a nickname, a family name, or a phone number. Results are grouped by family so parents and children can be checked in together. People who have a 'Death' life event are not listed.
3. Tap the people who have arrived (or Select all for a family) and click Check In. Pressing Enter checks in a single match straight away.
4. First-time visitors: click New Visitor (or the Add them as a new visitor link when a search finds no one), confirm the name and phone taken from what you typed, and click Add & Check In. Churchit first looks for people who are already in Churchit with the same name and phone number (see New Visitors below). A new visitor is created and checked in as a Guest. Their 'Source' is Check-In and their 'Membership' is Visitor, so the member directory does not list them.
The Checked In list on the right shows everyone at the function (see below). Untick Print name tags to check people in without printing.
The station is also reachable from the Check-In Station button on any Function.

Grade, allergies and medical notes. Each person in the search results shows their age, their grade and, in red, their allergies. A stethoscope icon shows that the person has medical notes or an emergency contact. Tap the icon to read them. The tap does not select the person. Only users who can see Medical Notes on the Person (Church Check-In, Church People Editor and Church Manager) see the icon.

New Visitors. When you add a new visitor, the station first looks for people who are already in Churchit with the same first and last name, or with a similar name and the same phone number. If it finds someone, it shows them with their family and age. Click Check In next to the right person to check them in as a Guest. No new person is added. Click Add as New Visitor to add a new person. Type the phone number too: it helps the station find a person whose name is spelled differently. A visitor who has no household adult in Churchit, such as a visiting child, can have an emergency contact: open Emergency Contact in the New Visitor window and type the name and phone number.

Checked In list. The list on the right shows everyone checked in to the function, newest first, with their photo. It updates every 10 seconds while the page is open, so you also see the check-ins and checkouts of other stations. It stops when the browser tab is hidden or you go to another page. Each row shows In, Collected by the person who collected the child, or Checked out, with the time. The names of the booked rooms show under the function name. 🎵Songs

Songs can be added as items to a church function.
To show a song on a screen, open the song and click Present. You can also add the song as a slide to a sermon and leave its fields empty: the presentation then shows each slide of the song.

When you create a song, note the following:
- If you know the CCLI number of the song, add it. The Song Usage report shows it.
- Authors: the writers of the words and the music.
- Copyright: the copyright line of the song, as the publisher gives it.
- Tick Public Domain only when the words and the music are free of copyright. Visitors who are not signed in see the words of a song only when Public Domain is ticked. For any other song they see 'This slide is not public'.
- Default Key: the key that the team usually plays the song in. Tempo (BPM) is the speed in beats per minute. Time Signature is for example 4/4 or 6/8.
- Themes: add tags to the song in the sidebar of the form, for example Advent or Easter. To find the songs of a theme, filter the Song list by the tag.
- You can add any number of slides to a song.
- The last slide of a song shows its copyright and its CCLI number with your church's CCLI License Number. Add the license number on your church. It is the number of your CCLI Church Copyright License.
- After you create a song, you can add it to the Schedule of a function.

Arrangements. An arrangement is one way that the team plays a song. Each arrangement has a name, a key, a tempo, notes and a ChordPro chart. Two arrangements of one song cannot have the same name. In a ChordPro chart, put each chord in square brackets before the syllable that you sing on it, for example [G]Amazing [D/F#]grace. Start a section with a line such as Verse 1 or Chorus, or put it between {start_of_verse} and {end_of_verse}. Leave a blank line between sections.

Chord Chart. Save the song, then click Chord Chart. Select an arrangement and a key. The chart shows each chord above its words. When you select a different key, Churchit moves every chord to that key, with the sharps or flats of the key. A chart in a major key moves only to major keys, and a chart in a minor key only to minor keys. To change the key, the arrangement or the song must have a key. Church Staff can open the chord chart.

Make Slides from ChordPro. Click Make Slides from ChordPro and select an arrangement. Churchit makes one slide for each section of the chart, with the words and no chords. This replaces all the slides of the song, so Churchit asks you first. The chart is the source of the slides: when you change the chart, make the slides again. Only a user who can edit the song can make slides.

Rehearsal media. Add chord charts, lead sheets, audio and video in the Media table. Attach a file or type a link. When you set Arrangement, it must be the name of an arrangement of the song. Leave it empty when the media is for every arrangement. The people who serve at a function see the media of each song on its schedule on their Ministry Team page, until the function ends. A media link that they saved works until 7 days after the function. They do not see the media when they declined, and they cannot open the songs themselves. A file downloads; a link opens.

Import song files. In the Song list, click Import Song Files. Select up to 50 ChordPro files (.cho, .chordpro, .chopro or .pro) or SongSelect files (.usr), each 1 MB or smaller, and click Import. Churchit makes one song for each file, with an arrangement named Default and one slide for each section. It copies the title, the CCLI number, the authors, the copyright, the key and the tempo. The themes of the file become tags. A SongSelect .usr file has no chords, so its Default arrangement holds only the words. When your list already shows a song with the same CCLI number, or the same title when the file has no CCLI number, Churchit skips the file. A song of another church that is not shared does not count. After the import, Churchit shows the songs it made and the files it skipped. Churchit then deletes the uploaded files. To get a .usr file, download the song from CCLI SongSelect.

Add public-domain hymns. Churchit has 33 hymns whose words and music were published in 1930 or earlier, by writers who died in 1955 or earlier. In the Song list, click Add Public-Domain Hymns. Select the hymns you want and click Add. Churchit makes one song for each hymn, with Public Domain ticked, an arrangement named Default with the chord chart, and one slide for each verse and chorus. The chords are on the first verse and the chorus. When your song list already shows a song with the same title, Churchit skips the hymn. The hymns come from WorshipCommons (worshipcommons.org). The songs are yours: you can change or delete them.

Export songs. To export one song, open it and click Export. To export more songs, select them in the Song list and click Actions, then Export Songs. Select the format. OpenLyrics opens in FreeShow, OpenLP and Quelea, and has the words without chords. ChordPro is the chart as you typed it, with the chords. Churchit uses the first arrangement of each song that has words in its ChordPro chart. You can export up to 200 songs at a time. More than one song downloads as a zip file. A song with no such arrangement is not exported, and the zip file has a list of these songs.

The Song Usage report counts how many times each song is on the Schedule of a function in a date range, with its CCLI number and the date it was last used. It also counts in how many of these uses the words were displayed or printed, and in how many the function was streamed or recorded. Tick Displayed and Printed on the schedule row, and Streamed and Recorded on the function. CCLI has no public reporting API, so copy these counts into your CCLI report. The report opens on the last six months.

🧠Bible Memory

The Bible Memory feature in the portal provides an interface for users to memorize Scripture. Each person has a personalized list  which tracked as Bible Memory Items, and every completed practice run is logged as a Memory Session.

Memorizing on the Portal

From the portal menu, click "Bible Memory"

Create a Memory Goal:
- Click + Add passage, type a passage such as John 3:16-18, choose a translation, and click Add to my list. Only translations whose text is ready are offered.
- Select "Hide memorized" to filter out already memorized verses.

Memory Modes:
- Use "Blur" mode to become familiarize with the reference text.
    - Click "+ Increase Blur" to blur out a few words at a time.
    - When finished, click "Done" to increase memory progress by 5% (99% Maximum - 'Type' mode must be used to increase memorization % to 100.
- Use the "Type" mode to type the first letter of each word.
    - A perfect run (zero mistakes) awards 50% progress and increments the "perfect runs" counter
    - Under three mistakes awards 10% toward the memorization progress.
    - Mistakes subtract 2% progress for each mistake.

Passages from a group. If a manager assigned passages to you through a group, choose the group at the top of the Bible Memory page to see only those passages. Choose All groups to see all of your passages again.
Reveal. In Blur mode, press and hold the eyes button to see all words. When you let go, the words that you blurred are blurred again.

Assigning Passages for Memorization

Church Managers can add a passage to one or more users' memory lists. This is useful for weekly memory verses or small-group assignments.

From the Bible Memory Item list:
1. Click 'Assign a Passage'.
2. Type the passage and choose its translation.
3. Pick a Group, a User, or both. When a Group is chosen, everyone in the group who has a portal login is assigned the passage.

Notes on assignment:
- You can assign a passage only in a translation you can read, and only to people of your own churches.
- Each assignment creates a Bible Memory Item stamped with Assigned By, which shows up under the reference on the recipient's Bible Memory list.
- If a passage is already in the user's list in that translation, the assignment is skipped.
- When assigning to a Group, if the Person record has no linked 'Portal User' listed, a follow-up dialog will be displayed showing which persons don't have user accounts yet.
- When assigning to a User, if a person is not available, that means the person has not been invited to the portal yet and doesn't have a linked User account (i.e. they don't have an account to log into the portal).

Group assignments. When you assign a passage to a Group, each item that the assignment adds for a member of the group keeps the group. A member who already has the passage keeps the item that they have, and that item gets no group. A user that you name in User and who is not a member of the group gets no group. You cannot set or change the group of an item by hand. Only a group assignment sets it. When you delete a group, its members keep their passages, and the passages show no group.

Reporting

The Bible Memory report lists memory progress, with filters for Memorized (Yes, No or all), a User and a Group. To see the progress of a group:
1. Open the Bible Memory report.
2. In Group, select the group.
3. To see all passages, clear Memorized.
The Group filter shows only the passages that the group assignment added. A member who had the passage before the assignment does not show in the group. To see that member, clear Group and select the user. If you cannot open the group, the report shows an error.
If your user is limited to some churches, the report shows only the passages of users whose person record is in those churches. Otherwise it shows all passages, also the passages of users with no person record.
The References Memorized card on the Study workspace tracks total memorizations over time.

📝Sermons

A Sermon is a collection of slides that can be presented at a church event. Each sermon has a title, an optional 'Prepared By' field linking to a Person, and optional notes.

When creating a sermon, note the following:
- Add slides to the 'Slides' table to build your sermon content. Each slide shows a document, such as a Belief or a Song, or a passage of Scripture.
- For a Scripture slide, leave the Slide Type empty and type the passage in Scripture, such as Psalm 23. Choose a translation, or leave it blank for your church's default. The slide shows the verses in that translation. On the public website, a translation your church imported shows its reference only.
- For a document slide, you can select which fields from the linked document to show on the slide.
  - You may choose a field to use as the slide title by checking the 'Title' box.
  - If no fields are selected, the slide will show the linked document's name as the title. A Song slide with no fields selected shows each slide of the song, then a slide with its copyright and CCLI numbers. Visitors who are not signed in see a song only when Public Domain is ticked on it.
- The 'Presentation History' table tracks when and where the sermon has been presented, who presented it, and optionally includes a recording of the sermon.

Presenting a Sermon

To present a sermon, click the 'Present' button on the sermon document:
- Use the arrow keys, or spacebar to navigate between slides.
- Press Escape or click the `X` button to exit the presentation.

Example Workflow

1. The Pastor creates a new Sermon and adds Scripture slides and slides showing Beliefs, Songs, etc.
2. He adds per-slide notes to show in the presentation, and general sermon notes. The general notes are not presented, but they show on the website when the sermon is published.
3. On Sunday, he clicks 'Present' to display the sermon.
4. After the service, a row is added to 'Presentation History' table with the date, presenter, location, and optionally an audio/video recording of the sermon.

✍️Sermon Handouts

A Sermon Handout is a fill-in-the-blank outline of a sermon for listeners. It is printed as the sermon notes insert of a Bulletin whose function has the sermon on its schedule.

To create a handout, open the sermon and choose 'Create -> Create Handout'. The handout starts as a copy of the sermon's notes and is linked from the sermon's 'Handout' field.
- Wrap a word or phrase in square brackets, like [grace], to print it as a blank for listeners to fill in. The bracketed text stays visible in the handout itself, so it doubles as the answer key.
- Keep the handout to about half a page; the bulletin insert has fixed space.

🌐Sermons on the Website

Each published sermon has its own page on the website, at /sermon/ followed by the name of the sermon. Anyone with the link can open it. On the Sermons page, the title of each sermon opens its page. The sermon page shows the sermon date, the Scripture passage, the preacher, the series, the video, the audio recording and the notes. The notes of a published sermon are public: the Sermons page, the home page and the sermon page show them. If the sermon belongs to a church that is not published, its page shows "Page not found".

On the Sermon form, note the following:
1. Sermon Date is the date when the sermon was first preached. If you leave it empty, Churchit sets it from the earliest date in Presentation History.
2. Scripture is the passage of the sermon, such as John 3:16-21. Separate passages with a semicolon. Churchit tidies the reference when you save.
3. Video Link is an https link to the video of the sermon. A video on Let's Church, YouTube or PeerTube plays on the sermon page. Any other link shows as a Watch the video link. A link that does not start with https:// cannot be saved.
4. When you change the title of a published sermon, the address of its page changes too. Churchit adds a redirect from the old address to the new one, so old links keep working. You find these redirects in Website Settings under Route Redirects. A sermon that is not published gets no redirect.

Search. The Sermons page shows the newest sermons first, by Sermon Date. Type part of a title or a passage, such as John, in the search box and click Search. Click Show all to see all sermons again.

Series images. A published Sermon Series shows its Series Image at the top of its card on the Sermons page. The image is a public file. An image that you attached before this change is private and does not show. Remove it from the series and attach it again.

Notes for listeners. When a sermon has a Handout, the sermon page shows the handout as Sermon Notes in place of the sermon notes. Each word in [square brackets] becomes an empty box that the listener fills in. The bracketed words do not show on the sermon page. Below the outline is a My Notes box. What a listener types stays in the browser on their own device, and Churchit does not receive it. Print prints only the notes. Copy puts the filled outline and the notes on the clipboard.

🎧Sermon Podcast

The website has a podcast feed of your sermons at /podcast.xml. Podcast apps such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify and AntennaPod read this feed. The Sermons page has a Subscribe to the podcast link to it.

The feed shows each published sermon that has public audio. When a sermon has no audio of its own, the feed uses the recording of its newest presentation in Presentation History, with the date of that presentation. When you publish a sermon, Churchit makes its audio and each presentation recording public. When you unpublish it, Churchit makes them private again. A recording that you added before this change becomes public the next time you save the published sermon.

The feed uses the name, About, Image and Email of the Church. Use a square image of 1400 to 3000 pixels, because Apple Podcasts refuses a smaller one, and attach it as a public file. If the image is private, the feed shows the Churchit icon. With more than one church, each church has its own feed, and the feed of a church also shows the sermons that are shared with all churches. To add the podcast to Apple Podcasts or Spotify, copy the address of the Subscribe link and add it in Apple Podcasts Connect or Spotify for Creators.

Make the Church image public. When you attach the image to the Image of the Church, clear Private in the upload window before you click Upload. An image that is already attached is usually private. To make the image public, open the File list, open the image by its file name, clear Is Private and save. The Church then uses the public file. Anyone who has the link to a public file can open it. 📰Bulletins

A Bulletin is the printed handout for one function: a Letter sheet printed on both sides and folded in half. The front cover carries the church name, the function name, the date, a cover image, and a verse of the week. The inside and back hold whichever sections you switch on.

To create a bulletin, open the function and choose 'Create -> Bulletin', or add a Bulletin and pick the function. The new bulletin starts with:
- The sections switched on in Bulletin Settings. Switch any of them off for this bulletin only; a section with nothing to print is left out automatically.
- The Missionary of the Week, picked in turn (week by week, in title order) from the missionaries whose Status is not Inactive and whose support has not ended. Change it for this bulletin if you like. Missionaries marked as sensitive are never picked, and are refused, unless 'Include Sensitive Missionaries' is on in Bulletin Settings.
- Every open prayer request that is not private. Remove the rows you do not want printed; private requests cannot be added.
- Attach a cover image, type a verse of the week, such as John 3:16, and write the welcome message and announcements.

More than one church. Add a row under Sections Per Church in Bulletin Settings to give a church its own bulletin. A row replaces the whole set for that church rather than changing one part of it, so tick every section you want in it; a new row starts with the same options as the settings above. The Church Roles list is shared: it says which job titles the leadership section prints, such as Pastor or Elder. Who appears under them is always that church's own people, so each congregation's bulletin names its own pastor.

What the automatic sections print:
- Order of Worship: the function's schedule, with times and the titles of linked songs, sermons and other items.
- Church Leadership: the church roles chosen in Bulletin Settings, with the people who hold those positions on the function date.
- Upcoming: functions in the days after the function date (how many days is set in Bulletin Settings).
- Ministries: active ministries and their mission statements.
- Birthdays and Anniversaries: from the function date for the number of days set in Bulletin Settings, for active members or everyone. Names and days only; no ages or years. People with 'Hide from Directory and Bulletin' ticked on their Person record are left out, and so is a couple's anniversary when either of them is. People who have a 'Death' life event are left out too, and so is a couple's anniversary when either spouse has died.
- Church Verse: the verse set on the Church record, printed at the foot of the back cover. Like the verse of the week, it prints with its words in the church's Default Bible Translation once that translation has its text, and as the reference alone until then.
- Church Image: the image (such as a logo) set on the Church record, printed above the church name on the front cover.
- Contact Information: the church address, phone, email and website from the Church record. A branch that leaves its website blank prints its parent church's website. A blank phone or email falls back to Contact Us Settings.
- Sermon Handout Insert: when a sermon on the schedule has a Sermon Handout, a third sheet prints it twice, to cut into two half-page inserts.

Printing: click 'Print Bulletin' and download the PDF. Print it double-sided on Letter paper, flipped on the short edge, and fold it in half. Each panel has fixed space, so shorten a section or switch one off if text is cut off. For the handout sheet, print half as many copies and cut them down the dashed line.

Share a bulletin online. Tick Show in Portal on a bulletin to share it with your members. Members who log in see it on the Bulletins page. They can read it in the browser or download the PDF. A member sees only the bulletins of their own church. Visitors who are not logged in go to the login page first.

The church image. When a bulletin shows the church image, it uses the Image of the Church. Members see this image on the digital bulletin and in the PDF that they download only when it is a public file. When staff print the bulletin, the image always shows. To make the image public, open the File list, open the image by its file name, clear Is Private and save. When you attach a new image to the Church, clear Private in the upload window before you click Upload.

Public announcements. To show the announcements to everyone, tick Show in Portal and Show Announcements on the Website. The public Announcements page shows the announcements of the five newest bulletins that have both boxes ticked. It shows nothing else from the bulletin: no welcome, no prayer requests, no birthdays and no order of worship. Each image that you add to a bulletin is a public file, so members see it on the digital bulletin. Anyone who has the link to an image can open it. If the announcements hold an image that you added before you ticked the box, remove it and add it again. The website menu has no Announcements item by itself. The Website manual tells how to add one with the URL /announcements.

🖥️Presentations

When creating a sermon, note the following:
- Add as many slides as needed containing the content you would like to display.
- You may want to add the sermon as an item in a church event's schedule.

Presentations on the public website. A visitor who is not signed in sees only Scripture slides, published Beliefs, and published Missionaries that are not marked Sensitive. A Belief or Missionary slide shows only the fields that the Beliefs and Missions pages show. Every other slide, such as a Song or a Person, shows "This slide is not public". ✉️Church Emails

A Church Email sends one message to many people at once, by email or by text message. Each person gets their own copy, so no one sees the other addresses or numbers. Only a Church Manager can send one. Other staff can read the ones that were sent.

To send one:
1. Add a Church Email. You can also open a Group and click 'Actions -> Send Church Email', or tick people in the Person list and click 'Actions -> New Church Email'.
2. In 'Send As', choose Email or Text Message.
3. In 'Send To', choose who gets it:
    - Active People: everyone whose Status is Active, whatever their Membership.
    - Active Members: only the people whose Membership is Member and whose Status is Active.
    - Group: the members of one Group, whatever their Status.
    - Smart List: the people on a saved list of people or a saved Attendance Count. Churchit reads the list when it sends the message.
    - Chosen People: only the people you put in 'People'.
    Check 'Include Their Households' to also send the message to the people in the Family of each person above. In 'Household Members', choose Everyone, Adults, or Adults and Other Adults. Churchit adds only living people of the message's church, and each person gets one copy. Each added person must also allow the purpose.
4. In 'Purpose', choose what the message is about, for example Church News or Pastoral Care. Only people who allow that purpose get it.
5. For an email, write the Subject and the Message. To start from an Email Template, pick it in 'Template'. This fills in the Subject and the Message, and you can change them after. To send files with an email, save it and attach them in the sidebar. For a text message, write the 'Text'. Up to 160 characters is one text.
6. Click 'Preview' to see the message as one person gets it.
7. Click 'Send Test Email' or 'Send Test Text' to get a copy yourself.
8. Click 'Send'. Churchit tells you how many people it goes to and asks you to confirm.
To send it later, set 'Send At' before you click Send. It is sent within a few minutes of that time. Until then, 'Unschedule' turns it back into a draft.

A placeholder puts each person's own name into the message. Write it in double braces, for example "Dear {{ first_name }},". You can use these placeholders: {{ first_name }}, {{ last_name }}, {{ display_name }} and {{ full_name }}. Email Templates write the same names as {{ doc.first_name }}, and that works too. You can use placeholders in the Subject, the Message and the Text. Churchit does not save a message that uses any other placeholder, and it tells you which ones you can use.

'Preview' shows the message for the first person it goes to: their address or number, the subject, and the message with the placeholders filled in. To see it for someone else, pick them in 'Person'. The preview also shows how many people get the message, and for a text, how many characters it has.

Who gets it is worked out when it is sent, so people who joined or left since you wrote it are counted correctly. Churchit leaves out people who have a 'Death' life event and people who do not allow the Purpose. It also leaves out people with no email address, an email address marked Email Bounced, or an email address that unsubscribed. A household that shares one email address or phone number gets one copy. After it is sent, 'Sent To' shows how many people it went to, and the Church Email can no longer be changed. If sending stops, the Church Email goes back to Draft and a comment on it says why. If sending takes longer than the time allowed, the Church Email stays Sending and continues by itself about 15 minutes later. When you send it again, only the people who did not get it yet get it.

Each copy shows on the timeline of the person's Person form, with the subject and message as they got them. The Church Email lists the copies it sent in its own timeline.

A text message goes to each person's primary phone number. It goes only to people who said yes to texts. Nothing can be sent as a text until the SMS Settings are set up. To send texts, a user must be a System Manager or have a role in the Allowed Roles of the SMS Settings.
Churchit checks each text after it sends it. If your text provider (for example, Twilio) refuses a text, or the text is not in the SMS Log, Churchit marks that copy Error on the timeline of the person and of the message. A copy that left is marked Sent. Sent To counts only the texts that left. Churchit does not send a refused text again, also when you send the message again. To find the reason, look at the log of your text provider. Correct the number on the Person, then send a new message to that person. If the provider refuses every text, for example because of a wrong password, sending stops and the message becomes a draft again. When you send it again, only the people who did not get it receive it. A provider can accept a text and block it later, for example when a carrier filters it. Churchit cannot see this, so look for these texts in the log of your provider.

Every email has an 'Unsubscribe' link at the bottom. For Church News, a person who clicks it gets no more Church Emails from your church. For another purpose, the person stops getting emails for that purpose only. Members can also change their choices on the Communication Preferences page in the portal. When someone without a portal login asks to get Church Emails again, open their Person and click 'Resubscribe to Church Emails'. Nothing can be sent by email until an outgoing Email Account is set up. With multi-church on, a Church Email goes only to the people of its own church. Only that church's groups, smart lists and people can be picked.

Bounced Emails

When an email cannot reach an address, the mail server sends a report back to the church. This report is a bounce. Churchit reads bounces only when the church's email account receives mail. Open the Email Account that sends the church's email and tick Enable Incoming. The bounce goes back to the account that sent the email, so this account must send and receive. Churchit reads the bounces that Gmail, Microsoft 365 and Postfix mail servers send. It can miss a bounce from a different mail server.
When a bounce says that delivery failed, Churchit ticks Email Bounced on that address on every record that has it, such as a Person or a Family. Churchit does this only for an address that the church sent an email to in the last 7 days, because anyone can send an email that looks like a bounce. A bounce that says a rule blocked the email (a code that starts with 5.7) or that the mailbox is full (5.2.2) changes nothing. Church Emails do not send to that address. For a bounce from Gmail or Microsoft 365, the delivery status of the sent email also changes to Bounced. A bounce that says only that delivery is late changes nothing.
To find bounced addresses, open the People Data Quality report and set Problem to Email Bounced. Ask the person for a new address. When you change the address, Churchit clears Email Bounced. If you know that the address works again, untick Email Bounced on that row.

Who opened an email

Churchit can show who opened a Church Email. On the Email Account that sends the church's email, Track Email Status is ticked by default. Then each copy of an email has a small image that the reader cannot see. When the reader's mail app loads the image, the copy shows Read on the timeline of the person's Person form and on the timeline of the Church Email.
Many mail apps do not load images until the reader allows them. Then a person can read an email, and their copy does not show Read. So the number of Read copies is less than the number of people who read the email. Some mail apps, such as Apple Mail, load all images when the email arrives. Then the copy shows Read also when the person did not open it.
To stop this tracking, open the Email Account and untick Track Email Status. The emails that you send after this have no image. If you keep the tracking, tell people about it in the church's privacy notice.

Text replies and STOP

Churchit sends text messages, but it does not receive the replies. Your text provider gets them. So use a text provider that handles STOP replies itself. Twilio, for example, does this by default for numbers in the US and Canada. To change the keywords and the reply, put the number in a Twilio Messaging Service, turn on Advanced Opt-Out, and send your texts with that Messaging Service. In other countries, and with other providers, read the provider's opt-out rules. When a person replies STOP, the provider blocks every later text to that number. The person replies START to get texts again.
Churchit does not know about a STOP reply, so it continues to send texts to that number. The provider refuses them, and Churchit marks each of these copies Error. When a person asks you to stop their texts, record it in Churchit: add a Communication Consent for the person with the channel SMS and the status Withdrawn. Leave Purpose blank to stop texts for all purposes. A member can also turn off the Text switches on the Communication Preferences page.
This applies to every text that Churchit sends: Church Emails sent as text messages, group messages sent as texts, Page Parents at check-in, and volunteer requests and reminders. A message that also goes by email, such as a page to the parents, then goes to the person by email. A Notification that sends a text also stops at the provider after STOP, but Churchit keeps no copy of it to mark Error. To find these texts, look in the Error Log or in the provider's log.

✅Consent

Each person can say yes or no to being contacted by email, text message, push notification, phone or post. A Communication Consent records one answer: the person, the channel, a purpose (or blank for every purpose), Given or Withdrawn, when they said it and how. You cannot change a consent after you save it. If someone changes their mind, click Record a Change of Mind and save the new answer. The newest answer is the one that counts. A yes for every purpose does not undo a no for one purpose. To start that purpose again, record a yes for that purpose. When two answers have the same date and time, the one saved last counts. Recorded On cannot be in the future. A person's consents are listed on the Connections tab of their Person form.

A Communication Purpose is a reason the church contacts people, such as Church News, Pastoral Care or Child Safety. A person can say yes to one purpose and no to another. A Church Email has Church News unless you choose another Purpose, and a page to parents from the Check-In Station has Child Safety, so you cannot delete these two. Reminders of a function sign-up have Event Registrations, and ministry team requests and reminders have Serving. You cannot delete these either. A person gets a ministry team text only with text consent for Serving, but the ministry team emails go to everyone asked to serve. You can add your own purposes.

In the UK, the EU and other places, the law says people must agree before a church contacts them. In that case, tick Contact People Only With Their Consent on the Church. The church then contacts only people who said yes. When the box is not ticked, the church contacts people until they say no. The church never contacts a person who has died.

Asking people to renew their consent: A church that ticks Contact People Only With Their Consent can ask people to confirm, from time to time, that they still want its emails. Open Notifications, duplicate "Consent Renewal" and turn on the copy. Two years (730 days) after a person last said yes to email, the copy emails their primary email address with a Yes, keep in touch link. The person needs no login. The link records a new yes, with How set to Email Link, for each email purpose the person allows. Their choices do not change, and the next reminder comes two years after that. Each yes asks only once: if the person does nothing, Churchit does not ask again until they next change their choices. To ask after a different time, change Days After on your copy. People in a church that does not tick the box, people who said no last, and people who have died get no reminder.

Texts need a yes first: A person gets text messages only after they say yes to texts. This is true also when Contact People Only With Their Consent is not ticked. A person can say yes on the Communication Preferences page, on a Connection Card, or to you. To record a yes that a person tells you, add a Communication Consent with the channel SMS. Push notifications also need a yes first, with the channel Push.

When someone clicks Unsubscribe in a Church News email, or turns off Church News by email on the Communication Preferences page, everyone at your church who uses that email address is recorded as having said no to Church News. The link in an email with another Purpose records a no to that Purpose only. A Church Email leaves out everyone who said no to its Purpose. When you resubscribe someone from their Person form, only that person is recorded as having said yes.
Automatic emails and text messages (Notifications) leave out an address only when no person who uses it may be contacted. Staff always get the work notices meant for them.

Communication Preferences: Signed-in members choose how the church may contact them on the Communication Preferences page in the portal. The page shows their primary email address and phone number, and one row for each Communication Purpose. Each row has an Email switch. It also has a Text switch when text messages are set up in SMS Settings and the member has a phone number, and a Push switch when the member allowed notifications on a device (see Notifications on Phones). A switch saves at once. It records a consent for that member only, with How set to Member Portal. If a member stopped every email to their address, the page tells them. When they turn on an email switch, emails to that address start again. A member with no email address on file sees a note that asks them to contact the church office.

Connection Cards: The Stay in Touch section of the Connection Card has two boxes: Email Me Church News and Events, and Text Me Church News and Events. The text box shows after the guest types a phone number. When Churchit processes the card, each ticked box records a yes to Church News for the card's person, with How set to Web Form. A ticked box records nothing when the card has no email address or phone number for it, when the card is Spam, or when the card is linked to a person who does not already have that email address or phone number. A phone number matches when it has the same digits as one on the person. A ticked box does not replace a no that the person gave before. A paper card that you add in the desk works the same way.

📱Notifications on Phones

Churchit can show notifications on a member's phone or computer, through the installed member app or the browser. The site sends them itself with Web Push, so you do not need an outside push service. No feature sends push notifications yet. When a feature sends them, only members who allowed notifications on a device and turned on Push for that purpose get one. Everyone else gets the message by email or text, where the feature sends those.

Set it up: A System Manager opens Push Settings. Type the Contact Address as mailto: and the church office email, for example mailto:office@example.org, and save. Click Generate Keys once. Then tick Enabled and save. Do not remove the keys later, because each browser that allowed notifications uses them.

Allow notifications on a device: A member opens the Communication Preferences page and clicks Allow notifications. The browser then asks them to allow notifications. The member does this on each phone or computer. Turn off stops notifications on that device. On an iPhone or iPad, the member must first add the site to the Home Screen and open it from there. This needs iOS 16.4 or later. When a browser stops accepting notifications, Churchit forgets that browser. A member who signs in on a shared device and allows notifications takes that device over from the person before them.

Push needs a yes first: After a member allows notifications on a device, the Communication Preferences page shows a Push switch for each purpose. Each switch is off until the member turns it on, as for texts. Staff can also record a yes or a no on a Communication Consent with the channel Push.

💬Staff Chat

Churchit has no chat. For chat between staff, a System Manager can install Raven on the same site. Raven is a free Frappe app with the AGPL license. Only a person with the Raven User role can use Raven. A member who has only the Church User role does not have this role and has no desk, so they cannot use it. To send a message to the members of a group, a leader uses Message the group on the Lead a Group page in the member portal.

Install Raven. On your own server, run bench get-app --branch main https://github.com/frappe/raven, then bench --site your site install-app raven. On Frappe Cloud, add Raven to the bench and install it on the site. Churchit refuses to install with an app that has a doctype with the same name as a Churchit doctype. Raven has no such doctype, so the install continues. After the install, the Communications sidebar has a Staff Chat link that opens Raven. Only a person who can use Raven sees it.

More than one church. Raven does not follow the church rules of Churchit. Each Raven user sees the name, photo and phone number of all other Raven users on the site, and can send a direct message to each of them. Raven workspaces do not change this. A person in a Raven channel sees all of its messages, also the messages from staff of a different church. So give Raven access only to staff who may see, and be seen by, the staff of all churches. Right after you install Raven, open Raven Settings and turn off 'Automatically add system users to Raven'. If it stays on, Raven adds each desk user when their User record is saved. Then give the Raven User role, or the Raven User role profile, by hand to each person who may use Raven. To stop a person, remove the role. Make one Raven workspace for each church. Set its Type to Private and tick 'Can only join via invite?'. Then add the staff of that church to it by hand.

Private records. A Raven Document Notification copies the text of a record into a chat message. Everyone in the channel can read that message, also people who cannot open the record. Do not set a Document Notification on Person, Person Note, Care Request, Counseling Case, Prayer Request or Background Check.

When adding a new location, note the following:
- Locations can be set as a 'group'. This will allow other locations to be 'grouped' under this location.
- Locations can be linked to an address. When linked, the map moves to the address. An address that is not on the map yet is looked up first, unless 'Look Up Addresses for the Map' is clear in People Settings. Then place the pin by hand.
- Shapes/drawings can be added to the map view for reference purposes (i.e. circle an area in an open space)

✅Tasks

Items that need to be completed. (Like a checklist or todo-list)

When you add a new task, note the following:
1. To add sub-tasks, tick Is Group, then make new tasks and choose the group task in Parent Task.
2. To give a task to a person, use Assign in the sidebar of the task. Only a user who can change the task can assign it, and you can assign only users who can see the church of the task.
3. When you assign an Open task, its status changes to Assigned. The assignment takes the due date of the task. When you change the due date, the open assignments move with it.
4. When you set the status to Completed, all assignments of the task close.
5. The day before a task is due, each person assigned to it gets an email and a desk notification, unless the task is Completed. To change the message or the number of days, a System Manager opens the Church Task Due notification. Other users cannot open it.

Work orders. A work order is a task for a repair or a job on an asset or a room. Set Type to Work Order. Then the Work Order section shows: Requested By (a person), Asset and Room. Set Priority to Low, Medium, High or Urgent. Only a Church Manager adds work orders. Church Staff can change them. The asset, the room and the person must belong to the church of the task, or be shared with all churches. When you move a task to another church, these links are checked again. The Open Work Orders report lists the work orders that are not Completed, with the earliest due date first. The Work Orders section on an asset or a room shows how many work orders name it. You cannot delete an asset or a room that a task names.

📝Meeting Minutes

Meeting Minutes record board or committee meetings.
- Optionally link the related Function.
- List who was present in the 'Attendees' table and write the record in the 'Minutes' field.
- Set 'Approved On' once the minutes are approved.
- If the meeting was recorded, attach the audio file in the 'Recording' section. It can be played back from the form.

🚪Rooms

A Room is a space that people can book, such as the sanctuary, a classroom or the fellowship hall. Tick Bookable to let people book it. Tick Requires Approval when an approver must approve each booking. Add users to Approvers to choose who approves the bookings of the room. Each approver needs the Church Staff or Church Manager role and access to the church of the room. When Approvers is empty, the Church Managers of the church approve the bookings.

Each church gives its rooms their own names, so two churches can each have a room called Sanctuary. One church cannot have two rooms with the same name. A shared room is on the list of every church, so its name must be different from every other room. A new room gets an ID such as ROOM-0001, and forms and lists show its name.

Setups. Add the ways a room can be set up, such as Banquet or Theatre, in the Setups table of the room. Give each setup its own name, and add the capacity, a description and a photo if you want. On a booking, choose one setup of the room in Setup, and add Setup Notes for the people who set up the room. The list shows only the setups of the chosen room. A booking without a room cannot have a setup. If you remove a setup from a room, the bookings that already use it keep it.

Questions. To ask each booking of a room some questions, such as the number of guests or the need for sound, choose a Question Set in Question Set on the room. A room can use only a Question Set of your church or a shared one. When you choose the room on a booking, the questions open in a dialog. Click Done to keep the answers on the booking. To change the answers later, click Answer Questions. A booking cannot be saved until each required question has an answer. If you move a booking to a room with other questions, the old answers are removed and you answer the new questions. If you change the Question Set of a room, its bookings keep their old answers until someone answers the new questions with Answer Questions. A blocked time asks no questions.

📅Room Bookings

To book a room, add a Room Booking. Note the following:
- The Room list shows only rooms with Bookable ticked. A booking of any other room is refused.
- On a room that does not require approval, a new booking is Approved at once. On a room with Requires Approval ticked, a new booking stays Requested until an approver approves it. A booking that an approver of the room makes is Approved at once.
- Each approver gets a ToDo for a Requested booking. An approver opens the booking and clicks Decide, then Approve or Deny. The ToDos close, and the user who made the booking gets an email with the decision.
- If a user who is not an approver changes the room, the start or the end of an approved booking, the booking goes back to Requested and waits for approval again. A change to the notes only keeps it Approved.
- Two bookings of one room cannot overlap. When a booking clashes, the message names the clashing bookings of your churches. It counts the bookings of other churches, but does not name them.

Conflicts. Tick Request Anyway to send a booking that clashes to the approvers. The booking gets the status Conflict, and each approver gets a ToDo. A Conflict booking does not keep other people from booking the room. An approver opens it and clicks Resolve, then one of these:
- Keep This Booking approves it and denies the Requested and Conflict bookings that it clashes with. If an Approved booking still has the room at that time, it is refused.
- Share the Room ticks Can Share Room on it and on each booking that it clashes with, and approves it.
Two bookings that both have Can Share Room ticked do not clash. A recurring Function copies its bookings with Request Anyway ticked, so a copy that clashes becomes a Conflict.

Blocked times. An approver of a room can keep the room free, for example for repairs or a holiday. Add a Room Booking with the status Blocked. It needs no Requester. A request for a blocked time is refused, also with Request Anyway ticked. When you save a blocked time, each Requested or Conflict booking at that time is denied. An Approved booking at that time refuses the blocked time: cancel or move it first. Only an approver of the room can add, change or remove a blocked time.

Equipment. To let people book a Church Asset, such as a projector or the van, open it and tick Bookable. On a Room Booking, choose the equipment in Equipment. You can leave Room empty to book equipment only. A booking of equipment only is Approved at once and belongs to your church. Equipment can be in only one booking at a time, and a Conflict booking keeps its equipment too. If another booking has the equipment at that time, the booking is refused and the message names the other booking. If that booking belongs to a church you cannot see, the message only counts it.

Lend equipment. Use a Room Booking to lend equipment. Add the equipment to Equipment and leave Room empty. A booking of equipment only is Approved at once.
1. When the borrower takes the equipment, open the booking and click Check Out. Checked Out On shows the time.
2. When the borrower brings the equipment back, open the booking and click Check In. Returned On shows the time, and the status changes to Completed.
Only a Church Manager can check equipment out or in. Nobody can type these times by hand. You cannot check out equipment that is still out on another booking: check in that booking first. A Completed booking does not hold its room or its equipment, so other people can book them again. The Assets on Loan report shows all equipment that is out and not returned. The booking with the oldest End is at the top, so late returns show first.

Rooms for a Function. To book rooms for a Function, open the Function and click Create, then Book a Room. Choose one or more rooms and any equipment, check the Requester and the times, and click Book. Each room gets its own booking, and the equipment goes with the first booking that saves. Book a Room does not show a room that has a Question Set. Book that room on its own Room Booking form.

Room calendar. Click Room Calendar in the Operations sidebar to see the room bookings by month, week or day. The calendar does not show Denied and Cancelled bookings. Remove the Status filter to see them. The Room Booking list has the same filter. Click a booking to open it. You see the bookings of your churches and the bookings of shared rooms.

Room requests from members. Members ask for a room on the Book a Room page of the member portal (/room-request). A member chooses a room, the start, the end and the purpose, and can add notes. The page offers only the bookable rooms of the member's church and the shared rooms. The member is always the Requester of the booking. On a room with Requires Approval ticked, the request stays Requested until an approver approves it. The member sees only their own requests and their status. The Room Request form does not show a room that has a required question. Staff book that room on the Room Booking form. Members cannot book equipment. Staff add it to the booking. A visitor who is not signed in must log in first. If your portal menu does not have Book a Room, open Portal Settings and add a row to Menu with the Title Book a Room, the Route room-request, the Reference Document Type Room Booking and the Role Church User.

Setup sheets. To give the people who set up a room what they need, open the Room Booking, click Print and choose Setup Sheet. The sheet shows the room, the start and end times, the purpose and the requester, the setup with its capacity and description, the setup notes, the equipment and the answers to the room's questions. Print it or click Get PDF.

Room Usage report. To see how your rooms and equipment are used, open the Room Usage report (under Rooms in the Operations sidebar). It shows one row for each booking whose times are in the dates you choose: the room, the equipment, the start and end, the hours, the status, the requester and the purpose. You can show only one room or one status. The report shows the bookings of your churches and the bookings of shared rooms. Church Managers and Church Staff can open it.

Rooms with more than one church. A room belongs to one church, and only that church can book it. Tick Shared with all churches on a room to let every church book it. A shared room has one diary for every church, so every church sees its bookings and a booking blocks that time for all of them. The Requester of a booking must be a person of the church that books the room.

🌐 Website Manual

The website module consists of two main features: a public website, and a user portal.

🌎Website

The public facing website is accessible by anyone and can be dynamically updated based on information in the Church App (i.e. Beliefs, Missionaries, Ministries, etc).
The top of the Web Site workspace shows the home page as a visitor sees it. Click the preview to open the website in a new tab.
User accounts with "Church Manager" permissions can modify the website by editing the website settings and publishing documents.
The website menu is the Top Bar Items table in Website Settings, and pages themselves are Web Page records. Publishing a Web Page adds it to the end of the menu. Un-publishing or deleting one removes its links from the menu and footer. A message confirms each change. Rename or move the new item in the Top Bar Items table. Editing a page that is already published does not put it back on the menu. Removing a link from the menu does not take its page down: after you save, the app offers to un-publish any Web Page that link pointed at. Answer No to keep the page reachable by its address.
The shipped pages (Home, Beliefs, Sermons, Missions, Ministries) are yours to edit; an upgrade leaves your wording alone and only ever repairs the record-reading code inside a page. Open one under Web Page: the content is in Main Section (HTML), a Jinja template (Content Type HTML with Dynamic Template on) that reads your published records, so the page updates itself as you publish sermons, beliefs, missionaries and so on. Prefer to write a page by hand? Switch Content Type to Rich Text or Markdown. Per-page styles go in the Custom CSS tab; the site-wide look is the Website Theme. To restore a shipped page's original look, copy its template from the app source (churchit/patches/after_install/templates/) back into Main Section (HTML).
No user account is required to view the website - anyone with an internet connection and a web browser can view the public website.
The site's look is a Website Theme, chosen under Website Settings → Website Theme: Churchit (the shipped look) or Frappe's plain Standard. Each has a light and a dark mode; visitors switch with the sun/moon button in the navbar, and the site follows their device setting until they do.
Every page ends with the same short footer: the church's name and founding year from the Church record on the left; the phone number from Contact Us Settings and the church address on the right (the address opens in OpenStreetMap); the copyright from Website Settings → Footer, if set, in the middle; and the footer links from the same tab centred underneath. The Contact Us page shows that same church address unless Contact address differs from the church address is checked in Contact Us Settings, which reveals fields for a different one.
Any field shown outside the desk is marked beside its label: an outgoing-link badge when anyone can see it, a people badge when only signed-in members can. Hover the badge to see which pages or portal forms show that field, and click it to open one. The marks are read from the pages themselves, so a page, portal form or public link added later is marked without anyone listing it.

🧭 Add an Item to the Website Menu

Some pages are not in the website menu by default, for example the Announcements page (/announcements) and the Watch Live page (/live). A Church Manager, or any user with the Website Manager role, can add them:
1. Open Website Settings and click the Navbar tab.
2. In the Top Bar Items table, click Add Row.
3. In Label, type the name to show in the menu, for example Announcements or Watch Live.
4. In URL, type the address of the page, for example /announcements or /live.
5. Click the edit icon at the end of the new row and tick Align Right. The menu items that Churchit installs have this box ticked, so the new item then shows next to them. When you publish a page, Churchit adds its item without this box ticked, and that item shows on the left. Tick Align Right on that item too to show it with the others.
6. To move the item, drag its row up or down.
7. Save.
When a page is off in Church Features, the menu does not show its item. With more than one church, the item opens the page of the church that the visitor selected.

📅 Calendar Feed

The Calendar page has two buttons above the calendar. Subscribe adds the calendar to the calendar app of a phone or computer. Download .ics saves the calendar as a file. Both give the calendar of the church that the page shows, without its branch churches. The link is https://<your site>/api/method/churchit.ics.get_public_calendar.
- The feed shows the same functions as the Calendar page: published functions from one month ago to six months ahead, with their address, description and online meeting link. Functions with Link Only ticked are not in the feed.
- With more than one church, add ?church=<church ID> to show the calendar of one published church.
- Add ministry=<ministry> to show only the functions of one published ministry, for example ?church=CHR-01&ministry=Youth. A ministry that is not published gives an empty calendar.
Calendar apps check the link for changes by themselves.

📍 Locations (Multi-Church)

Switch on Enable Multi-Church in Church Features and the website can show all of your churches, not just the main one. Open each Church and tick Publish to put it on the website. That is the only step. A church you leave unpublished does not appear anywhere on the site.

Once two or more churches are published, a Locations item appears in the website menu. It lists every published church, and All locations at the bottom opens the Locations page, which shows a card for each church with its photo, mission statement, description and address. The photo is the Image of the Church. Visitors see it only when it is a public file. The Study manual tells how to make it public.

When a visitor picks a location, the whole website switches to that church and stays there as they move from page to page. These follow the choice:
- the church name in the website menu, and the address and phone number in the footer
- the home page, calendar, sermons, ministries, missions and bulletins
- the funds offered on the Give page
- the About Us page, which shows that church's own About and Mission Statement when it has them, and the main church's wording when it does not
- anything a visitor sends in from those pages, such as a prayer request, which belongs to the church whose page they used

A few things worth knowing:
- A record ticked Shared with all churches, such as a sermon, a song or a room, appears on every church's pages. The Foundations manual explains sharing.
- A member who is signed in sees their own church first, whether or not it is published.
- If only one church is published, or multi-church is switched off, there is no Locations item and the website looks exactly as it does for a single church.

📺 Watch Live

To show your live stream on the website, open the Church and put the link to the stream in Live Stream Link. The link must start with https://. The /live page then shows the stream:
1. A YouTube video link, a youtube.com/channel/ link that ends in /live, a Let's Church media link, a PeerTube video link or an Owncast player link (it ends in /embed/video) plays on the page.
2. Any other link, such as a youtube.com/@name/live link, opens the site of the stream.
3. A branch church without its own link uses the link of its parent church.
4. If no church above the selected one has a link, /live shows "Page not found".
The Calendar page shows a Watch live button on each function with Streamed ticked while it runs. The Ministries manual tells more. The website menu has no Watch Live item by itself. To add one, see Add an Item to the Website Menu above, and use the URL /live.

A YouTube channel. A youtube.com/@name/live link does not play on the page. It opens YouTube. To play the stream of your channel on the page, use the link with your channel ID: https://www.youtube.com/channel/channel ID/live. A channel ID starts with UC, for example UCabcdefghijklmnopqrstuv. To find it, sign in to YouTube with the channel account and open https://www.youtube.com/account_advanced. Copy the Channel ID. You can also open your channel page, click more about this channel, click Share channel and click Copy channel ID. This link always plays the stream that is live now, so you do not change it for each service.
A channel link does not play in every browser. After you set the link, open /live and make sure that the stream plays. If it does not play, use the watch link of the stream, for example https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=video ID. A watch link plays one stream only, so put the new link on the Church before each service.

🔒 Members-only Pages

To show a web page only to members, open the Web Page, tick Members Only and save. A visitor who is not signed in goes to the login page and comes back to the page after login. A signed-in user who is not an active member, for example a visitor or a contact, sees a Not Permitted message. Staff who can edit web pages can always open the page. A members-only page does not show in the website search.
A link to a members-only page in the menu, the footer or the portal shows only to the people who can open the page. Other visitors do not see the link or the page title, and the page is not in the site map. This applies to links that use the page route as you see it on the page form. If you type the route in a different way, for example with an accent, the link shows to everyone, but the page stays closed.

🔌 Turn a Website Page Off

Open Church Features and open the Website Pages section. Each page that Churchit puts on the website has one row. Clear Enabled on a row and save to take that page off the website. Visitors then get "Page not found", the form on that page takes no new entries, and the links to the page go from the menu, the footer, the member portal and the site map. Nothing is deleted, and the records in the desk do not change. Tick Enabled again to bring the page and its links back.

When you clear a module in Church Features, its website pages go too. For example, with Study off, the Sermons, sermon, podcast, Bible and Bible Memory pages are off. Web Site is different: it hides only the desk tools, and the public website stays on. A page that you made yourself is not in this list. To take one of your own Web Pages off the website, clear its Published box. With the Sermons page off, the home page does not show the latest sermon, the link to all sermons or the sermon count. If you changed your home page, check it for links to pages that are off.

🧱 Pages made with Frappe Builder

Frappe Builder is a separate app to design web pages by drag and drop. A System Manager can install it next to Churchit and make new pages with it. Note the following:
1. A Builder page takes its address before Churchit pages, web forms and Web Pages. Before you use an address, open it in a private window. Use it only when it shows "Page not found".
2. Do not use an address that is listed under Website Pages in Church Features, also when that page is off. A page that is off shows "Page not found" in place of a Builder page at that address.
3. A Builder page cannot keep the records of one church apart from the others. Show church records, such as events, sermons or people, only on Churchit pages, and link to those pages from your Builder page.
4. Churchit does not put its website badge on the fields that a Builder page shows.
5. The shipped pages (Home, Beliefs, Missions, Ministries and Sermons) stay Web Pages. Churchit ships no Builder pages.

👤User Portal

The user portal requires login credentials and provides users a personalized interface for accessing church information relevant to their account (i.e. Personal Profile, Prayer Requests, Alms Requests, Function Sign-Ups, etc).
Portal users cannot access all information in the Church App, they can only access information related to their 'Person', or information shared with them (i.e. non-private prayer requests).

Each portal page shows a menu of the other portal pages. On a computer, the menu is a column on the left. On a phone, it is a row below the top bar that you scroll sideways.
The menu is the Menu table in Portal Settings. The Role on each row controls who sees it. To add, remove or move a page in the menu, change that table.
