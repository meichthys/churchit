# Privacy Policy

We treat your data like Manna - we don't keep it for tomorrow!
Please treat your own data like the 10 Commandments - keep a backup copy!

AI is off unless a Church Manager or a System Manager turns it on. When it is on, only a Church Manager, a System Manager or a person with the Church AI User role can connect an AI app. The app can see and change everything that its user can see and change. A hosted AI app sends what it reads to the company that runs it, and that company's terms apply to it. A local AI model keeps the data on the computer that runs it. Ask the AI company how it keeps and uses the data before you turn AI on.
