This page tells you how our church uses AI with the information we keep about you. Our office staff can edit it, and we publish it only when it describes what we do.

What we promise

  • AI is off in our church software unless we turn it on.
  • Only the staff members we name may connect an AI app, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to our church software, or chat with AI in it. The AI sees only what that staff member can see.
  • We ask our staff to use an AI model that runs on their own computer, or to give an AI app only what a task needs.
  • We do not ask AI to write a sermon or a prayer for our pastor.
  • No AI talks to you for us. When you write to the church, a person reads it and a person answers. If AI helps us write something, a person reads it and decides before anyone else sees it.

Where the information goes

Most AI apps, such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, send what they read to the company that runs them, and that company's own terms apply to it. An AI model that runs on our own computer keeps the information on that computer.

What we cannot promise

If a staff member copies information into an AI tool by hand, or a browser add-on reads the pages a staff member opens, our church software cannot stop it. We ask our staff not to.

Questions

Ask the church office. If you think AI used your information wrongly, tell us, and we will look into it.
