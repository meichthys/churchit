Open [Church Features](/desk/church-features) and open the **Website Pages** section. Each page that Churchit puts on the website has a row. Clear **Enabled** on a row and save to take that page off the website. Visitors then get "Page not found", its form takes no new entries, and its links go from the menu, the footer, the member portal and the site map. Nothing is deleted. Tick Enabled again to bring the page back.

- When you turn off a module in Church Features, its website pages go too. For example, with Study off, the sermon pages, the podcast, the Bible and Bible Memory are off.

- The **Web Site** module switch is different: it hides only the desk tools, and the public website stays on.

- Your own Web Pages are not in this list. To take one off, clear its **Published** box.

- With the Sermons, Calendar or Contact page off, the home page leaves out its links to that page. If you changed your home page yourself, check it for links to pages that are off.

## No public website

If your church already has its own website, tick **Turn Off the Public Website** in the Website Pages section of [Church Features](/desk/church-features) and save. A visitor who is not signed in then sees only the login page. Members sign in and keep the member portal and the pages that are on, such as Calendar, Give and Bulletins.

The pages about the church go for members too: Home, Beliefs, Sermons, Missions, Ministries, About Us, Contact Us, Locations, Watch Live, Find a Group, the Connection Card, the prayer request form for visitors, and your own Web Pages that are not Members Only.

Visitors cannot give online or sign up for a function until they sign in. The podcast feed stays on, and the link in a ministry team request email still opens without a login. Clear the box and save to bring the website back.

**Example:** First Church already has a website made by a designer. It wants Churchit only for the member portal, online giving and the calendar. It ticks Turn Off the Public Website and puts links to /give and /calendar on its own website. Members who follow those links sign in once.
