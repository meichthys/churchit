When AI is turned on (see the Foundations manual), a saved [Web Page](/desk/web-page) that is published and not Members Only gets AI helpers. The AI reads only text that the public can already read. Nothing is saved until you save the page.

- **Draft with AI** beside **Description** in the Meta Tags section writes the short text that search engines show.

- **Alt Text with AI** in **Main Section**, when the Content Type is Rich Text, writes alt text for each picture that has none. Alt text is what a screen reader says for a picture. The AI cannot see the pictures: it reads the page and the file names. When they do not tell what a picture shows, it writes "Photo from the church". Click **Use**, check each text and save.

- **Translate with AI** makes a copy of the page in another language that is enabled on your site. Click it, choose a language and click **Open Draft**. A new Web Page opens, not saved and not published, with the language in brackets after its title and the language code after its address, such as about-es. Copy any JavaScript of the page yourself.

A page longer than 12000 characters cannot be translated: split it into two pages first. Churchit refuses a translation that changed the page's template code or added scripts or links to other sites.

**A fluent person must check every translation.** AI makes mistakes in every language, and more in a rare one. It can change the meaning of a belief or a name. Before you publish the draft, ask a person who speaks the language well, and who knows what your church believes, to read it.
