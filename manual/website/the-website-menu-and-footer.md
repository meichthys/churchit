The website menu is the **Top Bar Items** table in [Website Settings](/desk/website-settings), on the **Navbar** tab. Some pages are not in the menu at first, for example Announcements (/announcements) and Watch Live (/live). To add one:

1. Open [Website Settings](/desk/website-settings) and click the **Navbar** tab.

2. In the **Top Bar Items** table, click **Add Row**.

3. In **Label**, type the name to show in the menu, for example Watch Live.

4. In **URL**, type the address of the page, for example /live.

5. Click the edit icon at the end of the row and tick **Align Right**, so that the item shows next to the other items. The items that Churchit installs have this box ticked. An item that Churchit adds when you publish a page does not, so tick it on that item too.

6. Drag the row up or down to move the item, then save.

With more than one church, a menu item opens the page of the church that the visitor chose. When you remove a link from the menu, the page stays online. After you save, Churchit offers to un-publish each Web Page that the link opened. Answer **No** to keep the page open by its address. When a page is off in Church Features, the menu hides its item.

## Logo

To show your logo in the menu bar, open the **Navbar** tab of [Website Settings](/desk/website-settings) and set **Brand Image**. The menu bar then shows the logo in place of the church name. This works only while **Brand HTML** is empty.

## Footer

Every page ends with a short footer. It shows the church name and founding year from the [Church](/desk/church) record, the phone number from [Contact Us Settings](/desk/contact-us-settings), the church address (it opens in OpenStreetMap), and the copyright from the **Footer** tab of Website Settings.

The footer links are **I'm New** (the Connection Card), **Prayer Request** (the prayer request form for visitors) and **Member Portal**. Change them in the **Footer Items** table on the Footer tab of Website Settings. When a page is off in Church Features, its link goes too. If you remove all the footer links on purpose, an update of Churchit does not put them back.

The login page also shows the footer, with help on how to get a login from the church office and a link to fill in a Connection Card. To hide it, clear **Show Footer on Login** in Website Settings.

## About Us and Contact Us

The **About Us** page shows the **About** text and the **Mission Statement** of the [Church](/desk/church) record, and the History and Team Members from [About Us Settings](/desk/about-us-settings). The introduction in About Us Settings shows only when the Church has no About text. Write your main introduction on the About tab of the Church.

The **Contact Us** page shows the church address, unless you tick **Contact address differs from the church address** in [Contact Us Settings](/desk/contact-us-settings) and type another one. A message from the contact form goes to **Forward To Email** in Contact Us Settings, or to the email of the Church when that is empty.

Each message is also kept on the Church: open the Church and look at its activity at the bottom of the form. When **Send Acknowledgement Email** is ticked, the visitor gets a short thank-you email. One visitor can send at most 10 messages an hour.
