When your church has more than one location, the website can show all of them. Switch on **Enable Multi-Church** in the Multi-Church section of [Church Features](/desk/church-features) (see the Foundations manual). Then open each [Church](/desk/church) and tick **Publish** to put it on the website. A church that you do not publish does not appear anywhere on the site.

When two or more churches are published, a **Locations** item appears at the end of the website menu. It lists every published church, with (selected) after the church that the page shows. **All locations** opens the [Locations page](/locations), with a card for each church: its photo, mission statement, description and address. The photo is the Image of the Church, and visitors see it only when it is a public file (see Sermon Podcast in the Study manual for how to make an image public).

When a visitor picks a location, the whole website switches to that church and stays there from page to page:

- the church name in the menu, and the address and phone number in the footer,
- the home page, calendar, sermons, ministries, missions and bulletins,
- the funds offered on the Give page,
- the About Us page, which shows the About and Mission Statement of that church, or the main church's when it has none,
- anything that a visitor sends from those pages, such as a prayer request or a contact message, which goes to that church.

**Example:** Grace Church has a main campus and a Downtown campus. Both are published. A visitor picks Downtown. The calendar shows the Downtown events, the footer shows the Downtown address, and a prayer request that the visitor sends goes to the Downtown staff.

**Good to know**

- A record with **Shared with All Churches** ticked, such as a sermon, appears on the pages of every church. The [Foundations manual](/desk/manual%3A-foundations) explains sharing.

- A signed-in member sees their own church first. The member portal always shows the member's own church.

- A message from a branch's Contact page goes to the email of that branch. A message from the main church, or from a branch with no email, goes to Forward To Email in Contact Us Settings, or to the main church's email.

- With only one published church, or with multi-church off, there is no Locations item and no Locations page.
