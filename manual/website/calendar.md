The **Calendar** page shows your published functions. A function with **Link Only** ticked is not on the calendar, the feed or the home page, but anyone with its link can still open it. On a phone, the Calendar opens as a list of the events of the month: tap **month** to see the grid. Click an event to open its details. A function with **Streamed** ticked shows a **Watch live** button while it runs.

Two buttons above the calendar share it:

- **Subscribe** adds the calendar to the calendar app of a phone or computer. Calendar apps check it for changes by themselves.
- **Download .ics** saves the calendar as a file.

The feed holds the published functions from one month ago to six months ahead, with their address, description and online meeting link. It shows the calendar of the church that the page shows, without its branch churches.

To share the calendar of one church or one ministry, copy the Subscribe link and add to its end:
- `church=` and the ID of a published church, to show only that church, for example `?church=CHR-01`.
- `ministry=` and the name of a published ministry, to show only its functions, for example `?church=CHR-01&ministry=Youth`. A ministry that is not published gives an empty calendar.

Put `?` before the first addition and `&` before the next one.
