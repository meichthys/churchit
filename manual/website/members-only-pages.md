title: Members-only Pages

A members-only page is a page of the website that only your church's members can read. Use it for news and papers for the church family, not for the public: the agenda of a members' meeting, the annual report or budget, church policies, a newsletter, or how to get into the building.

The member portal is different: each of its pages shows a person their own records. A members-only page shows the same text to every member, and you write it like any other [Web Page](/desk/web-page).

To make one, open the Web Page, tick **Members Only** and save.

- **Members who are signed in** see the page in the menu and can open it. Only active members count.

- **Visitors who are not signed in** do not see the link. If they open the address, they go to the login page and come back to the page after they sign in.

- **People who are signed in but are not members**, for example a visitor with a portal login, do not see the link. If they open the address, they see a Not Permitted message.

- Staff who can edit web pages can always open it.

People who cannot open the page never see its title in the menu, the footer, the member portal, the site map or the website search. To show a members-only page in the member portal too, add a row with its route to the **Menu** table in [Portal Settings](/desk/portal-settings). In a menu, type the route exactly as the Web Page form shows it, so that the link stays hidden from visitors.

**Example:** The church secretary makes a Web Page called Annual Budget 2027 and ticks Members Only and Published. Members see Annual Budget 2027 in the menu and on the This week page of the member portal. A visitor who gets the link from a member is asked to sign in.
