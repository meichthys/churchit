Members and staff can install the website as an app on a phone or a computer. The app has the name and icon of your church and opens in its own window. Under the icon, a phone shows the church name when it has 12 characters or fewer, or else the **Abbreviation** of the [Church](/desk/church).

Staff open the app on the desk, and members on the member portal. Touch and hold the icon on a phone (or right-click it on a computer) to go straight to the Calendar, Prayer Request, Give or Sermons page.

After a user signs in, a card at the bottom of the page offers the app:

- On Android, and in Chrome or Edge on a computer, **Install** opens the install window of the browser.
- On an iPhone or iPad, the card tells the user to tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- Some browsers, such as Firefox on a computer, cannot install apps, so they show no card.

**Not now** hides the card on that device for 30 days. A user can install the app at any time: staff click **Install App** in the user menu, and members click **Install** on the Preferences page of the member portal. To stop the card from showing by itself, clear **Offer to Install the App** in the App section of [Church Features](/desk/church-features).
