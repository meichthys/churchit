The member portal is an app at [/portal](/portal) that members and staff open with their login, on a phone or a computer. It shows a person only what belongs to them or what the church shares with them. Staff who have a Person record open it from the menu at the top of the desk sidebar: choose **Member Portal**. Who gets a login, and how, is in the People manual.

A visitor who is not signed in goes to the login page. It tells them to ask the church office for a login and offers the Connection Card. Old addresses of portal pages, such as /household, open the same page in the app, so links in emails and bookmarks keep working.

## The menu

On a computer, the menu is a column on the left. On a phone, five tabs sit at the bottom: **This week**, **Check-in** (or **Sign-Ups** when your church does not use self check-in), **Groups**, **Prayer** and **More**, which opens everything else.

The menu is the **Menu** table in [Portal Settings](/desk/portal-settings) (in the Setup folder of the Web Site sidebar). The **Role** on each row decides who sees it. To add, remove or rename a page in the menu, change that table. A row for a page outside the app, such as a members-only page, opens that page. A page that is turned off in Church Features leaves the menu.

## What members can do

- **This week**: the first page. It shows what the member signed up for, when they serve and when their groups meet. When check-in is open, the first thing on the page is a button to check in. A request to serve shows the buttons **Yes, I can** and **I can't this time**. Beside the week are the bulletin's notices, the newest prayer requests with an **I prayed** button, the latest bulletin and the members-only pages that the member can open.

- **My Household**: the people in the household, the household photo and the address. An adult of the household can change the photo, add a child, and edit their own details and the details of their children. To add another adult or remove someone, the member asks the church office.

- **Check-in**: when your church uses self check-in, the member opens it a little before the service, chooses who is coming and taps **Show our pass**. Name tags print when a volunteer scans the pass at the door (see the Ministries manual).

- **My Details**: the member changes their name, birthday, allergies, email addresses, phone numbers and marital status, and chooses what other members see about them in the directory. The church office changes the address.

- **Preferences**: how the member gets each kind of message, notifications on the device, the install button and light or dark mode.

- **Sign-Ups**: **Upcoming** lists the member's places at functions with their status (Confirmed, Pending, Waitlisted or Cancelled). **Open** lists the functions that take sign-ups now. **Attended** is the attendance history. A function with questions or choices opens the church's sign-up page, [/register](/register). While sign-ups are open, a member can change or cancel a sign-up, and a freed place goes to the next person on the waitlist.

- **Serving**: where the member is asked to serve. A member can ask someone else on the team to take their place, add the dates they cannot serve, and set how often they serve (see the Ministries manual).

- **Book a Room**: the rooms the member asked for, and the answer to each (see the Operations manual).

- **Groups**: the member's groups, when they meet and who leads them, and the public groups of the church to join. A group's page shows who else is in it. An email or phone number shows only when that person chose to show it in the member directory. **Group Files** lists the files shared in the member's groups. A leader uses **Groups I lead** to accept people who ask to join, take attendance and message the group.

- **Prayer**: the member's own prayer requests and the Prayer List (see the Prayers manual).

- **Care Requests** and **Benevolence Requests**: ask the care team for practical or financial help, and follow the status.

- **Directory**: the households of the church, with the details each person chose to share. **Save contact** adds a household to the phone.

- **Bible**, **Bible Memory** and **Bulletins**: read the Bible, memorize passages (see the Study manual) and read or download the bulletins.

- **Pledges** and **Giving Statements**: what the member promised to give and has given, and the yearly statements to print (see the Finances manual).

An open page updates by itself when something changes for the member, for example when a place opens on a waitlist, a child is checked in, or a leader answers a join request. The app uses the logo of the Church, and its accent color is the Primary Color of the website's Website Theme.
