The Communications module is how the church tells people things. Send a Church Email by email or text message to the whole church or to one group, print and share the weekly bulletin, keep the letters that the church receives, and respect each person's choice about how you may contact them.

Church Office users write Church Emails and bulletins. Only a Church Manager (or a System Manager) sends a Church Email. Other staff can read the emails that were sent.

The [Communications workspace](/desk/communications) has two shortcuts: **Scheduled Emails** (the emails that wait to be sent, the soonest first) and **Letters To Be Shared**. Below them, a chart shows the Church Emails sent each month. The sidebar lists **Church Emails**, **Bulletins**, **Email Templates**, **Letters To Be Shared**, **Communication Consents** and, when Raven is installed, **Staff Chat**. Missionary Letters and Person Letters are in the **Letters** folder. Bulletin Settings, Communication Purposes, Push Settings and SMS Settings are in the **Setup** folder.
