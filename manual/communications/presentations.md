Churchit shows the slides of a [Sermon](/desk/sermon) full screen in the browser: open the sermon and click **Present**. The Study manual explains how to build the slides, and what visitors see on a published sermon. Slide notes show on the screen, so do not write private notes there. To present songs, or for more complex services, we recommend [FreeShow](https://freeshow.app/).
