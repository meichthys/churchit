A Notification is an automatic email that Churchit sends when something happens, such as a new prayer request or a task that is due. The people it is for also see it in the bell at the top of the desk. When the site has no Email Account that can send, the email is not sent, but the bell still shows it.

These notifications are on when Churchit is installed. A System Manager can change who gets them and what they say in Notifications (in the Settings sidebar):

- **New Prayer Request for the Prayer Team**, **Approved Prayer Request for the Prayer Team** and **New Private Prayer Request** (see the [Prayers manual](../prayers/README.md)).
- **New Care Request**, **New Alms Request** and **Alms Request Approved** (see the People and Finances manuals).
- **Church Task Due** and **Room Booking Decided** (see the [Operations manual](../operations/README.md)).

The reminders for overdue follow-up cards, overdue care requests and planned visits are turned on and off in **People Settings**, under Reminders. A System Manager can change their text in Notifications, but not turn them on or off there.

## Shipped notifications

Churchit also ships notifications that are off until a System Manager turns them on:

- **Birthday Greeting**: emails an active person on their birthday.

- **Anniversary Greeting**: emails the primary contact of a married, active couple on their anniversary.

- **New Member Welcome**: emails a person when their Membership becomes Member and their Status is Active, with a link to the member portal.

- **Visitor Follow-Up**: emails the Church Managers when someone is added as a Visitor (or with no Membership).

- **Function Reminder 24h**: emails every user with the Church User role, which is every member with a portal login, the day before each published function that does not repeat. Turn it on only if you want all members to get these reminders.

- **Meeting Minutes Published**: emails the minutes to the Church Managers when they are published.

- **Position Term Ending**: emails the Church Managers 30 days before a position ends.

- **Background Check Expiring**: emails the Church Pastoral Care users 30 days before a background check expires.

- **Expense Pending Approval**: emails the Church Managers about a new expense.

- **Consent Renewal**: see [Consent](consent.md) above.

A shipped notification cannot be turned on itself. To use one:

1. Open **Notifications** (in the Settings sidebar) and open the notification by its name.
2. Click the **...** menu, then **Duplicate**.
3. Tick **Enabled** on the copy and save it.

Your copy keeps its text when Churchit is updated. To get a newer text, duplicate the shipped notification again. A receipt for each giver of a collection is a setting in Giving Settings (see the [Finances manual](../finances/README.md)).
