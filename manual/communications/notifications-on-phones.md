Churchit can show notifications on a member's phone or computer, through the installed app or the browser. No extra service is needed. No feature sends push notifications yet. When one does, only members who allowed notifications on a device, and turned on Push for that purpose, will get them. Everyone else gets the message by email or text, when the feature sends those.

**Set it up.** A System Manager opens [Push Settings](/desk/push-settings), types the **Contact Address** as mailto: and the church office email, for example mailto:office@example.org, and saves. Then click **Generate Keys** once, tick **Enabled** and save. Do not remove the keys later, because each device that allowed notifications uses them.

**Allow notifications on a device.** A member opens the **Preferences** page of the member portal and clicks **Allow notifications**, then allows them in the browser. They do this on each phone or computer. On an iPhone or iPad, the member must first add the site to the Home Screen and open it from there (iOS 16.4 or later).

A member who allows notifications on a shared device takes that device over from the person before them. After that, the page shows a Push switch for each purpose, off until the member turns it on.
