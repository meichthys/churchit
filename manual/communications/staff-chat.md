Churchit has no chat of its own. For chat between staff, a System Manager can install [Raven](https://github.com/frappe/raven), a free Frappe app, on the same site. The Communications sidebar then has a **Staff Chat** link. Only a person with the **Raven User** role can use Raven. Members cannot: to message a group, a leader uses **Message the group** in the member portal.

**More than one church.** Raven does not follow Churchit's church rules. Each Raven user sees the name, photo and phone number of all other Raven users, and a person in a Raven channel sees all of its messages, also from the staff of another church. So give Raven only to staff who may see the staff of all churches:

1. Right after you install Raven, open Raven Settings and turn off **Automatically add system users to Raven**.
2. Give the **Raven User** role by hand to each person who may use Raven. To stop a person, remove the role.
3. Make one Raven workspace for each church, with **Type** Private and **Can only join via invite?** ticked, and add the staff of that church to it.

**Private records.** A Raven Document Notification copies the text of a record into a chat message that everyone in the channel can read, also people who cannot open the record. Do not set a Document Notification on Person, Person Note, Care Request, Counseling Case, Prayer Request or Background Check. Keep Raven AI off: other AI bots can answer in a channel, and every member of the channel, from every church, reads the answer.
