Your website has a podcast feed of your sermons at /podcast.xml. Podcast apps such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify and AntennaPod read this feed. The Sermons page has a **Subscribe to the podcast** link to it.

The feed lists each published sermon that has public audio. When a sermon has no audio of its own, the feed uses the recording of its newest presentation in Presentation History, with the date of that presentation. The feed uses the name, About, Image and Email of the [Church](/desk/church). With more than one church, each church has its own feed, and it also lists the sermons shared with all churches.

## Add your podcast to Apple Podcasts or Spotify

1. Attach a square image of 1400 to 3000 pixels to the **Image** of the Church. Apple Podcasts refuses a smaller one.

2. Make the image public: in the upload window, clear **Private** before you click **Upload**. If the image is already attached, open the [File](/desk/file) list, open the image by its file name, clear **Is Private** and save. If the image is private, the feed shows the Churchit icon. If only System Managers can upload public files on your site, ask a System Manager.

3. On the Sermons page, copy the address of the **Subscribe to the podcast** link.

4. Add it in Apple Podcasts Connect or Spotify for Creators.
