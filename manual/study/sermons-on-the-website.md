Each published sermon has its own page on the website, at /sermon/ followed by its name. Anyone with the link can open it. The [Sermons](/sermons) page lists the published sermons, the newest first, 12 at a time. Visitors can search by part of a title or a passage, such as John. The home page shows the newest sermons too.

The sermon page shows the date, the Scripture passage, the preacher, the series, the video, the audio and the notes. The Sermons page and the home page show only the start of the first paragraph of the notes.

## Publish a sermon

Tick **Publish** on the sermon and save. Publishing makes these things public:

- the sermon page and the full notes,
- the **Audio Recording**, and every recording in Presentation History, so that the podcast can play them.

When you untick Publish, the recordings become private again. Do not publish a sermon whose recordings must stay private.

## Video Link

Paste an https link to the video of the sermon. A video on Let's Church, YouTube, PeerTube or Owncast plays on the sermon page. Any other link shows as a **Watch the video** link. A link that does not start with https:// cannot be saved.

## Notes for listeners

When a sermon has a Handout, the sermon page shows the handout as **Sermon Notes** in place of the notes. The Sermons page and the home page then show no notes for that sermon, because the notes hold the answers. Each word in brackets becomes an empty box. Below the outline is a **My Notes** box. What a listener types stays in their own browser, and Churchit does not receive it. **Print** prints only the notes, and **Copy** copies the filled outline and the notes.

## Sermon Series

A published [Sermon Series](/desk/sermon-series) shows on the Sermons page as one card that lists its sermons, with its description and its **Series Image**. A sermon in a series that is not published shows on its own card, and its page does not name the series. Attach the Series Image as a public file: untick **Private** in the upload window. A private image does not show on the website.

## When you rename a sermon

When you change the title of a published sermon, the address of its page changes too. Churchit adds a redirect from the old address to the new one, so old links keep working. You find these redirects in Website Settings under Route Redirects.

## With more than one church

A sermon belongs to its church. Tick **Shared with All Churches** to show it on the website and in the podcast of every church. The page of a sermon of a church that is not published shows "Page not found".
