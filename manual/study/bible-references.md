Wherever Churchit asks for a Bible reference, type it the way you would write it, such as John 3:16, rom 8:28-30 or Psalm 23. Separate several passages with a semicolon: Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:8-9. As you type, the desk suggests passages.

You type references for the Church Verse on the [Church](/desk/church) form, the Verse of the Week on a [Bulletin](/desk/bulletin), the Bible References of a [Belief](/desk/belief), the Scripture of a [Sermon](/desk/sermon) and of its slides, and [Bible Memory](/desk/bible-memory-item) passages.

When you save, Churchit writes the reference out in full, such as Romans 8:28-30, and keeps the passages in the order you typed them. A reference that Churchit cannot read is refused, so check the book name and the chapter and verse numbers. Only the 66 books of the Bible are supported, so books such as Tobit are refused.
