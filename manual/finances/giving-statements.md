A Giving Statement lists a giver's gifts for a period, usually a year, for their tax records.

## Make the statements

1. Open **Giving Statements** and click **Generate Statements**.
2. Check the **From Date** and **To Date**. They start as January 1 to December 31 of last year.

Churchit makes the statements from the submitted collections in those dates. Run it again at any time: it rebuilds the statements with the latest gifts.

- **Group Statements By**, on the Statements tab of Giving Settings, makes one statement for each household (at first) or one for each person. A household statement is addressed to its primary contact. If the primary contact changes, or you change this setting, run Generate Statements again.

- When no gift in those dates is linked to a person, Generate Statements makes no statement and says so.

- A statement shows only gifts to funds that have **Tax Deductible** ticked (see [Funds](funds.md)).

- A non-cash gift shows its description in place of an amount, and the total counts only money.

- Generate Statements never removes a statement that you made by hand, a statement that was emailed, or a statement of another church. When a rebuild changes a statement that was emailed, it shows Not Sent again.

- Members see their own statements, and their household's, on the **Giving Statements** page of the member portal, where they can open and print them. So a statement is visible to the giver as soon as you generate it.

- **Print** opens the Contribution Statement, with the household name and mailing address.

## Email the statements

1. In the Giving Statements list, click **Email Statements**.
2. Enter the same From Date and To Date, and click **Email**.

Churchit sends each statement as a PDF to the giver's primary email, in the background. It tells you how many will be emailed, how many have no email address and how many were already emailed. The list shows each statement as **Emailed**, **Not Sent** or **No Email**.

- Print and mail the statements that have no email address. If the site cannot send email yet, a System Manager must set up outgoing email: you can still print every statement from the list.

- A giver who unsubscribed from all email gets no statement email.

- A statement is emailed only once. If a gift changes and you generate the statements again, the next Email Statements sends the new statement. To send all of them again, tick **Send again to statements already emailed**.

- To change the text of the email, edit the Email Template Giving Statement.

:::note[Example]
In January, the treasurer generates the statements for last year and checks a few of them. She clicks Email Statements: 180 go by email and 25 have no email address. She prints those 25 and mails them.
:::

With more than one church, a statement is issued by the church that received the gifts. A giver who gave at two churches gets a statement from each.
