Collections, expenses and fund transfers go through three states:

- **Draft**: you saved it, but it is not final. You can still change it. A draft does not change any fund balance, report or statement.

- **Submitted**: you clicked **Submit**. It is final and counts in the funds, the reports and the giving statements. You cannot change it.

- **Cancelled**: you clicked **Cancel** on a submitted record. It stays for your history but no longer counts. To correct it, click **Amend**: Churchit makes a new draft copy that you fix and submit. You cannot amend the collection of an online gift (see Record a refund).

So enter the gifts, check them, and submit only when the totals are right.

**Good to know**

- When you submit or cancel a collection, an expense or a fund transfer, a short message tells how much each fund changed. When money goes out of a fund and its balance goes below zero, the message gives the new balance.

- When you save one of them with a date more than 30 days after today, or more than one year before today, an orange message asks you to check the date. Churchit still saves it, so you can enter old records.

- You cannot rename a collection, an expense, a fund transfer or a giving statement. Its ID stays the same as the ID on the printed paper.

- Reports for a date range include all of the last day, so a gift at 11:59 PM on the last day counts.
