A Budget says how much the church plans to spend on each expense type in a period, usually a year. Churchit then compares the plan with what you actually spent.

1. Add a Budget with the start and end dates.
2. Add a row in **Expense Lines** for each expense type, with the budgeted amount and notes if you want. **Add All Expense Types** under the table adds a row for each type. The **Total Budgeted** adds itself up.
3. Save.

An expense type can be in a budget only once. Churchit warns you when a budget has a group type and one of its sub-types (their spending would count twice), and when its dates overlap another budget. The Status part of the form shows the total spent, the percent used and what remains.

Click **View**, then **Budget vs Actual**, to compare the budget with the submitted expenses of each type. It also shows what you expected to spend by today, the draft expenses that wait, and whether each type is over or under budget.

Choose a comparison, such as the same period last year, to see the spending of an earlier time beside it. The **Budget Summary** chart on the Finances workspace shows the same comparison. With more than one church, each church can have its own budget for the same dates, and **Include Branch Churches** adds the spending of the branches.

:::note[Example]
The budget for this year gives Utilities $6,000. In June, Budget vs Actual shows $3,400 spent on Utilities: 57% used at the middle of the year, so the church is a little over plan.
:::
