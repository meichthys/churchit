An Expense records money that the church pays out, such as a utility bill, a missionary's support or a repair. Each expense has an **Expense Type**, and the expense type names the fund that pays it. When you submit the expense, the money comes out of that fund.

## Add an expense

1. Open **Expenses** and click **Add Expense**.
2. Fill in the **Date**, the **Amount** (more than 0), the **Vendor** and the **Type**. The vendor's Default Expense Type fills Type for you.
3. Save. The expense is **Pending** until someone submits it.
4. Click **Print** to get the **Expense Voucher**, a check request with lines for signatures.
5. Click **Submit** when the expense is approved.
6. After you pay it, fill in **Paid On** and **Payment Reference**, such as the check number.

When an expense would take its fund below zero, a saved expense shows an orange note with the balance that the fund would have, so the person who submits it sees it first. To undo an expense, cancel it.

**Good to know**

- Choose a **Ministry** on an expense to add it to that ministry's Total Expenses when you submit it.

- Churchit makes a draft expense each day for each missionary support payment that is due, when the missionary has Auto-create Expenses on (see the [Missions manual](../missions/README.md)). These drafts show the **Missionary**. Check and submit them.

- Churchit has no approval step for expenses apart from Submit. To email the Church Managers about each new expense, a System Manager turns on a copy of the **Expense Pending Approval** notification (see [Notifications](../communications/notifications.md) in the Communications manual).

## Expense types

Expense Types sort your expenses for reports, such as Utilities or Office Supplies. Each expense type names the fund that pays it. Expense types can be grouped: tick **Is Group** on Utilities, then choose Utilities as the **Parent Expense Type** of Electricity and Water. The Expenses report then shows the types under a group when you choose the group.

Expense types are shared by every church, but the fund that one names may not be. If a branch is told that the fund belongs to another church, share that fund, or give the branch an expense type of its own with a fund it can use.

## Suggest Type with AI

When AI is turned on (see the [Foundations manual](../foundations/README.md)), Church Finance and Church Manager users see **Suggest Type** on a new or draft expense. The AI reads the notes of the expense, the vendor's name when the vendor has **Is a Business** ticked, and the names of your expense types. It never gets the amount, the fund or bank details. Click **Use This Type** to fill Type, then save.

Tick Is a Business on each vendor that is a company, shop or organization: the name of a vendor who is a person is never sent. A vendor whose name or email matches a person in your directory counts as a person, even with Is a Business ticked. Suggest Type does not show on an expense made for a missionary, because the Missionary sets its type.
