Alms are money that the church gives to someone in need, such as help with rent or groceries. An [Alms Request](/desk/alms-request) records the need, the decision and the payment. (For money given to the church, see Collections.)

**Example:** A member asks for help with an electric bill of $180. The care team adds an alms request for the family, and approves it after a visit. The treasurer gets an email, writes the check and clicks Create Expense. The money comes out of the benevolence fund, and the request shows Distributed.

## Handle a request

1. Add an [Alms Request](/desk/alms-request) with the **Recipient** (a person or a family), the **Amount** and the need. With more than one church, the **Requester** must be a person of your church.

2. The care team sets the **Status** to **Approved** or **Declined**.

3. When the money is paid, click **Actions**, then **Create Expense**. Pick an Expense Type if the request has none. Churchit makes and submits the Expense, takes the money out of the fund, and sets the request to **Distributed**. If you set the status to Distributed yourself, Churchit asks whether to record the expense now.

**Good to know**

- Each request gets one expense. If you cancel that expense, the request goes back to Approved. To pay it again, amend the cancelled expense and submit it.

- You cannot delete a request while its expense is submitted. Cancel the expense first.

- When someone adds an alms request, the Church Pastoral Care users and the Church Managers of its church get an email and a desk notification (**New Alms Request**). When a request is approved, the Church Finance users get one (**Alms Request Approved**) so that they pay it.

## Requests from members

Members ask for help on the **Benevolence Requests** page of the member portal. They click **Ask for help**, choose who the help is for, and type the amount and the need. The page lists their requests with the status. A member can change or delete a request only while it is Pending, and can add a comment that the care team sees.

## Case notes

On a saved alms request, click **View**, then **Case Notes**, to read the notes about the person who gets the alms. Click **Actions**, then **Add Case Note**, to add a note in the Benevolence category. For a Family, the notes go on its primary contact, or on the Requester when the family has none. Only the Pastoral Care team and the Church Managers can read Benevolence notes. The finance team does not see them.
