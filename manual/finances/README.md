The Finances module keeps the records of the church's money. It records the gifts that come in, in the Sunday collection or online, and the money that goes out as expenses and help for people in need. It keeps a balance for each fund, gives each giver a yearly statement, and tracks pledges and budgets. Churchit is not full accounting software: when your church also uses one, export a journal to it (see Export to your accounting system).

Church Finance users (the Church Treasurer role profile) and Church Managers work in this module. Only they submit and cancel collections and expenses. Only Church Managers and System Managers change Giving Settings. Other staff do not see gifts. Members give online on the Give page, and see their own pledges and giving statements in the member portal.

The [Finances workspace](/desk/finances) starts with the work that waits for you, the oldest first: **Gifts with No Person** (paid online gifts that no person is linked to), **Donations Needing Review** (online gifts whose charge does not agree with the gift), **Pending Expenses** (expenses that are saved but not submitted) and **Pending Alms Requests** (requests that wait for a decision, or approved requests that wait for their expense).

Under them, charts show weekly giving, giving by fund, expenses and the budget summary. The sidebar lists **Collections**, **Online Donations**, **Expenses**, **Funds**, **Pledges** and **Alms Requests**. The reports are in the **Giving**, **Fund Activity**, **Expenses & Budgets** and **Accounting** folders. Giving Settings, Payment Types, Expense Types and Data Imports are in the **Setup** folder.
