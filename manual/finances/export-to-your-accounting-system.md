title: Export to your accounting system
emoji: 📒

Churchit keeps its own fund ledger. If your church also uses an accounting system, such as QuickBooks, you can send it a journal of the gifts, expenses and fund transfers.

1. Open **GL Account Mappings** and add one mapping for each record. **Mapping For** says what the record is, **Record** is the fund, payment type, expense type or church, and **Account** is the account exactly as your accounting system shows it.

2. Map each fund to the account its money goes to, each payment type (Cash, Check, Card) to the account the money comes into, each expense type to its expense account, and your church to the account that pays expenses.

3. Open the GL Journal report and set the dates. Each submitted collection, expense and fund transfer gives lines whose debits equal its credits. The Memo names the record, never a giver. If a record has no mapping, the report lists every record to map.

4. Export the journal as CSV from the report menu, and import it into your accounting system.

The journal shows only submitted records. If you cancel a record after you export its dates, the journal shows no reverse line, so use a negative donation for a refund after an export. With more than one church, each church keeps its own mappings, and a shared fund needs a mapping in each church that uses it.
