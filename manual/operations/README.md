The Operations module helps you run the church buildings and the church office. Use it to book rooms and equipment, keep a list of what the church owns, give jobs and repairs to people, and keep the minutes of your meetings.

Church Managers add and change everything in this module. Church Staff can read rooms, assets, locations and published minutes, change tasks, and book rooms for themselves. Members ask for a room in the member portal.

The [Operations workspace](/desk/operations) starts with the work that waits for you. **Open Tasks** shows how many tasks are not Completed. **Booking Requests** shows how many room bookings wait for a decision (Requested or Conflict) and opens them as a table. **Tasks Board** shows the open tasks as cards, in a column for each status.

Under them, the charts show the open tasks by priority and the approved and completed room bookings of each week. The sidebar lists **Rooms**, **Room Bookings**, **Assets**, **Tasks** and **Meeting Minutes**. The **Reports** folder holds Room Usage, Assets on Loan and Open Church Work Orders. **Locations** is in the **Setup** folder.
