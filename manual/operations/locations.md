A Location is a place where rooms and assets are, such as a building, a floor or a storage shed. Locations can sit inside each other: the Main Building holds the Ground Floor, which holds the classrooms. Locations are in the **Setup** folder of the sidebar.

The Locations list opens as a tree. It starts at All Locations. Click the arrow next to a location to see the places in it, and click a name to open it. To see a plain list, click **Tree View** at the top and choose **List View**.

When you add a location:

- Tick **Is Group** to let other locations sit under it, such as a building that holds rooms. Choose the group in **Parent Location** on each location under it.

- Link an **Address** to show the place on the map. A location with no address takes the address of its parent. Churchit looks up a new address on the map unless **Look Up Addresses for the Map** is off in **People Settings**. Then place the pin by hand.

- The **Map** section opens by itself when the location has a map position. Click **Map** to open it yourself. You can draw shapes on the map, for example to mark a parking area.

- The **Rooms and Assets** section at the top shows the rooms and assets in this location.

With more than one church, rooms, assets, locations and meeting minutes can each be shared with all churches by ticking **Shared with All Churches**.
