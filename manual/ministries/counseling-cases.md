A Counseling Case is a confidential file about pastoral counseling with one person: the person, the counselor, the case type, a summary, the status and a row for each meeting in the **Sessions** table. Only Church Pastoral Care and Church Manager users can open counseling cases.

:::note[Example]
A couple asks the pastor for marriage counseling. The pastor opens a Counseling Case for the husband with the type Marriage and the status Intake. After the first meeting, the status changes to Active and the pastor adds a session with a short note. After six sessions, the pastor sets the status to Closed.
:::

- The status is **Intake**, **Active**, **Closed** or **Referred** (passed on to another counselor or service).
- You can add more Case Types in the Setup folder.
- With branch churches, only the person's own church can open a counseling case for them.

Counseling cases are kept more private than other records:

- A case never shows on the timeline of the Person form. It shows only on their Connections tab, to the people who may open it.

- Churchit keeps no history of changes to a case. When you change or delete a session note, the old text is gone.

- A case is never sent to an AI model.

- When a person's data is removed, the case stays without its history and comments (see the [People manual](../people/README.md)).
