The Ministries module holds the life of the church week by week. A **Function** is any service, meeting or event on the church calendar, from Sunday worship to a youth retreat. With functions you plan the order of service, take sign-ups, schedule the people who serve, check children in safely and record who came. The module also keeps your ministries, your song library and your counseling cases.

Church Managers add and change functions, ministries, songs and settings. Church Staff can read them, and can add sign-ups, serving assignments and blocked dates, but change only the ones they made. Church People Editor users approve sign-ups, Church Finance users record payments, Church Pastoral Care users keep counseling cases, and Church Check-In volunteers run the Check-In Station. Members sign up, see when they serve and check in from the member portal.

To turn off the whole module, untick Ministries in [Church Features](/desk/church-features). This also removes the Calendar, Register, Ministries, sign-up, attendance and serving pages from the website and the member portal.

The [Ministries workspace](/desk/ministries) starts with the work that waits for you: **Pending Team Responses** (serving requests with no answer yet), **Waitlisted Sign-Ups** (sign-ups that wait for a free seat), **Coming Up This Week**, **Church Calendar** (it opens in a new tab) and the **Check-In Station**.

Under them, cards count the ministries and the songs, and a chart shows the weekly attendance of the last three months. The sidebar lists **Functions**, **Coming Up This Week**, **Ministries**, **Check-In Station**, **Songs** and **Counseling Cases**. The folders hold **Function Planning** (sign-ups and their reports), **Attendance**, **Ministry Teams**, **Reports** and **Setup**.
