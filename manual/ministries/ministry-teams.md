A ministry team is the people who serve in a ministry, such as greeters, ushers, nursery workers or the sound team. Churchit asks each person by email, or by text, whether they can serve at a function, keeps their answer, reminds them before the day, and helps you fill the gaps.

**Example:** The Hospitality ministry has a Greeter position with eight people. Sunday Worship needs two greeters. You click Fill Open Needs on the Function: Churchit asks the two people who served longest ago. Tom opens the link in the email and clicks Accept.

Sarah declines because she is away, so the team coordinator gets an email, and you click Fill Open Needs again to ask the next person. The ministry has Remind Days Before set to 3, so three days before Sunday, Tom and the new greeter get a reminder.

## Positions

A [Ministry Team Position](/desk/ministry-team-position) is one job on a ministry's team, such as Greeter in the Hospitality ministry.

- Add the people who can fill the position under **People**. Only these people can be asked for it.

- **Most Times a Month** on each row limits how often a person is asked. 0 means no limit. Members set this number themselves on the **Serving** page of the member portal, under **How often you serve**.

- Tick **Needs Background Check** when the position needs a current, cleared [Background Check](/desk/background-check), such as Nursery Worker. Under **Required Checks**, pick the kinds of checks it needs, for example Criminal Background and Child Safety Training. Leave it blank to accept any cleared check. Each one must be cleared and still current on the day of the function.

- Tick **Block People Without Checks** to refuse an assignment for someone without the checks. Without it, you only get a warning. Adding such a person to People also shows a warning.

On the [Ministry](/desk/ministry), set the **Team Coordinator**, who gets an email when someone declines, and **Remind Days Before**: how many days before a function the people serving get a reminder. 0 sends no reminder.

## Plan who must serve

On a [Function](/desk/function), list the positions that it needs, and how many people each needs, in **Team Needs** on the Service Plan tab. Recurring functions and Fill From Template copy these needs. **Notes** on a need is a note to the people in that position: each person sees only the note of their own position.

## Ask people to serve

**Fill Open Needs.** Click **Actions**, then **Fill Open Needs**, on the Function. The Ministry Team Schedule and Ministry Team Grid reports have the same button: it fills every function in the dates, church and ministry of the filters.

- For each position, it takes people from the position's People, and asks first the person who served longest ago, or never.

- It does not ask a person who is blocked on that date, who serves at the same time, who lacks a check that the position needs, who already has their Most Times a Month, or who has died. It asks a person for one position of a function only.

- It never asks a person again for a place that they were already asked about, also when they declined. After someone declines, click it again to fill the place.

- It does not fill a function that has started.

**Ask one person.** On the Connections tab of the Function, add a [Ministry Team Assignment](/desk/ministry-team-assignment/new) with the position and the person. Churchit warns you, but still saves, when the person has a blocked date then, serves at another function at the same time, lacks a needed background check, already has their Most Times a Month, or has no email address. The function, position and person cannot change after you save: delete the assignment and make a new one.

## Requests, answers and reminders

Each new assignment is **Pending**. Once a day, each person gets one email that lists their new requests. To send the waiting requests at once, click **Send Requests Now** on the [Ministry Team Assignment](/desk/ministry-team-assignment) list. **Requested On** stays empty until the request is sent, so the list shows which requests still wait.

- Each request has a link to **Accept** or **Decline**, with an optional reason. The link works without signing in. The person can also answer on the **Serving** page of the member portal, and on its home page. They can change their answer until the function starts.

- The **Status** of the assignment shows the answer: Pending, Accepted or Declined.

- When someone declines, the Team Coordinator gets an email, and the place is open again.

- Each person who has not declined gets one reminder, once the function is Remind Days Before away. A request sent that same day counts as the reminder for that day, so a person asked late gets the reminder the next day.

- If the site can send texts, each request and reminder also goes by text to the people who said yes to texts for Serving (see Consent in the Communications manual). The emails go to everyone asked to serve.

No email goes out until the site has an outgoing [Email Account](/desk/email-account). A person with no email address and no text consent is never asked, so check the warning when you save. Change the words in the [Email Templates](/desk/email-template) Ministry Team Request, Ministry Team Reminder, Ministry Team Decline and Ministry Team Replacement.

On the Serving page, **Show the schedule** opens the order of service of each function where the member serves. While they have not declined, it also shows the key and arrangement of each song, the team notes, the note for their position and the rehearsal media of the songs.

**Email the team.** To write to everyone serving at a function, click **Actions**, then **Email the Team**, on the Function. It goes to each person asked who has not declined, in BCC. The email is kept on the function's timeline, and a message names anyone without an email address.

## When a volunteer cannot come

A volunteer who cannot come can ask someone else to take their place. On the Serving page, or on the page that the request email opens, they click **Ask someone else** and choose a person. The list shows only the people of the position who are free then.

- The person they choose gets a request at once. The volunteer still serves until that person accepts. A volunteer can have only one such request waiting at a time.

- When the person accepts, the volunteer's assignment changes to Declined with the reason Replaced by and the name, and the Team Coordinator gets an email that names who takes the place.

- When the person declines, nothing changes for the volunteer.

- Either way, the volunteer gets an email with the answer.

**Example:** Sarah is on the nursery for Sunday but her son is sick. On the Serving page she clicks Ask someone else and chooses Mary. Mary accepts from her email. Sarah's assignment changes to Declined, Replaced by Mary, and the nursery coordinator gets an email that Mary takes the place.

## Blocked dates

A [Ministry Team Block](/desk/ministry-team-block) holds the dates when a person cannot serve, such as a vacation. Tick **Whole Household** to block everyone in their family. Members add and remove their own blocks on the Serving page. Blocks only warn: they do not stop an assignment, but Fill Open Needs skips the person.

A block can repeat. Set **Repeat** to Weekly, Every Other Week or Monthly on the Same Weekday, and if you want, a **Repeat Until** date. Monthly uses the weekday and week of the From Date, for example the first Sunday. A repeating block must end before it repeats: a weekly block can be at most 7 days long. A monthly block that starts on a fifth Sunday covers only the months that have a fifth Sunday.

**Example:** Tom works every other weekend. He adds a block on the Serving page from Saturday to Sunday, with Repeat Every Other Week. Fill Open Needs then never asks him on those weekends.

With branch churches, only the person's own church can add a block for them.

## Calendar link for volunteers

Members can see the functions where they serve in the calendar on their phone or computer. On the Serving page, they click **Make a calendar link**, then **Subscribe**, or copy the link into their calendar app. The calendar keeps itself up to date. Anyone with the link can see where the member serves, so **Stop this link and make a new one** replaces a link that was shared by mistake.

## Team Schedule and Team Grid

- The [Ministry Team Schedule](/desk/query-report/Ministry Team Schedule) report shows the positions of each function in a date range: how many people each needs, how many accepted or did not answer yet, how many places are open, and who was asked. Tick **Only Open Positions** to see the gaps. On a Function, **View**, then **Team Schedule**, opens it for that function.

- The [Ministry Team Grid](/desk/query-report/Ministry Team Grid) report shows several weeks at once: a row for each position and a column for each function. Each cell shows the open places in red and the people asked: green for Accepted, orange for Pending and gray for Declined. Click an open place to ask someone for it. It shows 8 weeks at first, and at most 60 functions.

**Last Served** and **Times Served (90 Days)** on the Connections tab of the [Person](/desk/person) form count the functions where the person accepted an assignment. Filter the Person list on them to find people who have not served lately.
