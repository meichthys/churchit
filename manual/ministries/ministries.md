A [Ministry](/desk/ministry) is an area of the church's work, such as Youth, Worship or Hospitality. Functions, serving positions and expenses can belong to a ministry, so you see each ministry's events, team and spending in one place.

- **Name**, **Group** and **Team Coordinator** are at the top of the Details tab. Two ministries of one church cannot have the same name.

- **Status** is Active or Inactive. A bulletin with the Ministries section prints the mission statement of each active ministry.

- The **Website** section holds **Publish**, **Mission Statement** and **Description**. The Ministries page of the website shows each published, active ministry with its description and mission statement.

- **Total Expenses** adds up the submitted expenses that name the ministry. Only Church Managers and the finance team see it. The Ministries report, which check-in volunteers can open, shows no money.

- **Recurring Functions** lists the functions that repeat for this ministry. Click **Create Recurring Function** to start one (see Recurring Functions).

- The **Connections** tab lists the ministry's functions, its team positions and its expenses.
