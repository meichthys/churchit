The [Songs](/desk/song) list is your church's song library. Each song keeps its words as slides, its arrangements with chord charts, its rehearsal media and its copyright details. Add songs to the Schedule of a function to plan a service, show the words on a screen, and count the uses for your CCLI report.

**Example:** The worship leader imports Amazing Grace from a SongSelect file. Churchit makes the song with its slides. She adds an arrangement in G with a ChordPro chart, and an MP3 for rehearsal. On Sunday's Function, she adds the song to the schedule in the key of A.

The team sees the key A, the arrangement and the MP3 on the Serving page of the member portal, and the worship leader prints the chord chart in A from the desk. During the service, the song's slides show on the screen.

## Add a song

1. Open [Songs](/desk/song) and click **Add Song**.

2. Type the title, the **CCLI** number if you know it, and the **Authors** (the writers of the words and the music).

3. In the Copyright section, type the **Copyright** line as the publisher gives it. Tick **Public Domain** only when the words and the music are free of copyright.

4. Fill in the **Default Key** (the key the team usually plays), the **Tempo (BPM)** and the **Time Signature**, such as 4/4.

5. Add the words on the **Slides** tab, one slide for each verse or chorus.

6. Save.

Add themes as tags in the sidebar of the form, such as Advent or Easter, then filter the Song list by tag. The **Connections** tab lists the functions whose schedule has the song.

**Copyright on the screen.** The last slide of a song shows its copyright and its CCLI number, with your church's **CCLI License Number** from the [Church](/desk/church) record. Visitors who are not signed in see the words of a song only when Public Domain is ticked.

**Present a song.** Open the song and click **Present** to show its slides on a screen. To show a song inside a sermon presentation, add it as a slide to the sermon with no fields chosen (see the Study manual).

## Arrangements and chord charts

An arrangement is one way that the team plays a song. Add arrangements on the **Music** tab, each with a name, a key, a tempo, notes and a **ChordPro** chart. Two arrangements of one song cannot have the same name. In a ChordPro chart, put each chord in square brackets before the syllable that you sing on it, for example [G]Amazing [D/F#]grace. Start each section with a line such as Verse 1 or Chorus, and leave a blank line between sections.

- **Chord Chart**: save the song, click **Chord Chart**, and choose an arrangement and a key. The chart shows each chord above its words. When you choose another key, Churchit moves every chord to that key. A major key moves only to major keys, and a minor key only to minor keys. The arrangement or the song must have a key. Church Staff can open the chord chart.

- **Make Slides from ChordPro**: click **Actions**, then **Make Slides from ChordPro**, and choose an arrangement. Churchit makes one slide for each section of the chart, with the words and no chords. This replaces all the slides of the song, so it asks you first.

## Rehearsal media

Add chord charts, lead sheets, audio and video in the **Media** table on the Music tab. Attach a file or type a link, and choose the arrangement it is for, or leave Arrangement empty for every arrangement. The people who serve at a function see the media of each song on its schedule on the Serving page of the member portal, until the function ends. They do not see it when they declined.

## Bring songs in

- **Import Song Files**: in the Song list, click **Import Song Files** and choose up to 50 ChordPro files (.cho, .chordpro, .chopro or .pro) or SongSelect files (.usr). Churchit makes one song for each file, with an arrangement named Default and one slide for each section. It skips a file when a song with the same CCLI number (or title) is already in your list. To get a .usr file, download the song from CCLI SongSelect. A .usr file has no chords.

- **Add Public-Domain Hymns**: in the Song list, click **Add Public-Domain Hymns**, choose from 33 hymns that are free of copyright, and click **Add**. Each one comes with Public Domain ticked, a chord chart and its slides. They come from WorshipCommons (worshipcommons.org), and you can change or delete them.

## Export songs

To export one song, open it and click **Actions**, then **Export**. To export more, select them in the Song list and click **Actions**, then **Export Songs**. Choose **OpenLyrics** (words without chords, for FreeShow, OpenLP and Quelea) or **ChordPro** (the chart with chords). You can export up to 200 songs at a time, as a zip file. A song with no arrangement that has words is not exported.

## Song Usage and CCLI

The [Song Usage](/desk/query-report/Song Usage) report counts how many times each song was on the Schedule of a function in a date range (the last six months at first), with its CCLI number and the date it was last used. It also counts in how many uses the words were displayed or printed, and in how many the function was streamed or recorded.

Tick **Displayed** and **Printed** on the schedule row, and **Streamed** and **Recorded** on the function. CCLI has no public way to receive these numbers, so copy them into your CCLI report.

## Suggest Songs with AI

When AI is turned on (see the Foundations manual), save a [Function](/desk/function), then click **Actions**, then **Suggest Songs**. The AI reads the title, Scripture and series of the sermon on the schedule, and the titles, tags, keys and last use of your songs. It never gets lyrics, charts or names of people. It suggests up to 8 of your own songs, each with a tick. Click **Add** to put the ticked songs on the schedule with their default key, then save the function.
