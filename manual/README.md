title: Churchit Manual
---
This is the user manual of Churchit, the free church management app. It is written for church staff and volunteers: what each part of the app is for, how to do the everyday tasks, and the rules that are easy to miss.

Start with Getting Started for the words and the moves that work everywhere in the app. Then open the manual of the part of church life that you work in, such as People, Finances or Ministries.

The same text is in the app: open Help, or click **Manual**, right under **Home**, in the sidebar of a module. Links such as [People](/desk/person) open the desk of the demo site.
