A Belief is one statement of what your church believes, such as "Salvation by grace through faith", with the Bible passages it rests on. Published beliefs make up the **Beliefs** page of your website.

1. Open **Beliefs** and click **Add Belief**.
2. Type the **Title** and the **Belief Statement**. Each title must be unique.
3. Type the **Bible References**, such as Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:8-9. They show under the statement on the website.
4. Tick **Publish** to show it on the website, and save.

The Beliefs list and page show the beliefs in the order you added them. A belief can also be a slide in a sermon presentation (see the [Study manual](../study/README.md)). With more than one church, a new belief is shared with all churches (see [Share a record with every church](more-than-one-church.md#share-a-record-with-every-church)).
