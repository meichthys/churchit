The Church record holds your church's details: its name and abbreviation, address, phone and email, about text, mission statement, verse, image, and settings such as the default Bible translation. Churchit uses them on the website, the bulletin, the Church Directory, giving statements and emails. A new site starts with one church called My Church: open it and fill in your own details first. To see what each field means, click **...**, then **Take the Tour**, on the Church form.

## The Church form

- **Details tab**: the **Church Name**, the **Abbreviation** (a short name, required and unique, used where the full name does not fit), the **Legal Name** (the registered name, for giving statements), the **Image** (a photo or logo), and the **Address**, **Phone** and **Email** in the Contact section. The **Live Stream Link** for the website's Watch Live page is there too (see the [Website manual](../website/README.md)).

- **About tab**: the **About** text for the website's About Us page, the **Mission Statement**, and the **Church Verse** (a Bible reference, such as John 3:16).

- **Settings tab**: the **Tax ID** for giving statements, the **CCLI License Number** for song slides, the **Default Bible Translation**, and **Contact People Only With Their Consent** (see [Consent](../communications/consent.md) in the Communications manual).

Where these details show:

- **Website**: the About Us page uses the About and the Mission Statement. The footer and the Contact Us page use the address, phone and email. A blank phone or email uses **Contact Us Settings**.

- **Bulletin**: the image on the front cover and the verse, with its words in your Default Bible Translation, on the back cover, when those sections are on.

- **Church Directory**: the name, the Legal Name when it differs, the address, the website and the verse on the cover, and the image when Church Image on Cover is ticked.

- **Giving statements**: the Legal Name, the Tax ID and the address.

A branch church that leaves **Legal Name**, **Tax ID**, **Default Bible Translation** or **Website** blank uses its parent church's. Leave Legal Name and Tax ID blank on a branch when the main church is the registered charity that issues the receipts. The name, address and contact line on anything a branch prints are always its own.

Church Managers and System Managers change the Church record. Other staff read it.
