The [Sample Data](/desk/sample-data) page fills the app with a made-up church, so you can try each part before you enter your own records. A Church Manager or a System Manager can open it, and only a System Manager can create or delete sample data.

- **Create Sample Data** adds made-up people, families, funds and other records to your church, and demo logins that use their email address as the password. Do not use sample data on a site that people can reach from the internet.

- **Delete All Data** removes the sample records and every record of those kinds that you added yourself. Remove sample data before the church starts to use the site.
