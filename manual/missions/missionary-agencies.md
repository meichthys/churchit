A Missionary Agency is the organization that sends and cares for a missionary, such as a mission board. Link each Missionary to its agency in the **Agency** field. The website shows the agency name on the missionary's card, and you can filter the Missionaries list by agency.

To add an agency, open **Missionary Agencies**, click **Add Missionary Agency** and type the **Agency Name**. Add its website, its contact details and notes. On a Missionary form, point at the agency to see its website and notes. The logo shows only on the agency's own form.

With more than one church, every church shares the list of agencies.
