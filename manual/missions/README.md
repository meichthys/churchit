The Missions module keeps a record of the missionaries that your church supports and the agencies that send them. Use it to record how much the church gives, to keep the letters that missionaries send, and to show your missionaries on the website, in the bulletin and in the Church Directory.

Church Managers add and change missionaries and agencies. Church Staff can read them.

The Missions workspace shows how many missionaries you support now, how many agencies you work with, a chart of missionaries by status and the Missionary Map. The map shows every missionary that has a pin, also the Sensitive ones, because only staff see it.

The **Current Missionaries** card counts every missionary who is not Inactive, so Pending missionaries count too. Click the card to open the Current Missionaries list. The sidebar lists **Missionaries**, **Missionary Agencies**, **Missionary Letters** and **Letters Not Yet Read**. **Missionary Statuses** and **Support Frequencies** are in the **Setup** folder.
