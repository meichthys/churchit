The Prayers module collects the prayer requests of your church and the prayers that people offer for them. Members share requests in the member portal, guests send them from the website, and staff add them in the desk. Each request says who may read it, from the pastors only to the whole church.

Church Pastoral Care and Church Manager users look after the requests: they read every request, approve requests for the prayer list and set the status. The prayer team reads and prays for the requests in the member portal. Other staff see only the requests that they made themselves.

The Prayers workspace starts with the requests that wait for you: **Urgent Prayer Requests**, **Awaiting Approval** and **Ending This Week** (the open requests whose End Date is in the next 7 days). Under them, a chart shows the active requests by type. The sidebar lists **Prayer Requests**, **Awaiting Approval** and **Prayers**.

The **Reports** folder holds the Active Prayer List, Urgent Prayer Requests, Ending This Week, Recently Created and Recently Answered. Request Statuses and Request Types are in the **Setup** folder. The Summary workspace also shows the **Active Prayer Requests** and **Prayers Answered This Month** cards.
