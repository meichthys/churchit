*"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you."* (James 1:5)

New to Churchit, or need a refresher? This page explains the words and the moves that work everywhere in the app. Each module, such as People or Finances, has its own manual: pick one from the sidebar, or click **Manual**, right under **Home**, in that module's sidebar. On a form, the help icon at the top opens the manual for that form.
