- Logging in opens the **Summary** page. Its icons open each module, the same as the dock. **Needs Attention** counts your open assignments and the open care requests, and links to urgent prayer requests, pending alms requests and open follow-up cards. To start somewhere else, click your initials at the bottom of the dock, choose **Settings**, and pick a **Default Workspace** on the Settings tab.

- Click a module in the dock to open it. In its sidebar, click a folder to open it. The lists that other records choose from, such as types and statuses, are in the **Setup** folder at the bottom.

- If a Frappe page, such as Email Account, opens with a dock that you do not know, click the church icon at the top of that dock to go back to Churchit.

- Click **...**, then **Take the Tour**, on a form to see its main fields one by one.
