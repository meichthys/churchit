**Why does a list show fewer records than I expect?**

A list remembers the filters and the sort that you last used on it. The number next to **Filters** at the top of the list shows how many filters are on. Click the x next to it to clear them. A shortcut with a count, or a link from another page, also puts its filter on the list.

**Can I add my own choices to a drop-down list?**

Yes. Most lists of choices, such as types and statuses, offer **Create a new ...** at the end. A Church Manager can also add choices in the Setup folder of each module. To add your own fields to the Person or Family form, see [Church fields](../people/person.md#church-fields) in the People manual.

**Can a report be emailed to me every week?**

Yes, if your login has the Church Manager role profile. Open the report, click **...**, then **Setup Auto Email**, then **Add Auto Email Report**. Choose who gets it, how often, and the filters to use. The email shows only the records that the chosen user can see in the app.

**How do I see a guided tour of a form?**

Open the form, click **...** at the top right and choose **Take the Tour**. The tour shows the main fields one by one.

**Where are Frappe's own tools?**

Frappe's own tools are in the Framework app, which the Apps screen shows to System Managers only. The **Settings** sidebar lists the settings that a church needs, such as Users, Email Accounts (in the Email folder) and Print Settings (in the Printing folder). To hide a module that your church does not use, untick it in **Church Features**.

**What is in the sample data, and how do I remove it?**

The [Sample Data](../foundations/sample-data.md) page fills the app with a made-up church, so you can try each part before you enter your own records: people and families (with a child, his grandmother and a person who may not collect him), groups, follow-up cards, giving with a pledge campaign, a function with paid sign-ups and a discount code, songs, a room with an approver, a projector on loan, sermons with a fill-in-the-blanks handout, a bulletin and a members-only page.

Each sample login, such as mary.johnson@example.com, has its email address as its password.

Only a System Manager can create or delete the sample data. Click **Delete All Data** on the same page to remove it. The next new record of each kind starts at number 1 again, such as PRSN-0001. Remove the sample data before your church starts to use the site.

:::danger[Delete All Data]
Delete All Data deletes every record of those kinds, also the records that you made yourself, with their history, comments and attachments.
:::

**Why is Churchit free? Is there a catch?**

There is no catch. Churchit is free, apart from the cost of hosting it. Its first developer was saddened to see businesses profit from churches' need to be organized. "Freely you have received; freely give" (Matthew 10:8): the church can support itself through the gifts and generosity of its own members.
