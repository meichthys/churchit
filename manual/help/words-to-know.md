- **Module**: a part of the app for one area of church life, such as People, Finances or Missions.

- **Dock**: the narrow bar on the far left, with an icon for each module. Point at an icon to see its name. The modules that most churches use each week come first.

- **Sidebar**: the list next to the dock. It shows the pages of the module that you are in. The pages you use every day are at the top, and the others are in folders.

- **Workspace**: the first page of a module. Most workspaces show the work that waits for you, then a few numbers and charts.

- **Apps screen**: a page with a tile for each app that you can use. Click the logo at the top of the dock to open it.

- **Record** (Frappe also says document): one saved item, such as one Person, one Fund or one Song.

- **Form**: the page where you fill in and change a record. A field with a red * must be filled in.

- **List**: a page that shows the records of one kind, such as all People. **Report view** shows them as a table where you can filter, group and choose the columns.

- **Draft, Submitted and Cancelled**: some records, such as collections and expenses, are final only after you **Submit** them. A Draft is saved but can still change. A Submitted record cannot change unless you cancel it first. A Cancelled record stays for your history.

- **Role**: what a login may see and do, such as Church Staff or Church Finance. A **Role Profile** is a set of roles for one job (see Logins in the People manual).

- **Member portal**: the app at /portal where members see their own household, sign-ups and giving.
