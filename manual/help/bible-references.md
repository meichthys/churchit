Fields that hold a Bible reference, such as the Church Verse, a bulletin's Verse of the Week, a belief's Bible References, a sermon slide or a Bible Memory passage, suggest books, chapters and verses as you type. Short names work, so jn finds John. Separate several passages with a semicolon, such as Romans 3:23; Ephesians 2:8-9.

A reference that is not in the Bible is outlined in red with the reason, such as "Mark 16 has 20 verses", and the record cannot be saved until you fix it. Point at a reference to read its words in your church's default translation. A sermon slide or a Bible Memory passage that names its own translation shows that translation.
