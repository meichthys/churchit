The [Assimilation Funnel](/desk/query-report/Assimilation Funnel) report shows how far new people go after their first visit. Set **First Visit From** and **First Visit To** (the last 3 months at first). It lists each living person whose first attended function is in those dates, with four steps:

- **First Visit**: every person in the list.
- **Came Back**: attended again within **Came Back Within (Weeks)** (8 at first).
- **Connected**: is in a group or has served on a ministry team.
- **Member**: became a Member since the first visit.

A person can reach a step without the one before it. The numbers at the top and the chart show how many people reached each step.

**Example:** In the spring, 40 people visited for the first time. The funnel shows 22 came back, 9 joined a group or served, and 3 became members. The welcome team invites the people who came back but did not connect to a newcomers' lunch.
