Click **Map of Addresses** on the [People workspace](/desk/people) to see the addresses of your people on a map. Click a pin to open its address. Church Manager, Church Staff and Church People Editor users see every address. Other users see only the addresses that they added.

- When you add or change an address, Churchit sends the address text, never a name, to the OpenStreetMap search service, and fills in its position within a few seconds. If the service cannot find the address, it is not on the map: correct it and save again.

- The map shows the addresses on the current page of the list. Click 100 or 500 at the bottom to see more.

- To keep every address on your site, clear **Look Up Addresses for the Map** in [People Settings](/desk/people-settings). Nothing is sent while it is clear, and new addresses stay off the map. The Location and Missionary forms then do not place their pins either.

- To use another OpenStreetMap server, open [Geolocation Settings](/desk/geolocation-settings), set Provider to Nomatim and set the Base URL.
