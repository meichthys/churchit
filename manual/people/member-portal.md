The member portal is the app where members see and correct their own records (see [Member Portal](../website/member-portal.md) in the Website manual for a tour of all its pages). This section explains the parts that staff manage: logins, households and the member directory.

## Portal accounts

A member needs a login to use the member portal. There are two ways:

- **Invite**: staff click **Actions**, then **Invite to Portal**, on the Person (see [Logins](logins.md)).

- **First sign-in**: when your church lets people sign up on the website, or a System Manager adds a Website User, Churchit connects the new login to a Person at its first sign-in. It looks in the church of the login for one living person with the same primary email (not a Shared From row) and no login.

If it finds no one, it adds a Visitor with the Source Portal, who shares nothing in the directory. If it finds more than one, it connects nobody and lists them under Possible Duplicates: merge them, or set the **Portal User** on the right person. Staff logins are never connected this way.

## My Household

Members see their household on the **My Household** page of the member portal: the Family photo and name, the mailing address and the living members with their Household Role, and the age of each Child.

- A member clicks **Edit** beside their own name to change their details. An Adult can also edit each Child under the Adult Age.

- An Adult can click **Change photo** to put a new photo on the family. The photo is public, so other members see it in the directory.

- An Adult can click **Add a child** (younger than the Adult Age), and type the first name, last name, birthday and gender. The child joins the household with the role Child and the relationship Son or Daughter, as a Visitor with the Source Portal. If the child may already be in Churchit, a Possible Duplicate opens for staff to check.

- Members cannot add another adult or remove anyone. The page tells them to contact the church office.

## My Details

On **My Details**, a member changes their name, gender, birthday, allergies, emails, phone numbers (with the primary ones), marital status, anniversary and photo. The birthday goes into the Birth life event, so Birth Date and Age follow. Members cannot change their addresses there: they tell the church office.

- **Hide me from the member directory** leaves them out of the member directory, the printed Church Directory and the bulletin's birthdays and anniversaries.

- They choose whether the directory shows their email, phone, address and birthday.

- **Leave me out of the church's AI features** keeps them away from Churchit's AI features. The part says whether the church uses AI now, so a member can choose before AI is turned on. A parent can tick it for a child. Only the person, a parent of a child, or a System Manager can clear it again.

## Send Details Link

To let a person correct their details without a login, open the Person and click **Actions**, then **Send Details Link**. Churchit emails a link to an **Update Your Details** page, where the person can change their name, gender, birthday, allergies, emails, phones, marital status, anniversary and directory choices. They cannot change addresses.

The link works once, for 7 days. To send a new link, click Send Details Link again. The item shows only when you can change the person and they have a primary email. The person's timeline shows when you sent it. The site must have an outgoing email account.

:::note[Example]
Before printing the new directory, the office sends a details link to every family without a login. Each family checks its phone numbers and birthdays and clicks Save. The office does not retype anything.
:::

## Member directory

The **Directory** page of the member portal lists the people of the member's church, so members can find each other. To turn it on, tick **Member Directory** in **People Settings**.

- It lists people whose Status is Active and whose Membership is filled in and is not Visitor or Contact, such as Members and Regular Attenders. It leaves out people who have died and people who hide from the directory.

- The same people can open it, also when they hide themselves from it. Others see a note to contact the church office.

- Each family is one entry, with its photo and names. The primary email, primary phone, mailing address and birthday (day and month only) show only when the person chose to share them.

- **Save contact** adds an entry to the phone's contacts, with only what the entry shows.

- Only public photos show. When you attach a photo in the desk, clear **Private** if members may see it.
