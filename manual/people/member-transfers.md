A Member Transfer records a member who moves to another church, or comes from one, often with a letter of transfer between the churches. Church Manager and Church People Editor users handle transfers.

1. Add a Member Transfer with the person, the **Direction** (In or Out) and the other church.
2. Update the **Status** as the transfer moves on: Requested, Pending, Letter Issued, then Confirmed (or Cancelled).
3. Print the **Letter of Transfer** from the form when you need one.

When you confirm a transfer **Out**, the person's Membership changes to Contact, their Status to Inactive, and their Inactive Reason to Transferred to another church. When you confirm a transfer **In**, the person gets a Membership life event dated that day, which names the other church, and becomes an active Member.

With more than one church, a move between your own branches is a transfer with a **To Church**: confirming it moves the person, and their member portal access, to that branch.

:::note[Example]
The Garcias move to another city and join a church there, which asks for a letter. The office adds an Out transfer, prints the Letter of Transfer and mails it. When the other church confirms, the office sets the status to Confirmed: the Garcias become Inactive contacts, and their history stays.
:::
