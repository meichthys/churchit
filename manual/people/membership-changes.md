Churchit keeps a record each time a person is added, and each time their Membership or Status changes. The [Membership Changes](/desk/query-report/Membership Changes) report lists these changes for a date range (the last 90 days at first), with the date, the person, the field, the old value and the new value. Use the **Field** and **New Value** filters to find, for example, everyone who became a Member this year. A person who is deleted drops out of this report.
