The Church Directory is a printed (or PDF) book of your church family: each household with its photo, names, phone numbers, emails and address, and extra pages such as birthdays, groups and missionaries. Church Staff and Church Managers make it.

**Example:** Each fall, the church secretary prints a directory booklet for the members. She ticks Members Only, Photos and Hide Non Active People, adds the Birthday List and the Notes Page, and ticks Booklet and Page Numbers. She prints the PDF double-sided, folds the sheets in half and staples them.

## Make the directory

1. Open **Church Directory** in the People sidebar.
2. Choose the options (see below). The preview updates after each choice.
3. Click **Print Directory** to open the directory as a PDF, and print it from there.

For a booklet, print double-sided, flip on the short edge, keep the sheets in order and fold them in half.

## The options

**People**

- **Members Only**: leaves out everyone who is not a member. It is off at first, so visitors and contacts print too.

- **Hide Non Active People**: leaves out everyone whose Status is not Active, with their birthday, and a couple's anniversary when either is not Active. A family whose primary contact is not Active is listed by its surname only.

- **Group by Family** (on at first): lists each family together, with the primary contact first, then the spouse, then the others. When it is off, each person is listed by name.

People with **Hide from Directory and Bulletin** ticked, and people who have died, are always left out, with their birthdays and anniversaries. A family whose primary contact is left out is listed by its surname only, such as Doe Family. A family whose members are all left out is not listed.

**Show**
- **Photos**: shows the photos.
- **Positions**: shows each person's church positions that they hold today.
- **Membership** (on at first): shows how each person is connected, such as Member or Visitor.
- **Family Relationships** (on at first): shows each person's relationship in the family, such as Wife or Son.

**Contact Details** (all on at first)
- **Phone Numbers** and **Emails**: each person's primary phone and email.
- **Addresses**: each family's primary address. A member whose own primary address differs shows it under their name.

**Extra Pages**

- **Birthday List** and **Anniversary List**: lists near the end. A couple is listed by their own names, such as John & Mary Smith.

- **Missionaries**: the missionaries that are not Inactive, with their people. A Sensitive missionary shows no country, email or website. The people of a listed missionary are not printed again on the family pages.

- **Notes Page**: a lined page for handwritten notes.

**Groups**
- **Group List**: the active groups, with each name and description.
- **Group Members**: the members of each group that has **Show in Directory** ticked.
- **Member Roles**: each member's role, such as John Smith | Leader.

**Printing**

- **Church Image on Cover**: the image of the [Church](/desk/church), such as a logo, at the bottom of the cover.

- **Page Numbers**: numbers each page except the cover.

- **Booklet**: a half-size booklet, two pages on each side of a letter sheet, in folding order. Up to three Notes pages are added so that the page count works.

- **Blank Back Cover**: an empty last page.

The cover shows the church's name, address, website, mission statement and Church Verse from the [Church](/desk/church) record. A branch with no website prints its parent church's website.

## Other printed papers

Open a person and click **Print** for the **Baptism Certificate**, the **Membership Certificate** (Letter paper, landscape) and the **Member QR Card**.
