A Care Request records that someone needs practical help: meals after a surgery, a hospital visit, a ride, help after a death. It tracks who takes care of it and when it must be done, so nobody falls through the cracks. Members can ask for care in the member portal, and staff add requests in the desk.

Church Pastoral Care and Church Manager users handle care requests. Church Staff can add and read them.

:::note[Example]
Ruth Brown asks for meals after her hip surgery, in the member portal. Her Care Assignment names Deacon Mike as her caregiver, so the request goes on Mike's To Do list, due in 7 days, and he gets an email. Mike moves the card to In Progress on the Care Board, arranges a week of meals with the Hospitality team, writes what was done in Resolution Notes, and sets the request to Resolved.
:::

## Add a care request

1. Open **Care Requests** and click **Add Care Request**.
2. Pick the **Person** that the request is for. **Caregiver** fills in from their Care Assignment (see below). You can change it.
3. Choose the **Type**, such as Meals or Hospital Visit, and the **Urgency**. You can add more Care Request Types.
4. Describe the need and save.

**Due On** is set from the urgency: Urgent 1 day, High 3 days, Medium 7 days, Low 14 days. When you change the urgency, the due date is counted again. You can also type a date, but not one before Submitted On. A request from the member portal starts with Medium urgency.

## Who is told

- When the caregiver has a desk login, the request goes on their To Do list, dated when it is due, and they get an email. When you choose a new caregiver, the request moves to that person's list. When you resolve or close it, it leaves the list.

- When there is no caregiver with a desk login, the Church Pastoral Care users and the Church Managers of the church get an email and a desk notification (**New Care Request**). A caregiver with no desk login gets nothing, and Churchit tells you so: let them know yourself.

- To email the caregiver and the care team when a request is overdue, a Church Manager ticks **Email When a Care Request Is Overdue** under Reminders in **People Settings**. **Days After the Request Is Due** sets when (1 day at first).

- The member who asked sees the status of their request in the member portal, and it updates when staff change it. Only staff can change the status.

## Status and the Care Board

A request moves through **Requested**, **Triaged** (someone has looked at it), **In Progress**, **Resolved** and **Closed**. The Care Requests list opens on the requests that are not Resolved or Closed, the soonest due first. Clear the Status filter to see all.

The **Care Board** shows the open requests as cards in a column for each status. Drag a card to another column to change its status. Open it from the Care Board shortcut on the People workspace, or choose **Kanban** in the view menu of the list. Write what was done in **Resolution Notes**. Reopening a resolved request puts it back on the caregiver's To Do list.

Churchit keeps a history of changes to care requests, care assignments, visits and member transfers, in Activity at the bottom of the form. With more than one church, the caregiver must be able to see the request's church.

**AI suggestions.** When AI is turned on and a Church Manager has turned on suggestions on new requests (see the [Foundations manual](../foundations/README.md)), a new care request shows an **AI Suggestion** part with a suggested category and urgency to the care team. **Use Category** and **Use Urgency** fill in the form without saving it. The AI never replies or chooses the caregiver.
