The People module is the heart of Churchit: it keeps every person and family your church knows, from the first-time visitor to the longtime member. Use it to keep contact details up to date, organize small groups, welcome and follow up visitors, care for people in need, and print the church directory.

Church Office users (the Church People Editor role) add and change people, families and groups. Church Care Team users look after care requests, visits, background checks and notes. Other staff can look people up. Members see and correct their own household in the member portal.

The [People workspace](/desk/people) starts with the work that waits for you: **Open Care Requests** and the **Care Board** (for the care team and managers), **Follow-ups Needed**, **Pending Join Requests**, **New Connection Cards** and **Open Possible Duplicates**. Shortcuts open the **Rolodex**, the **Check-In Station** and the **Map of Addresses**.

Under them, cards count the members, families and groups, the first-time visitors, the new members of the last 30 days and the people who may have stopped coming. The charts show new members by month and baptisms by year.

The People sidebar has the pages you use every day at the top: **People**, **Families**, **Groups**, **Rolodex** and **Church Directory**. The other pages are in folders: **Care**, **Visitors & Follow-up**, **Group Tools**, **Membership**, **Dates**, **Letters**, **Data** and **Setup**. Each folder ends with its reports.
