A Background Check records a screening that a person had, so that leaders know who is cleared to serve, for example with children. Churchit does not run the check: order it from your provider, then record the result here. Church Pastoral Care and Church Manager users handle background checks.

1. Open **Background Checks** and click **Add Background Check**.
2. Pick the person and the Background Check Type: Criminal Background, Sex Offender Registry, Child Abuse Clearance, Driving Record, Child Safety Training, or your own.
3. Note the provider and their reference number, and attach the report if you have it.
4. Move the **Status** from **Requested** to **Pending** to **Cleared** or **Not Cleared**. A result needs a **Completed On** date.

**Expires On** fills in from the type's **Valid For (Years)**: Criminal Background, Sex Offender Registry and Driving Record 3 years, Child Abuse Clearance 5, Child Safety Training 2. A type with 0 years never expires. You can change the date. Each night, a cleared check past its date becomes **Expired**.

- The person's form shows their latest check: a cleared one on the Connections tab, and one that is Requested, Pending, Not Cleared or Expired at the top of the form, above the tabs.

- A ministry team position or a group can require some check types and block people without them (see the [Ministries manual](../ministries/README.md) and Group above). They accept any current cleared check of the right type.

- The **Checks Expiring** list (in the Care folder) shows the cleared checks that expire in the next 30 days. The shipped **Background Check Expiring** notification can email the care team 30 days before (see [Notifications](../communications/notifications.md) in the Communications manual).

:::note[Example]
The children's ministry requires a criminal background check and child safety training for nursery workers. When Anna volunteers, the office orders her check and records it as Requested. Two weeks later the provider clears her, and the office sets Cleared with the date. Anna can now be scheduled in the nursery. Three years later, her check shows on Checks Expiring a month before it ends.
:::
