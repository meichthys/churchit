A Care Assignment gives a person a caregiver for a time, such as a deacon or an elder who looks after a group of households. When you pick that person on a Care Request, Caregiver fills in from their current assignment.

Add a Care Assignment with the person, the caregiver and the start date. Leave the end date empty for no end. The list shows each assignment as **Current**, **Upcoming** (before its start date) or **Ended** (after its end date). Click one to see only those.

:::note[Example]
The church divides its families among six deacons. Each family's members get a Care Assignment to their deacon. When one of them asks for a meal, the request goes straight to the right deacon.
:::
