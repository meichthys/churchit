A [Visitation Log](/desk/visitation-log) records a visit to a person by someone from the church: a hospital visit, a home visit, a welcome visit. It holds the **Person**, the **Visit Date**, the **Visit Type**, who visited (**Visited By**), the **Status**, the **Duration**, **Follow-Up Needed** and notes. Only Church Pastoral Care and Church Manager users can open visitation logs.

1. Open [Visitations](/desk/visitation-log) and click **Add Visitation Log**.
2. Pick the person, the date and the type, and who visits. You can add more [Visit Types](/desk/visit-type).
3. Set the **Status**: **Planned**, **Scheduled** or **Completed**.
4. After the visit, write what happened and tick **Follow-Up Needed** if someone should visit again.

- When **Email Before a Planned Visit** is on under Reminders in [People Settings](/desk/people-settings), a Planned or Scheduled visit emails the visitor and the Church Managers before the visit. **Days Before the Visit** sets when (1 day at first).

- With Follow-Up Needed ticked, **Schedule Follow Up** on the form opens a new Scheduled visit for the same person, type and visitor.

- A visit that needs a follow-up stays on the **Follow-ups Needed** list until the person has a newer Scheduled or Completed visit.

- A person's latest completed visit shows as **Last Visited** on their Person form. Print a list of visits with the **Visitation Schedule** print format.

**Pastoral Care Watch List.** This report, in the Care folder, lists the people who may need a pastor now: people with a visit in the last 30 days that needs a follow-up and has nothing newer, and people with an open urgent prayer request. It shows their phone and email. Change **Window (Days)** to look further back.

**Example:** The pastor visits Mr. Jones in hospital and ticks Follow-Up Needed. Mr. Jones appears on the Watch List. The pastor clicks Schedule Follow Up for next Tuesday, and Mr. Jones leaves the Follow-ups Needed list.
