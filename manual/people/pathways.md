A [Pathway](/desk/pathway) is a path that people take through the life of the church, such as Came Back, Joined a Group, Baptized and Serving. Each step is a smart list (see Lists of People). A person has done a step when they are on the step's list.

1. Save a smart list for each step, for example a list of people whose Groups count is 1 or more.
2. Add a Pathway, give it a name, and add one row for each step, in the usual order, with the step's **Smart List**.
3. Click **Open Progress**, or open the [Pathway Progress](/desk/query-report/Pathway Progress) report and choose the pathway.

The report lists each living person who has done at least one step, with a tick for each step done and the first step not done yet. With more than one church, a pathway counts only the people of its own church. To start a follow-up when a person reaches a step, add an **On Smart List** trigger with the step's list to a Follow-up Workflow (see Follow-up).
